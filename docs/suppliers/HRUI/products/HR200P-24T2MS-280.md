## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR200P-24T2MS-280 is a high-performance 24-port Gigabit PoE switch featuring 24 Gigabit PoE ports and 2×2.5G SFP uplink ports, delivering reliable high-speed connectivity for SMB and surveillance networks. With a 260W PoE power budget, IEEE 802.3af/at compliance, and HiPoE 60W support on ports 1–2, it easily powers IP cameras, wireless access points, VoIP phones, and other PoE-enabled devices. Integrated VLAN isolation, PoE Watchdog, loop protection, and extended transmission up to 250 meters ensure stable, secure, and efficient network operation for demanding deployment environments.
Product Features:
Support 24*1Gbps PoE ports+2*2.5Gbps SFP ports
Comply with IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3ab, and IEEE802.3z standards
Ethernet Ports support 10/100/1000Mbps self-adaptive
Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)
Adopt a storage and forwarding exchange mechanism
PoE ports automatically enable PoE watchdog mode in Extend and VLAN modes
Support loop protection function
The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis
Ports 1-2 support HiPoE 60W
Support PoE priority to be enabled by default, with a priority order of 1-24
```
