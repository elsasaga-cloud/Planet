## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR720P-8T is a high-performance industrial Gigabit PoE switch, engineered to deliver reliable power and data integrity in harsh environments. Featuring
8×
10/100/1000Mbps PoE ports with IEEE 802.3af/at support and 60W HiPoE on Port 1, it enables flexible deployment across transportation monitoring, outdoor surveillance, EV charging systems, and industrial automation.
With a 16 Gbps switching capacity, the HR720P-8T delivers non-blocking, line-rate forwarding across all ports — ideal for bandwidth-intensive applications. Backed by 6kV surge, 15kV ESD, and 2kV power input protection, along with -40°C to 75°C wide-temperature tolerance and triple power input, the HR720P-8T ensures stable, uninterrupted operation. Its IP40-rated aluminum housing and DIN35 rail mounting make it a perfect fit for space-constrained industrial cabinets and mission-critical deployments.
Product Features:
Port Configuration:
8 × 10/100/1000Mbps PoE ports for stable power and data delivery in industrial applications.
High PoE Support:
Port 1 supports 60W HiPoE for compatible devices.
PoE Standard:
IEEE 802.3af/at (≤30W per port).
Extended Transmission:
Supports 250m long-distance PoE for flexible deployment across wide monitoring areas.
Industrial Design:
IP40 enclosure with DIN35 rail installation for durable and efficient cabinet integration.
Wide Temperature Range:
Operates reliably from -40°C to 75°C in harsh or extreme environments.
Dual/Triple Power Input:
Triple power input with anti-reverse protection and 2000V power-input insulation for safe, uninterrupted operation.
Protection:
6kV surge protection and 15kV ESD protection for enhanced reliability in industrial scenarios.
Certifications:
CE, FCC, and RoHS compliance for international safety and environmental standards.
```
