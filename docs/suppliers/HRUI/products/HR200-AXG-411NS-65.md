## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
The HR200-AXG-411NS-65 is a high-performance 6-Port Gigabit Intelligent PoE Switch, designed for efficient power distribution and robust network connectivity. It features 4 Gigabit PoE ports, 1 Gigabit uplink RJ45 port, and 1 Gigabit uplink SFP port, fully compliant with IEEE 802.3af/at and Hi-PoE standards. With a bandwidth capacity of 12 Gbps and a total power output of 65W, this switch ensures seamless data transmission and reliable power supply. Port 1 uniquely supports Hi-PoE power delivery of up to 60W. Ideal for a wide range of environments, it operates effectively within a temperature range of -10°C to 50°C and also includes features such as port isolation and a maximum transmission distance of 250 meters.
Product Features:
Port Configuration:
4 Gigabit PoE ports, 1 Gigabit SFP uplink port, and 1 Gigabit RJ45 uplink port for flexible connectivity options.
Standards Compliance:
Designed in accordance with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3ab, IEEE 802.3z, and IEEE 802.3x standards for reliable performance.
Flexible Connectivity:
RJ45 ports support 10M, 100M, and 1G auto-negotiation for versatility.
Flow Control:
Supports Full Duplex (IEEE 802.3x) and Half Duplex (Back Pressure) flow control methods.
Auto MDI/MDIX Support:
Automatic port flipping capability eliminates the need for crossover cables, simplifying setup.
Advanced Mechanism:
Utilizes a storage and forwarding exchange mechanism for efficient data handling.
Status Monitoring:
The panel features LED indicators that monitor operational status and help with fault diagnosis.
Flexible Modes:
Supports VLAN, Default, and Extend modes for customized network management.
Power Output:
Delivers up to 60W of PoE power from Port 1, ideal for power-hungry devices.
```
