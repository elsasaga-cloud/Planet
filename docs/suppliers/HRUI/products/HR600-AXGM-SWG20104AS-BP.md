## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR600-AXGM-SWG20104AS-BP, a 14-port Gigabit Industrial Ethernet PoE Switch. Featuring 10 Gigabit PoE ports (Ports 9-10 with BT support) and 4 Gigabit SFP ports (Ports 13-14 support up to 2.5G, also compatible with 1.25G/155M), along with an additional console port, this switch is engineered for demanding industrial environments.
Designed with an integrated bypass protection function, it ensures uninterrupted communication, making it ideal for critical automation applications. Mounting options include DIN rail and wall installations to suit various setups. The switch accommodates dual power inputs (DC48-55V) and operates seamlessly in extreme temperatures ranging from -40°C to 75°C. It complies with industrial standards and is certified by FCC, CE, CCC, and RoHS, guaranteeing dependable performance in harsh conditions.
The HR600 series is perfect for diverse applications, including wind power, electric power, assembly automation, subway public information systems (PIS), electric power SCADA, wastewater treatment, metallurgy, intelligent transportation, railways, and military operations. Recognized for its low-power, eco-friendly design, this switch is an ideal choice for green industrial automation.
Product Features:
Port Configuration:
10 Gigabit PoE ports (ports 9-10 support BT) + 4 Gigabit SFP ports (ports 13-14 support 2.5G and are compatible with 1.25G/155M) + 1 Console Port.
Standards Compliance:
IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3x, IEEE 802.3z/ab, IEEE 802.1Q, IEEE 802.1p, IEEE 802.1d/w.
PoE Management:
Features PoE power management and PoE watchdog utilizing data stream detection.
Spanning Tree Protocols:
Supports STP (802.1D), RSTP (802.1w), MSTP (802.1s), and ERPS.
Network Management:
Compatible with SNMP v1/v2/v3, IGMP Snooping, static multicast filtering, and MLD Snooping.
Security Features:
802.1X port-based access and AAA certification for enhanced security.
PoE Support:
Compatible with IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and IEEE802.3bt (90W).
Management Interfaces:
Access via WEB, Telnet, CLI, and SSH.
Durable Design:
IP40 protection with a rugged, high-strength metal case, suitable for DIN35 Rail mounting.
Environmental Resistance:
EMC grade 4 design, operational between -40°C and 75°C.
Redundant Power Supply:
Supports redundant power supply connections with anti-reverse protection.
Surge Protection:
General 6KV, Differential 4KV; ESD ratings of 15KV (Air) and 8KV (Contact).
EMC Compliance:
Adheres to EMC EN55032 standards for electromagnetic compatibility.
```
