## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
The HR600-AFGM-82AS is a state-of-the-art 2.5G managed PoE switch designed for industrial applications. Featuring 8 Gigabit PoE ports, 2 high-speed 2.5G SFP uplink ports, and 1 console port, this switch delivers an impressive bandwidth of 26Gbps. It employs advanced Layer 2 management capabilities, supports the ERPS protocol for enhanced ring network redundancy, and is built to withstand tough environments with its IP40-rated protection. With surge protection up to 6KV and ESD immunity of 15KV, this switch is equipped for reliable operation. It supports a wide temperature range of -40°C to +75°C and has redundant power supply options, making it ideal for mission-critical applications.
Product Features:
Standards Compliance:
Compatible with IEEE 802.3, 802.3u, 802.3x, 802.3z/ab, 802.1Q, 802.1p, and 802.1D/W networking standards.
Power over Ethernet (PoE) Management:
Supports IEEE 802.3af (15.4W) and 802.3at (30W) standards, with advanced PoE power management and PoE watchdog functionality based on data stream detection.
Redundancy Protocols:
Supports Spanning Tree Protocol (STP), Rapid STP (RSTP), Multiple STP (MSTP), and Ethernet Ring Protection Switching (ERPS) for network reliability.
Network Monitoring:
SNMP v1/v2/v3 support for efficient network management, alongside IGMP Snooping, Static multicast filtering, and MLD Snooping to optimize multicast traffic.
Enhanced Security:
Includes 802.1x port security and AAA certification to reinforce network security protocols.
Management Interfaces:
Offers multiple management options including WEB, Telnet, CLI, and SSH for convenience and flexibility.
Durable Design:
Constructed with a rugged high-strength metal casing, featuring IP40 protection, DIN35 rail mounting options, and meeting EMC grade 4 standards; operational from -40°C to 75°C.
Power Redundancy:
Supports redundant power supply connections and safeguards against reverse polarity connections.
EMC Standards Compliance:
Meets EMC standards including EN55032 for electromagnetic compatibility and interference resistance.
```
