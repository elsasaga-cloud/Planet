**Brief:** 6 Port 10G uplink 2.5G web managed Ethernet Switch, using high quality and high speed network chip solution, this product completely solves the problem of loud noise of small switches deployed in the office environment through the fanless silent design. This switch has 5* 100/1000/2500M RJ45 ports and 1* 10G SFP port, providing seamless connection to 100/1000/2500M Ethernet for easy management and maintenance. This switch can be widely used in security network video surveillance, hotels, campuses, and network engineering of small and medium-sized enterprises.

## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR-SWTGW215AS, a robust 6-port Ethernet switch designed with a 10G uplink and 2.5G web management capabilities. Built with high-quality, high-speed networking chip technology, this switch addresses the common issue of noise pollution in office environments through its innovative fanless design, ensuring silent operation.
The switch features five 100/1000/2500M RJ45 ports and one 10G SFP port, facilitating seamless connectivity across multiple Ethernet standards for straightforward management and maintenance. Ideal for a variety of applications, the HR-SWTGW215AS is perfect for security network video surveillance, hotels, educational campuses, and the network engineering needs of small to medium-sized enterprises.
Product Features:
Port Configuration:
5 x 2.5Gbps RJ45 ports and 1 x 10Gbps SFP+ port for versatile and high-speed connections.
Standards Compliance:
Fully compliant with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3ab, IEEE 802.3bz, IEEE 802.3z, IEEE802.3ae, and IEEE 802.3x standards, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of devices.
Self-Adaptive Ethernet Ports:
Supports auto-sensing for 10M, 100M, 1G, and 2.5G speeds, allowing for seamless integration with existing networks.
Automatic Port Detection:
Features Auto MDI/MDIX capabilities, simplifying cable connections and eliminating the need for cross-over cables.
Status Monitoring:
Panel indicator LEDs provide real-time monitoring of operational status, aiding in quick fault analysis.
Flow Control:
Implements IEEE 802.3x standard for full duplex and backpressure for half duplex to optimize network performance.
Quality of Service (QoS):
Supports data priority settings for efficient bandwidth management.
```
