## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
The HR600-SWG2082AS-BP is a high-performance, 10-port Gigabit Industrial Ethernet Switch designed to meet the rigorous demands of industrial applications. This robust switch features 8 Gigabit Ethernet ports along with 2 flexible 2.5G SFP ports (backward compatible with 1.25G / 155M) and a convenient console port for easy management.
Engineered with advanced bypass protection, this switch ensures seamless network operation even during power failures. Its versatile mounting options—DIN rail and wall—allow for efficient installation in various environments. The switch operates on a wide power range of DC 12-55V and is built to withstand extreme temperatures from -40 to 75°C.
This industrial switch is certified for compliance with FCC, CE, CCC, and RoHS standards, ensuring it meets stringent industrial environment requirements. Its reliable design guarantees stable operation in automation systems, providing uninterrupted communication crucial for industrial processes.
Ideal for applications in wind power, electric power distribution, assembly automation, subway passenger information systems (PIS), electric SCADA systems, sewage treatment facilities, metallurgy, intelligent transportation, rail transportation, military operations, and more, this switch offers a green, low-power solution for network connectivity.
Equipped with advanced network management features, including redundant ring protocols, loop protection, VLAN support, Quality of Service (QoS), speed control, port mirroring, and online firmware upgrades, this switch is designed for seamless integration into complex industrial networks. With an emphasis on durability and reliability, all components are rated for industrial-grade use, offering redundant power inputs and outputs to maintain continuous operation in critical communications.
Product Features:
Flexible Port Configuration:
8 Gigabit Ethernet Ports, 2 * 2.5G SFP Ports (backward compatible with 1.25G / 155M), 1 Console Port
Compliance with Standards:
Supports IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3z/ab, IEEE802.1Q, IEEE802.1p, IEEE802.1d/w.
Bypass Protection:
Integrated functionality to maintain network continuity during power loss.
Network Protocols:
Supports STP (802.1D), RSTP (802.1w), MSTP (802.1s), and ERPS for reliability and redundancy.
Advanced Management:
Compatibility with SNMP v1/v2/v3 and management through WEB, Telnet, CLI, and SSH.
Multicast Filtering:
IGMP Snooping, static multicast filtering, and MLD Snooping for efficient bandwidth management.
Enhanced Security:
Supports 802.1x port authentication and AAA certification for improved network security.
Robust Design:
IP40 rated, housed in a rugged high-strength metal case, suitable for DIN35 rail mounting.
Environmental Resilience:
EMC Grade 4 design ensuring rugged performance in harsh environments, with operating temperatures from -40°C to 75°C.
Redundant Power Supply:
Supports dual power supply access with anti-reverse connection protection.
Surge Protection:
6KV general surge immunity, 4KV differential, and 15KV (air) / 8KV (contact) ESD protection.
EMC Compliance:
Meets EN55032 standards for electromagnetic compatibility.
```
