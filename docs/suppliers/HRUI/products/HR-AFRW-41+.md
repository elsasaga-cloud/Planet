## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
Plug and Play PoE Extension, No AC Power Required
HR-AFRW-41+ is a rugged, reliable, and simply weatherproof 100Mbps PoE extender. No AC power or rewiring is required, enabling plug and play for immediate deployment. It features 1 PoE input and 4 PoE output ports, supports HiPoE 60W input, and complies with IEEE802.3af/at. With Extend mode enabled, transmission distance reaches 250 meters (10Mbps).
Compliant with IEEE 802.3/802.3u/802.3x, the extender supports Auto MDI/MDIX and relay extension. LED indicators facilitate fault analysis. Encased in a waterproof enclosure, it withstands harsh environments. Ideal for IP cameras, access points, and IoT devices, it is wall mountable for flexible installation.
Product Highlights
• 1*100Mbps PoE in port+4*100Mbps PoE out ports.
• Comply with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3x standards.
• Support HiPoE 60W in.
• Support IEEE802.3af/at power supply standard.
• Ethernet Ports support 10/100Mbps self-adaptive.
• The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis.
• Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX).
• Support Extend mode.
• Having network and PoE relay extension roles.
• Flow control method, full duplex adopts IEEE 802.3x standard, and half duplex adopts Back pressure standard.
```
