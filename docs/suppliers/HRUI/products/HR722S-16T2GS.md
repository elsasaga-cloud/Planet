## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR722S-16T2GS is designed for demanding industrial environments, providing robust connectivity and reliable performance for applications such as factory automation, intelligent transportation, and video surveillance.
It offers advanced L2+ management capabilities and enhanced availability, enabling the construction of a secure, high-performance access layer for mission-critical applications.
Product Features:
Port Configuration:
16 × 1Gbps RJ45, 2 × 1Gbps SFP, and 1 console port for flexible industrial connectivity.
Protocol Support:
Compliant with IEEE 802.3/802.3u/802.3x/802.3z/ab, 802.1Q/1p, and 802.1d/w for reliable interoperability.
Advanced Network Management:
Supports STP/RSTP/MSTP and SNMP v1/v2 for efficient network control and redundancy.
Enhanced Security:
Features 802.1X and AAA authentication, plus IGMP and MLD Snooping for secure multicast management.
Flexible Management Options:
Offers WEB, Telnet, CLI, and SSH interfaces to match diverse operational preferences.
Rugged Industrial Design:
Durable metal housing with IP40 protection and DIN-35 rail mounting for harsh environments.
Industrial-Grade Reliability:
Wide operating temperature of –40°C to +75°C, with redundant power input and reverse-polarity protection.
Standards Compliance:
Meets industrial EMC and safety standards for stable performance in noisy environments.
```
