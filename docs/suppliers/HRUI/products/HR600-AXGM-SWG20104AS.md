## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR600-AXGM-SWG20104AS, a state-of-the-art industrial managed PoE switch designed for high-performance connectivity in demanding environments. This 2.5G ring network switch features 10 Gigabit PoE ports and 4 Gigabit uplink SFP ports, alongside a console port for streamlined management. With 9-10 ports supporting BT90W power supply and 13-14 ports accommodating 2.5G, you can enjoy unparalleled bandwidth of up to 34Gbps. The switch employs Layer 2 management, ensures ERPS protocol compliance, and offers ring network redundancy for enhanced reliability. Built to withstand extreme conditions, the device boasts IP40 protection, 6KV surge protection, 15KV ESD protection, and operates within a temperature range of -40°C to +75°C. The DC48-55V power input with redundant power supply options guarantees uninterrupted operation in critical applications.
Product Features:
Standards Compliance:
Fully supports IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3z/ab, IEEE802.1Q, IEEE802.1p, and IEEE802.1D/W protocols for universal connectivity and performance.
Power over Ethernet (PoE) Management:
Incorporates cutting-edge PoE power management and PoE watchdog capabilities based on data stream detection, ensuring efficient power usage.
PoE Standards Supported:
Compatible with IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and IEEE802.3bt (90W) for flexible power delivery across devices.
Redundancy Protocols:
Supports STP (802.1D), RSTP (802.1w), MSTP (802.1s), and ERPS for resilient network architecture.
Network Management:
Features SNMP v1/v2/v3 for effective network management, alongside IGMP Snooping, Static Multicast Filtering, and MLD Snooping for optimized multicast performance.
Enhanced Security:
Supports 802.1x port authentication and AAA (Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting) certification to bolster network security.
Management Interfaces:
Offers multiple management options including WEB, Telnet, CLI, and SSH for convenience and flexibility.
Rugged Design:
Housed in a robust, high-strength metal case with IP40 protection. Designed for DIN35 rail mounting, ensuring it stands up to industrial environments.
Environmental and EMC Compliance:
EMC grade 4 design ensures reliable operation in various conditions, with a working temperature range of -40°C to 75°C. Supports redundant power supply with anti-reverse connection to safeguard against electrical risks.
EMC Standards Compliance:
Meets EMC EN55032 standards for interference and noise control, ensuring smooth operation in complex environments.
```
