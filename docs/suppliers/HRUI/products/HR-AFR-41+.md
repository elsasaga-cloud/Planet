## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
Efficient. Scalable. Easy to Install.
HR-AFR-41+ is a Fast Ethernet PoE extender designed for expanding network and surveillance coverage. Featuring 1× 100Mbps PoE IN port and 4× 100Mbps PoE OUT ports, it extends both power and data over a single Ethernet cable — maximizing port density while minimizing installation costs. Supporting HiPoE 60W input and IEEE 802.3af/at PoE standards, it delivers stable power and 10/100M connectivity for IP cameras, access points, and other PoE-powered devices.
Compliant with IEEE 802.3, 802.3u, and 802.3x standards, the extender supports 10/100Mbps auto-negotiation, Auto MDI/MDIX, and a long-range mode (activated via DIP switch) that extends PoE and data transmission up to 250 meters at 10Mbps. Real-time LED status indicators enable quick fault analysis. With flexible desktop, wall-mount, or DIN-rail installation, the HR-AFR-41+ is ideal for multi-device deployments such as hotels, schools, parking lots, and large-scale surveillance projects.
Product Highlights
• 1*100Mbps PoE in port+4*100Mbps PoE out ports.
• Comply with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3ab, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3x standards.
• Support HiPoE 60W in.
• Support IEEE802.3af/at power supply standard.
• Ethernet Ports support 10/100Mbps self-adaptive.
• The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis.
• Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX).
• Support Extend mode.
• Having network and PoE relay extension roles.
• Flow control method, full duplex adopts IEEE 802.3x standard, and half duplex adopts Back pressure standard.
```
