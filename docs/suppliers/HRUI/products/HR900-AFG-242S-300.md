> 类目：PoE Switch（site id 224）  
> 接口：24* 1000Mbps PoE ports / 2 *1000Mbps SFP ports；PoE 预算 280W；交换带宽 —；电源 AC 100-240V 50/60Hz / DC 52V 5.76A

**Brief:** 26 Port Gigabit Ethernet PoE Switch, adopting high-quality and high-speed network IC, this switch thoroughly solves the problem of high noise when deploying small switches in office environments through a fan free silent design. This switch has 24* Gigabit PoE network ports and 2* Gigabit SFP ports, providing seamless connection for 10/100/1000M Ethernet for easy management and maintenance. The PoE power supply port can automatically detect and supply power to powered devices that comply with the IEEE802.3af/at standard. Non-PoE devices will not be powered by intelligent detection and only transmit data. It can be widely used in security network video surveillance, hotels, campus, small and medium-sized enterprise network engineering and other occasions.

## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
The HRUI AI PoE switches provide a range of models featuring both 100M/Gigabit PoE, RJ45, and SFP uplink ports. These switches meet the IEEE 802.3af/at/High-PoE standards for Power over Ethernet, with PoE port 1 capable of delivering a maximum output of 60W and other PoE ports capable of 30W. The devices offer three working modes: Default, VLAN (Port Isolation), and Extend. Additionally, the series supports PoE watchdog functionality in both VLAN and Extend modes, enabling the detection and automatic reboot of unresponsive or dropped PoE-powered devices.
With user-friendly installation and maintenance processes, these switches help establish a secure and high-performance network. HRUI AI PoE switches are ideal for various Ethernet access applications, including small to medium-sized enterprises, internet cafes, hotels, and schools.
FEATURES
• Power Over Ethernet
This series of switches provides PoE ports, compliant with IEEE 802.3af/at, enabling simultaneous data and power transmission to devices via a single, convenient Ethernet cable. By integrating power delivery into a single cable, these switches streamline cabling infrastructure and eliminate the need for separate power sources, thereby providing an efficient and cost-effective solution for powering network devices.
• Advanced Functions with DIP Switch
VLAN:
Downlink ports are isolated from each other, but can still communicate with uplink ports. This mode also enables PoE watchdog on ports.
Default:
All ports support communication with each other, with a transmission distance of up to 100 meters and an adaptive speed of 10/100/1000Mbps. This mode disables PoE watchdog on ports.
Extend:
The downlink PoE & data transmission distance can reach up to 250 meters, with a speed of 10Mbps. This mode enables PoE watchdog on ports.
• AI PoE Watchdog
Detect and reboot dropped or unresponsive PoE-powered devices.
• A Clear View of Power Usage
The product feature PoE Max indicators, allowing for easy monitoring of power consumption, enabling optimal power usage and ensuring efficient energy management.
• Plug and Play, Easy to Use
All Ethernet ports on our switches support MDI/MDIX compatibility, allowing for easy setup with no software installation or configuration needed.
• 19 Inch Rack Mout
• High Reliable Design
Support installed in a standard rack environment, easy to integrate with other equipment and manage cabling.
Support 4KV surge and 8KV ESD protection.
```
