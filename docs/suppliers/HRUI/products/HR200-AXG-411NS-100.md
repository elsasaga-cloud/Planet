## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
HR200-AXG-411NS-120, a cutting-edge Gigabit Intelligent Power over Ethernet (PoE) switch designed for robust network performance. This switch features 4 Gigabit PoE ports, 1 Gigabit uplink RJ45 port, and 1 Gigabit uplink SFP port, complying with IEEE 802.3af/at/ Hi-PoE standards. With a high bandwidth capacity of 12Gbps and a total power output of 100W, this switch is engineered to meet the demands of modern networks. Notably, Port 1 supports a powerful 60W Hi-PoE power supply. The HR200 is designed for a wide operating temperature range of -10 to 50°C and offers enhanced functionality, including port isolation and a remarkable 250m transmission capability, making it ideal for a variety of applications.
Product Features:
Multiple Ports:
Equipped with 4 Gigabit PoE ports, 1 Gigabit SFP port, and 1 Gigabit RJ45 port for versatile connectivity.
Standards Compliance:
Fully compliant with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3ab, IEEE 802.3z, and IEEE 802.3x standards.
Adaptive RJ45:
The RJ45 port automatically adjusts to support speeds of 10M, 100M, and 1G.
Flow Control:
Implements full duplex flow control via the IEEE 802.3x standard, while half duplex utilizes the Backpressure standard for reliable performance.
Auto MDI/MDIX Support:
Automatic port flipping capability eliminates the need for crossover cables, simplifying setup.
Advanced Mechanism:
Utilizes a storage and forwarding exchange mechanism for efficient data handling.
Status Monitoring:
The panel features LED indicators that monitor operational status and help with fault diagnosis.
Flexible Modes:
Supports VLAN, Default, and Extend modes for customized network management.
Power Output:
Delivers up to 60W of PoE power from Port 1, ideal for power-hungry devices.
```
