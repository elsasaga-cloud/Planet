## Feature and Benefits（原文）
```
The HR200-AXG-42NS-65 is a high-performance Gigabit Intelligent PoE Switch designed for seamless connectivity and efficient power delivery. Featuring 4 Gigabit PoE ports and 2 Gigabit SFP uplink ports, this switch complies with IEEE 802.3af, at, and Hi-PoE standards, ensuring reliable power supply and data transmission. With a total bandwidth of 12Gbps and a power budget of 65W, Port 1 supports an impressive Hi-PoE output of 60W. This switch also offers port isolation and a transmission distance of up to 250 meters, making it ideal for various applications across a wide temperature range of -10°C to 50°C.
Product Features:
Port Configuration:
4 x 1G PoE ports & 2 x 1G SFP uplink ports
Standards Compliance:
Fully adheres to IEEE 802.3, 802.3u, 802.3ab, 802.3z, and 802.3x standards
Flexible Connectivity:
RJ45 ports support 10M, 100M, and 1G auto-negotiation for versatility.
Flow Control:
Supports Full Duplex (IEEE 802.3x) and Half Duplex (Back Pressure) flow control methods.
Auto MDI/MDIX Support:
Automatic port flipping capability eliminates the need for crossover cables, simplifying setup.
Advanced Mechanism:
Utilizes a storage and forwarding exchange mechanism for efficient data handling.
Status Monitoring:
The panel features LED indicators that monitor operational status and help with fault diagnosis.
Flexible Modes:
Supports VLAN, Default, and Extend modes for customized network management.
Power Output:
Delivers up to 60W of PoE power from Port 1, ideal for power-hungry devices.
```
