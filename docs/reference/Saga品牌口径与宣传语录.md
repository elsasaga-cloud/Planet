## 1. 理念语录（可直接引用，英文原文）

### 1.1 Roadmap —「What We Believe」
- **Continuity Over Novelty** — "Upgrading from Access to Core is treated as a system extension rather than a replacement. By maintaining familiar workflows, we ensure that as bandwidth increases, operational complexity does not."
- **Rational Technology Selection** — "Silicon choice is an engineering decision, not a marketing one. Access Layer: Anchored on Marvell platforms selected for their industrial pedigree, field-proven stability, and mature ecosystem. Aggregation & Core Layer: Built on CENTEC solutions introduced only after comprehensive validation of ecosystem maturity, long-term supply alignment, and backbone-grade reliability."
- **Operational & Collaboration Efficiency** — "Simplified vendor qualification and procurement / Reduced repeated system re-validation and compatibility testing / **A single, accountable technical contact across the entire network lifecycle**."
- 产品观："These are not isolated hardware SKUs, but a coherent, scalable system that can evolve with network demands."
- 风险观："The transition from Access → Aggregation/Core is often the highest-risk point in a network's lifecycle."

### 1.2 How We Do Business
- "Saga Telecom is being **digital and paperless** to be efficient and eco-friendly **#gogreen**. It will ONLY be electronic information unless it is specifically requested."
- "**Simplified, Standard, Solution based.** This is applicable to our before-, in- and after-sales services."
- "All is about a truly helpful solution. If what you need is NOT available on our list, we will help source it (it will be an add-on price) if acceptable."
- "Saga Telecom proposes a **buffet-style business communication**" — ① all the information is available without asking（公开 Google 链接）；② business details are being continuously updated（季度总更新）；③ value and growth manifest itself.
- 收尾句："It starts from the promise of professionalism and goes far beyond. **It is not only about fiber optic connection, but also about human connection.**"

### 1.3 Client Oriented
- "We are backing our clients and following the market trend. **Let's evolve together!!**"（签名里 "Evolving Together" 的出处）
- 做法：收集客户反馈（features to be added / dysfunctions in sample testing / technical support required）"and trying to solve real problems"；案例 1 "Readily Available — Dual AC Input"、案例 2 "Newest Firmware"。

### 1.4 Easy and Transparent Business
- 每个客户一个按「国家 / 客户名」命名的 Google Drive 文件夹，含 Datasheet / User Manual / Certificate of Warranty / Warranty Policy / Web & CLI Manual / Product Photos / Shipping Document / Details of This Order。
- 价值句："When a client needs something urgently, but the salesperson cannot reply timely … This google drive link will be very helpful."

### 1.5 CCF（Commercial Contract Framework）
- "To enhance efficiency and transparency … a CCF (commercial contract framework) will be established and mutually agreed upon by both parties. Saga Telecom maintains internal standards and policies, reviewed and updated on a quarterly basis."
- 覆盖：Terms of Payment / Delivery and Logistics / Product Quality and Specifications / Warranty Coverage / RMA / Export Compliance / Other General Rights and Obligations。

## 2. Elsa 账号现行邮件体例（从已发邮件归纳，供首触样板对齐）

- 结构：**3 条短特性要点 → 1 个问题（"Do you need some units for testing?"）→ 1 张产品图附件 → 签名**；不写价格、不写公司简介段落、不提产地。
- 主题 = 产品品类名（"24 Port PoE Managed Switch" / "16 Port Industrial PoE Switch" / "OEM Core Switching Solution" …），全部主题时间线见 `docs/gmail/已触达清单说明.md` §2。
- 与本仓首触样板的差异：本仓样板多了"观察 → 相关性"开头与 P1 报价；两者**不混发**（Q-21）。
