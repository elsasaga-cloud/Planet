3. Warranty comes in effect from the date of delivery (trackable by the AWB number)
4. Purchased items are seen as standard or non-defective without negative feedback within 2 months from the date of delivery
5. Warranty is non-transferable and will be solely provided to the original buyer as per the invoice information
6. Immediate contact is strongly advised once any problem occurs, Saga Telecom should be well informed all along before standard solution is provided
7. Please properly keep purchase-related information (purchase order, proforma invoice, packing list etc.) for any possible repair, replacement or after-sales issues
8. Please do not remove or ruin the product label (used to track the material batch in our warehouse) on the device
9. Please do not repair or replace components of purchased items without our knowing or authorization
10. Unauthorized returns are not accepted without a valid confirmation from Saga Telecom, any risk or expenses involved are not covered in such cases
11. At Saga Telecom's option, the disputed device will be preferably repaired or replaced
12. Defective products within warranty but unable to be repaired or discontinued, 10%~20% discount will be offered when the customer purchases the alternative models with identical service features
13. If there are expenses during the repair, Saga Telecom will negotiate with the customer on how to split the cost, and generally give discounts in new orders to cover this cost
14. Saga Telecom reserves final adjudication rights and sole discretion during the warranty period

## Exclusive Liability
1. Out of warranty period

4. Hardware and software compatibility failure
5. Consumable materials and routine maintenance such as a fuse or bracket
