This covers the minor charges that may apply to an order

## PVC Mask
Standard MOQ: 300 pcs. Unit price may vary depending on the model. For quantities below the standard MOQ (e.g. 20 pcs / 50 pcs / 100 pcs), a higher unit price may apply

Examples:
1. Order quantity below 300 pcs (e.g. 50 pcs): In this case, 300 pcs will be produced in total. 50 pcs will be shipped with the order, and the remaining 250 pcs can either: 1.1 be shipped together with the current order, or 1.2 be stored in our warehouse for future orders. Please note that warehouse storage is free of charge for up to one (1) year
2. Order quantity above 300 pcs (e.g. 350 pcs): The exact quantity of 350 pcs will be produced for this order. No remaining balance will be kept

Remark: PVC masks supplied under standard orders are neutral devices without logo

## Certificate of Origin (COO)
USD15 per copy
1. The client is required to review and confirm the content in advance. Please note that the document cannot be modified once confirmed
2. The request must be submitted at least two (2) working days prior to shipment
3. Please advise whether the original copy should be: 3.1 attached to the outside of the package, or 3.2 placed inside the carton
