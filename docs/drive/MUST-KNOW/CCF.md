To enhance efficiency and transparency in daily business negotiations and transactions, a CCF (commercial contract framework) will be established and mutually agreed upon by both parties. Saga Telecom maintains internal standards and policies, reviewed and updated on a quarterly basis, which serve as the baseline for all commercial cooperation

## Scope of the Commercial Framework
The framework covers, but is not limited to, the following areas:
1. Terms of Payment
2. Delivery and Logistics
3. Product Quality and Specifications
4. Warranty Coverage
5. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Procedures
6. Export Compliance and Related Clauses
7. Other General Rights and Obligations

## Flexibility & Applicability
The specific terms under this framework remain open for discussion and adjustment based on individual orders or particular business scenarios. Once the commercial framework is mutually confirmed, it shall serve as the guiding principle for all subsequent transactions between the parties, unless otherwise agreed upon in writing
