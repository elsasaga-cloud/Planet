                 8-Port 100Mbps and 2-Port

  Product Highlights

  n 8*100Mbps PoE ports+2*1Gbps uplink RJ45 ports

  n Comply with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3x standards

  n Ethernet Ports support 10/100Mbps self-adaptive

  n PoE ports automatically activate PoE watchdog mode in VLAN and Extend modes

  n Flow control method: Full duplex uses IEEE 802.3x standard, while half duplex uses Back

    pressure standard

  n Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)

  n The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n Support three modes: VLAN, Normal, and Extend

  n Port 1 supports HiPoE 60W
