           10-Port Gigabit Managed PoE
                      Switch




  Product Highlights

  n 8*1Gbps PoE ports+2*1Gbps SFP ports+1*Console port

  n Support IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3ab, and IEEE802.3z network

    protocols

  n Comply with IEEE802.3at (30W) and IEEE802.3af (15.4W) power supply standards

  n Ethernet Ports support 10/100/1000Mbps self-adaptive

  n Support PoE management and PoE watchdog

  n The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n Support 802.1x port authentication, AAA authentication, TACACS+authentication

  n Support WEB, TELNET, CLI, SSH, SNMP, RMON management
