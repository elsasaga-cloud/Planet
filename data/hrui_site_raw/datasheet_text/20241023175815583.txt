  Product Highlights

  n 8* 1G PoE ports+1 *1G uplink port+1 *1G SFP port

  n Complies with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3ab, and IEEE 802.3x standards

  n RJ45 port supports 10M/100M/1G adaptive

  n PoE port automatically activates PoE watchdog mode in VLAN and Extend modes
