     28-Port Gigabit Managed Switch




  Product Highlights

  n Supports 24*1G RJ45 ports, 4 *1G SFP ports, and 1* Console port

  n Supports IEEE802.3/IEEE802.3i/IEEE802.3u/IEEE802.3ab/IEEE802.3z, storage and forwarding

  n RJ45 port supports 10M/100M/1G adaptation

  n Adopting a storage and forwarding exchange mechanism

  n The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n Supports 802.1x port authentication, AAA authentication, and TACACS+authentication

  n Support WEB, TELNET, CLI, SSH, SNMP, RMON management
