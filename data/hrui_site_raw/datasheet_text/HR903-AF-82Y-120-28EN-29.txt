            10-Port 100Mbps PoE Switch




  Product Highlights
  n 8*100Mbps PoE ports+2*100Mbps uplink RJ45 ports

  n Comply with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3x standards

  n Ethernet Ports support 10/100Mbps self-adaptive

  n PoE ports automatically enable PoE watchdog mode in VLAN and Extend modes

  n Flow control method: Full duplex adopts IEEE 802.3x standard, while half duplex adopts Back

    pressure standard

  n Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)

  n Adopt a storage and forwarding switch system

  n All ports support line speed switching

  n The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n Support VLAN, Default, and Extend modes
