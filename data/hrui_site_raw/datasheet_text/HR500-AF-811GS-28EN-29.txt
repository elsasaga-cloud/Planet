  Product Highlights

  n 8*100Mbps PoE ports + 1*1Gbps uplink RJ45 port + 1*1Gbps SFP port

  n Support IEEE802.3af (15.4W) and IEEE802.3at (30W)

  n PoE ports support PoE watchdog mode

  n IP40 protection level, high-strength shell, DIN35 rail installation

  n Industrial grade 4-level design, operating temperature from -40~75°C

  n Support redundant power supply connection and anti reverse connection

  n Support VLAN, Genius, and Extend modes

  n Port 1 supports HiPoE 60W
