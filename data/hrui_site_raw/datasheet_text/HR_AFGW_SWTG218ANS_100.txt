      8-Port 2.5G and 1-Port 10G
    SFP+ Web Managed PoE Switch




  Product Highlights

  n 8*2.5Gbps PoE ports and 1*10Gbps SFP+ port

  n Comply with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3ab, IEEE 802.3bz, IEEE 802.3ae, IEEE

    802.3x standards

  n Ethernet Ports support 10M/100M/1G/2.5Gbps self-adaptive

  n Support PoE priority, default enabled in order from 1 to 8 ports

  n Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)

  n The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n Flow control method, IEEE802.3x standard for full duplex and Backpressure standard for half

    duplex
