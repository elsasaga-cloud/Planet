                 4G Full Netcom 300M WiFi
                     Wireless Router




  Product Highlights

  n Independent wireless routing processing chip and 4G processing chip module, each performing

    its own function, dual-path concurrency, ensuring the stability and usability of the entire system.

  n Supports 4G access to Telecom, China Unicom, Mobile, and Broadcasting networks, supports

    APN/VPDN connections.

  n Supports routing mode, AP mode, and 4G LTE wireless access, flexible networking, suitable for

    various complex scenarios.

  n Provides 802.11b/g/n WiFi 300M wireless access, equipped with high-gain antennas, achieving

    a range of up to 30 meters in open and unobstructed environments. It also provides

    10/100Mbps wired access, supporting up to 16 devices in standby mode.

  n Supports WPA/WPA2, SSID hiding, and blacklist, effectively ensuring the security of user data.

  n Built-in quick setup wizard, no professional knowledge required, easy to use 4G LTE access for

    WiFi internet.
