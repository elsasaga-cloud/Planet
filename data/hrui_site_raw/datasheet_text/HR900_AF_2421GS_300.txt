              24-Port 100Mbps and 3-Port

  Product Highlights

  n 24 *100Mbps PoE ports+2 *1G RJ45 ports+1 *1G SFP port;

  n Compliant with IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3ab, IEEE802.3x, and IEEE802.3z standards;

  n RJ45 port supports 10/100Mbps adaptation;

  n When the PoE port is in Extend mode, ports 9-24 force 10Mbps to automatically activate the

    PoE watchdog mode;

  n Support PoE priority to be enabled by default, with a priority order of 1-24

  n Flow control method: IEEE 802.3x standard is used for full duplex, and Back pressure standard

    is used for half duplex;

  n The panel indicator LED will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis;

  n Supports three modes: VLAN, Default, and Extend;

  n 1 port supports 60W
