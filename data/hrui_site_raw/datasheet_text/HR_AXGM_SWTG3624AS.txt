             24-Port 2.5G and 6-Port 10G
             SFP+ Managed PoE Switch




  Product Highlights

  n Comply with IEEE 802.1d, IEEE 802.1w, IEEE 802.1s, IEEE 802.1p, IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u,

    IEEE 802.3x, IEEE 802.3z, IEEE 802.3ab, IEEE 802.3ae standards

  n Support L3 management, DHCP Server, QoS, ACL control, SNMP V1/V2/V3, IGMP Snooping

    v1/v2

  n Support Spanning Tree Protocol STP/RSTP/MSTP (ERPS), Loop Detection and Self Healing,

    and Remote Loop Monitoring and Control (802.3ah OAM)

  n Support multiple VLAN partitions, including MAC VLAN and protocol VLAN

  n Support IP address+MAC address+VLAN+port binding, DHCP Snooping, IP source and DAI

    protection

  n Support IPV4/IPV6, RIP v1/v2, OSPFv2

  n Support WEB, TELNET, CLI, SSH, SNMP, RMON management

  n Comply with IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and IEEE802.3bt (90W)

  n Support PoE watchdog, PoE port power supply and power configuration, PoE time management
