                 28-Port Gigabit PoE Switch




  Product Highlights

  n 24* 1G PoE ports+2* 1G RJ45 ports+2 *1G SFP ports;

  n Compliant with IEEE 802.3、IEEE 802.3u、IEEE 802.3ab、IEEE802.3x、IEEE802.3z

    standards;

  n RJ45 port supports 10M/100M/1G adaptation;

  n When the PoE port is in Extend mode, ports 17-24 force 10Mbps to automatically activate the

    PoE watchdog mode;

  n PoE priority is enabled by default, priority order 1-24

  n Flow control method: IEEE 802.3x standard is used for full duplex, and Back pressure standard

    is used for half duplex;

  n The panel indicator LED will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis;

  n Support Extend mode;

  n 1 port supports 60W
