 This type of standard POE splitter, complied with IEEE802.3af, IEEE802.3at, IEEE802.3bt power supply
 standards, can separate the power and data signals that transmitted through the network cable.
 Equipped with two output connection modes: DC plug and industrial Phoenix terminal, the default
 output voltage is 12V, and the maximum output power can reach 72W. The machine adopts metal
 shell design, better protection, side ventilation and heat dissipation, longer life, and the working
 temperature range is -10℃~+50℃.
