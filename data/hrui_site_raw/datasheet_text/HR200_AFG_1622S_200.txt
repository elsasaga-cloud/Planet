                 20-Port Gigabit PoE Switch




  Product Highlights

  n 16* 1G PoE ports+2 *1G RJ45 ports+2 *1G SFP ports;

  n Compliant with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3ab, and IEEE 802.3x standards;

  n RJ45 port supports 10M/100M/1G adaptation

  n When the PoE port is in Extend mode, ports 9-16 force 10Mbps to automatically activate the PoE

    watchdog mode;

  n Support PoE priority

  n Flow control method: IEEE 802.3x standard is used for full duplex, and Back pressure standard is used

    for half duplex;

  n The panel indicator LED will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n Support Extend mode

  n 1 port supports 60W
