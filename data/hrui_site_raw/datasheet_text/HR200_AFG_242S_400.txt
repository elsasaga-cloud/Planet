                 26-Port Gigabit PoE Switch




  Product Highlights

  n 24* 1G PoE ports+2 *1G SFP ports

  n Complies with IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3ab, and IEEE802.3z standards

  n Compatible with IEEE802.3at (30W) and IEEE802.3af (15.4W)

  n RJ45 port supports 10M/100M/1G adaptive and PoE functionality

  n Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)

  n The panel indicator LED will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

  n PoE priority is enabled by default, with priority order from 1 to 24 ports

  n Supports three modes: VLAN, Default, and Extend
