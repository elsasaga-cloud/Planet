HR100W-FE-2 - 10/100M Multi-mode Dual Fiber Media Converter

HR100W-FE-2

10/100M multi-mode dual fiber media converter with 1* 10/100M RJ45 port + 1*10/100M uplink SC port, the default transmission distance is 2Km, external power supply. The media converter adopts international advanced chip and design to ensure the stability and practicability. With ultra-low transmission delay and line speed forwarding ability, it can realizes flexible Ethernet expansion even in harsh environment.

    The HR100W-FE-2 is a cutting-edge 10/100M single-mode dual fiber media converter designed to enhance your network capabilities. Featuring one 10/100M RJ45 port and one 10/100M uplink SC port, this versatile device supports a default transmission distance of up to 25 km with an external power supply. Built with advanced international chips and superior design, the HR100W-FE-25 ensures exceptional stability and practicality. Its ultra-low transmission delay and efficient line speed forwarding capability enable seamless Ethernet expansion, even in challenging environments.


    Product Features:

    High-Efficiency Switching Core: Equipped with a built-in switching core that effectively manages flow control and reduces broadcast packets, ensuring optimal performance.

    Automatic Adaptation: Automatically adapts to 10/100Mbps network environments, facilitating smooth and efficient network upgrades without manual configuration.

    Full-Duplex & Half-Duplex Support: Automatically negotiates transmission modes for greater flexibility.

    Packet Handling: Capable of handling packet sizes of 1536-1552 bytes, ensuring smoother monitoring and data transmission.

    Comprehensive Power and Status Indicators: Equipped with clear indicator lights for easy monitoring of working conditions.

    Low Power Consumption: Designed for low heat generation, ensuring long-term stable operation with enhanced network security.

    Versatile Fiber Options: Compatible with dual-fiber multimode, dual-fiber single-mode, single-fiber single-mode, and multiple fiber interface configurations.

    Features and Highlights

    • Built-in high efficiency switching core to achieve flow control and reduce broadcast packet
    • Automatically adapt to 10/100Mbps network environment, free conversion network upgrade
    • Support full-duplex and half-duplex transmission mode, automatic negotiation
    • Support 1536-1552 byte packets, monitoring and transmission more smooth
    • Complete power link, working mode indicator light, working state at a glance
    • Low power consumption low heat, long time stable work, network security advanced
    • Support dual-fiber multimode, dual-fiber single-mode,single-fiber single-mode,and multiple fiber interface options


    Application Environment

    • Metro Optical Broadband Network: Data network operators such as telecommunications, cable TV, and network system integration,etc.
    • Broadband private network: Suitable for financial, government, oil, railway, electric power, public security, transportation, education and other industries.
    • Multimedia transmission: Integrated transmission of images, voice and data, suitable for remote teaching, conference TV, videophone and other applications.
    • Real-time monitoring: Simultaneous transmission of real-time control signals, images and data.
