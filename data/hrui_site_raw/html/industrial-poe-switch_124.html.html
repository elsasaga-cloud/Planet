HR600-AFGM-SWG2086S-BP

HR600-AFGM-SWG2086S-BP

Full Gigabit ring network industiral managed PoE switch, integrated with bypass protection function, 8*Gigabit PoE ports + 6*Gigabit uplink SFP ports + 1*Console port, bandwidth 28Gbps, Layer 2 management, support ERPS protocol, ring network redundancy, IP40 protection, Surge 6KV, ESD 15KV, DC48-55V power input, redundant power supply, adapt to -40°C~+85°C wide temperature

    The HR600-AFGM-SWG2086S-BP is a robust 14-Port Gigabit Industrial Ethernet PoE Switch engineered for high-performance and reliability in demanding environments. This switch features 8 Gigabit PoE ports, 6 Gigabit SFP ports, and 1 Console Port, offering versatile connectivity options. Its integrated bypass protection function ensures continuous operation, making it ideal for mission-critical applications. Designed for both DIN rail and wall mounting, it supports dual power inputs (DC 48-55V) and operates effectively in extreme temperatures ranging from -40°C to 75°C. Built to meet stringent industrial standards, the switch is certified for compliance with FCC, CE, CCC, and RoHS, guaranteeing a safe and sound investment for your automation systems.

    This industrial-grade switch is versatile enough to be used in a wide range of applications, including wind power generation, electric utilities, assembly automation, subway passenger information systems (PIS), electric power SCADA, sewage treatment, metallurgy, intelligent transportation, rail transportation, military operations, and more.

    The HR600-AFGM-SWG2086S-BP is an environmentally-friendly, low-power industrial Ethernet switch designed to optimize your network while ensuring minimal energy consumption.


    Product Features:

    Port Configuration: 8 Gigabit PoE Ports, 6 Gigabit SFP Ports, and 1 Console Port for flexible network design.

    Standards Compliance: Supports IEEE 802.3 series protocols including 802.3, 802.3u, 802.3x, 802.3z/ab, 802.1q, 802.1p, and 802.1d/w for broad compatibility.

    PoE Management: Includes PoE Management and a PoE Watchdog based on data stream detection for optimal power usage.

    Bypass Protection: Built-in bypass protection function to maintain uninterrupted service during power outages or failures.

    PoE Standards Supported: IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W)

    Spanning Tree Protocols: STP (802.1D), RSTP (802.1w), MSTP (802.1s), ERPS.

    Network Security: Support for SNMP v1/v2/v3, IGMP snooping, static multicast filtering, and MLD snooping.

    Authentication and Management: Supports 802.1x port access and AAA certification for enhanced security, with management options via WEB, Telnet, CLI, and SSH.

    Durability: IP40 rated protection with a rugged, high-strength metal chassis, suitable for DIN35 rail mounting.

    Redundant Power Supply: Supports redundant power supply connections with anti-reverse protection.

    Surge Protection: Features surge protection of 6KV general, 4KV differential, and ESD protection of 15KV (air) and 8KV (contact).

    Environmental Resilience: EMC grade 4 design that supports operational stability in harsh conditions, adhering to EN55032 standard.
