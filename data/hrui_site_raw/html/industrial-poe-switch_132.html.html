HR600-AXGM-SWG2086AS-BP

HR600-AXGM-SWG2086AS-BP

2.5G ring network industiral managed BT90W PoE switch, integrated with Bypass protection function, 8*Gigabit PoE ports + 6*Gigabit uplink SFP ports + 1*Console port, 1-2 ports support BT90W power supply, 13-14 ports support 2.5G, bandwidth 34Gbps, Layer 2 management, support ERPS protocol, ring network redundancy, IP40 protection, Surge 6KV, ESD 15KV, DC48-55V power input, redundant power supply, adapt to -40°C~+85°C wide temperature

    HR600-AXGM-SWG2086AS-BP, a cutting-edge 2.5G industrial managed PoE switch designed for robust, high-performance networking in challenging environments. This switch features a comprehensive 14-port configuration that includes 8 Gigabit PoE ports and 6 Gigabit uplink SFP ports, complemented by a console port for straightforward management.

    With its advanced bypass protection function, this switch ensures uninterrupted operation by maintaining data flow even during power outages. Ports 1-2 are designed for BT90W power supply, while ports 13-14 provide support for 2.5G connections, delivering a remarkable bandwidth of 34Gbps. Layer 2 management capabilities and support for the ERPS protocol enhance network reliability with ring network redundancy.

    Built with durability in mind, the HR600-AXGM-SWG2086AS-BP is IP40 rated for protection against dust and solid debris, featuring a rugged metal casing that can withstand extreme temperatures from -40°C to +85°C. Additional features include surge protection up to 6KV and ESD protection of 15KV, ensuring reliable operation in industrial settings.


    Product Features:

    Port Configuration: 8 Gigabit PoE ports (Ports 1-2 support BT90W) + 6 Gigabit SFP uplink ports (Ports 13-14 support 2.5G, compatible with 1.25G/155M) + 1 console port.

    Standards Compliance: Supports IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3z/ab, IEEE802.1q, IEEE802.1p, and IEEE802.1d/w protocols.

    Power Management: Advanced PoE power management with PoE watchdog based on data stream detection.

    Bypass Protection: Integrated bypass protection function ensures uninterrupted service.

    PoE Support: Compatible with IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and IEEE802.3bt (90W).

    Redundant Power Supply: Supports dual power supply access and anti-reverse connection for added reliability.

    Network Protocols: Supports STP (802.1D), RSTP (802.1w), MSTP (802.1s), and ERPS for enhanced network topology control.

    Management Options: Web-based, Telnet, CLI, and SSH management interfaces for easy configuration and monitoring.

    Security Features: 802.1x port access and AAA certification enhance network security.

    Durability: IP40-rated metal enclosure, designed for DIN35 rail mounting, with EMC grade 4 design.

    Operating Environment: Wide operating temperature range of -40°C to 75°C, making it ideal for harsh environments.

    Surge and ESD Protection: Offers general surge protection (6KV) and differential surge protection (4KV), along with ESD protection (15KV air, 8KV contact).

    EMC Compliance: Meets EMC EN55032 standards to ensure minimal electromagnetic interference.
