    HR722P-8T2GS-X, a robust 10-Port Gigabit Industrial Managed PoE Switch designed for high-performance and reliability in demanding environments. Ideal for industrial applications, this switch seamlessly integrates power over Ethernet (PoE) with advanced management features, ensuring reliable connectivity for all your network devices.


    Product Features:

    Comprehensive Protocol Support: Compatible with a wide range of protocols including IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3z/ab, IEEE802.1Q, IEEE802.1p, and IEEE802.1d/w to ensure versatile and seamless network integration.

    Intelligent PoE Management: Equipped with PoE power management capabilities and PoE Watchdog that utilizes data flow detection, you can prioritize power distribution and maintain device uptime.

    Robust PoE Standards: Supports IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and the high-capacity IEEE802.3bt (90W) standards—ideal for powering a variety of devices including IP cameras, wireless access points, and more.

    Cloud Management: Harness the power of the HRUI Cloud for simplified network management, allowing for remote access and configuration.

    Enhanced Management Protocols: Full SNMP v1/v2/v3 support enables streamlined network monitoring and management.

    Advanced Multicasting Features: Incorporates IGMP Snooping, static multicast filtering, and MLD Snooping filtering to optimize multicasting and reduce bandwidth overhead.

    Robust Security Protocols: Features 802.1x port authentication and AAA authentication, providing an extra layer of security for your network.

    Flexible Management Options: Offer multiple management interfaces including web, Telnet, CLI, and SSH for easy configuration and monitoring.

    Durable and Reliable Design: Constructed with an IP40 protection level and a high-strength shell, this switch is engineered for DIN35 rail installation, making it suitable for industrial environments.

    Extreme Operating Conditions: Designed for industrial applications with a wide operating temperature range of -40°C to 75°C, ensuring consistent performance in harsh conditions.

    Redundant Power Supply Support: Features redundant power supply capabilities with anti-reverse connection for enhanced reliability and uptime.


    The HRUI Gigabit managed industrial PoE switches offer a range of port configurations to suit various application requirements. Certain models support the IEEE 802.3af/at/bt standards, with select ports providing up to 90W PoE power for powering high-demanding PD devices.

    These products provide robust L2+ management capabilities for enhanced control and operational flexibility. Furthermore, it supports HRUI cloud management, enabling convenient remote 

    monitoring and administration.

    With the design of industrial-grade durability, these switches feature redundant power inputs for increased system reliability. The rugged IP40 aluminum alloy housing ensures efficient heat dissipation and the stable operation in a wide temperature range of -40°C to 75°C, which makes them suitable for extreme conditions.  These switches are ideal for applications such as industrial automation, intelligent transportation systems, and security monitoring.


    IEEE 802.3af/at/bt PoE Standard

    These products use advanced PoE++ (IEEE 802.3af/at/bt) with up to 90W per port, ideal for high-power devices like wireless APs and PTZ cameras. They offer smart power management, real-time monitoring, and energy savings.  Furthermore, they support SFP ports for fiber transmission up to 20km.



    Integrated PoE Features for Managing PD

    PoE Configuration

    Provide configuration support for PoE settings, including parameters such as temperature, type, level, power, current, total PoE power budget management, PoE power control, port priority allocations, and individualized power limits for each PoE port. This thorough strategy will optimize PoE management, promote efficient resource utilization, and improve device reliability and performance.


    PoE Schedule

    Support for customizing the schedule to turn the PoE function on or off based on specific needs. This flexibility not only helps optimize energy consumption but also reduces operational costs, making it an efficient solution for energy management.



    PoE Timer Management

    Support rebooting the PoE function of port during a specified time.  This will help reduce device crashes caused by buffer overflow, ensuring more reliable and stable performance during designated time intervals.



    Supports L2+ Network Management

    This product offers a comprehensive suite of L2+ network management features, including support for VLAN, port mirroring, traffic control, LACP, QoS, and DHCP Snooping, among others. It is designed to meet a variety of complex network requirements, enhancing network security, reliability, and scalability.



    Unified Management in the Cloud: Accessible, Scalable, and Efficient

    Provide cloud-based management for remote equipment monitoring and control, simplifying detection, configuration, deployment, and maintenance to reduce costs and improve efficiency and reliability.



    Secure Networking

    The products feature advanced cybersecurity measures, including SSHv2 and SNMPv3 protocols, to protect against cyber threats. Its robust security capabilities enhance switch management while maintaining cost-effective deployment for critical network operations.



    Enhanced Fiber Module DDM Capabilities

    Support for Fiber Module DDM (Digital Diagnostic Monitoring) includes tracking key parameters such as transceiver supply voltage, temperature, transmitter bias current, transmitter output power, and received optical power. By analyzing these metrics, you can effectively diagnose issues that may be affecting the transceiver optics performance.



    Industrial Design Enabling Durable Applications

    The products feature aluminum alloy casings, offering exceptional heat dissipation and mechanical strength. They are capable of stable operation across a broad temperature range (-40°C to 75°C), making them particularly suitable for application scenarios such as industrial sites and outdoor base stations.



    Triple Power Input Design

    The device features triple power inputs, enabling you to deploy multiple power sources for enhanced reliability and redundancy. This design guarantees that your system stays operational during power failures or unexpected shutdowns.
