HR-AFGW-242S-400

HR-AFGW-242S-400

The 26 Port Gigabit Web Managed Ethernet PoE Switch adopts high-quality and high-speed network IC. WIth the fan-less and silent design, this model completely solves the problem of high noise when the switches are deployed in an office environment. This switch has 24* Gigabit PoE RJ45 ports+ 2* Gigabit SFP  ports, which can provide seamless connection for 10/100/1000M Ethernet, supports WEB management, and is easy to manage and maintain. This product can be widely used in security network video surveillance, hotels, campuses, and small and medium-sized enterprises, network engineering and other occasions.

    HR-AFGW-242S-400, a state-of-the-art 26-port Gigabit Web-Managed Ethernet PoE Switch that delivers superior performance and reliability. Engineered with high-quality, high-speed network ICs, this switch effectively addresses the noise concerns commonly associated with smaller switches found in office environments, thanks to its innovative fanless design that ensures silent operation.

    Equipped with 24 Gigabit RJ45 ports and 2 SFP ports, it provides seamless connectivity for 10/100/1000 Mbps Ethernet. The switch supports three operational modes, including a one-key smart switching function for effortless management and maintenance. This versatile device is ideal for a wide range of applications, including security network video surveillance, hotels, campuses, and network deployments in small to medium-sized enterprises.


    Product Features:

    Port Configuration: 24 x 1Gbps PoE ports and 2 x 1Gbps SFP ports.

    Standards Compliance: Fully compliant with IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3ab, and IEEE802.3z standards.

    Adaptive Port Support: 10/100/1000Mbps self-adaptive ports for optimal performance.

    Auto MDI/MDIX Functionality: Support for Auto MDI/MDIX ensures hassle-free connections.

    Advanced Switching Technology: Utilizes a store-and-forward switch mechanism for enhanced data management.

    Non-Blocking Architecture: All ports facilitate line-speed switching for maximum throughput.

    User-Friendly Monitoring: Indicator LEDs provide real-time status updates and aid in fault diagnosis.

    Quality of Service (QoS): Prioritizes data packets to enhance performance for critical applications.
