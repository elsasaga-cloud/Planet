HR-BTG-PD12F

HR-BTG-PD12F

Gigabit BT PoE Splitter, complied with IEEE802.3af/at/bt standard, can separates the power and data signals that transmitted through the network cable. Equipped with two output connection modes: DC plug and industrial Phoenix terminal, the default output voltage is 12V, and the maximum output power can reach 72W. The device adopts metal shell design, better protection, side ventilation and heat dissipation, longer life, and the working temperature range is -10℃~+50℃

    The HR-BTG-PD12F is a cutting-edge standard Power over Ethernet (PoE) splitter designed to comply with IEEE 802.3af, IEEE 802.3at, and IEEE 802.3bt power supply standards. It efficiently separates power and data signals transmitted over network cables, making it an ideal solution for various applications. This versatile device features dual output connection modes—DC plug and industrial Phoenix terminal—ensuring compatibility with a wide range of devices. With a default output voltage of 12V and a maximum output power of 72W, you can rely on this splitter for robust performance. The HR-BTG-PD12F is housed in a durable metal shell that enhances protection against external damage, while its side ventilation design allows for optimal heat dissipation, ensuring longevity. It operates effectively within a temperature range of -10℃ to +50℃, making it suitable for both indoor and outdoor environments.


    Product Features:

    Multiple Port: Equipped with 1*1000M PoE-in Port + 1*1000M Data-out Port

    Standard Compliance: Fully compliant with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, and IEEE 802.3af/at standards for reliable performance.

    Flexible Output Options: DC power output options include a DC plug and a 2-pin industrial Phoenix terminal connection for enhanced usability.

    High Power Output: Delivers a maximum output power of 72W, accommodating power-hungry devices with ease.

    Robust Metal Housing: Constructed with a metal shell for improved durability and protection against environmental factors.

    Wide Operating Temperature Range: Designed to function effectively in diverse environments, with a temperature range of -10℃ to +50℃, ensuring performance stability in various conditions.

    Features and Highlights

    • 1*1000M PoE-in Port + 1*1000M Data-out Port
    • Comply with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE802.3af/at/bt standards
    • The voltage output supports DC plug and 2 PIN industrial Phoenix terminal connection
    • DC power output maximum 72W
    • Metal housing design, with better protection
    • Wide working temperature -10℃~+50℃


    Application Environment

    • Metro Optical Broadband Network: Data network operators such as telecommunications, cable TV, and network system integration, etc.
    • Broadband private network: Suitable for financial, government, oil, railway, electric power, public security, transportation, education and other industries.
    • Multimedia transmission: Integrated transmission of images, voice and data, suitable for remote teaching, conference TV, videophone and other applications.
    • Real-time monitoring: Simultaneous transmission of real-time control signals, images and data.
    • Resistant to harsh environments: Suitable for networking in harsh environments with strong electromagnetic interference and long distances.
