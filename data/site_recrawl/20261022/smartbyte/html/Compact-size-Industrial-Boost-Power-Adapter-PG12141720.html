Compact size Industrial Boost Power Adapter

LT-IBPA52-1O2-M

Download Annex

  • Overview
  • Features
  • Technical specification
  • Structure diagram
  • Order information

The SmartByte LT-IBPA52-1O2-M is the compact size industrial booster power adapter with 1-port DC 12-48V input, 2-port DC 52V output. It is the ideal PoE solution that if the power source is less than 40V, the LT-IBPA52-1O2-M can booster the power source to redundant 52V output. The Max PoE power budget can be 60W if input voltage is DC 12-23V and Max PoE power budget can be 120W if input voltage is DC 24-48V

 

LT-IBPA52-1O2-M is also high cost-effective easy-to-use device, which is specifically designed with durable components and strong housing case to operate reliably in electrically harsh and climatically demanding environments in a wide temperature range of -40 to 75 degrees. The LT-IBPA52-1O2-M is packaged in a compact, IP40 rugged case that allows either DIN-rail or wall mounting to have the efficient use of cabinet space. It is ideal for service providers, campuses and public areas to deploy PoE wireless access points, IP cameras or IP phones in any places easily and efficiently.


➢ Allow low input voltage, such as 12/24/48VDC be boost up to 52VDC to meet IEEE802.3af/IEEE802.3at/bt PoE requirement

➢ 1*DC 12-48V input, 2*DC 52V output

➢ DC 12-23V input support Max 60W PoE budget; DC 24-48V input support Max 120W PoE budget;

➢ Support 6KV surge protection and ESD: Air-15kV, Contact-8kV Protection

➢ IP40 fan-less and Din-rail hardware design with compact size(30*85*85mm)

➢  Operation temperature: -40 ℃ ~+75℃
