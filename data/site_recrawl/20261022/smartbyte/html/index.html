Core Assurance for Products and R&D


35% of our workforce are dedicated R&D engineers, and core team members possess over 15 years of experience in industrial networking product development and project management.

With in-house PCBA design capabilities, we can quickly respond to customer customization requirements, including board redesign, feature addition/removal, and cost optimization solutions.

We are proficient in industrial embedded firmware development, with full mastery of core industrial communication protocols, enabling protocol adaptation and customized development based on customer needs.

Our R&D is driven by a production-ready mindset, allowing rapid private customization and version iterations to ensure stable delivery of bulk equipment orders.

With deep expertise in Marvell chip platforms, we can lead the entire R&D process for switches that meet stringent industrial-grade specifications.

Production and Supply Chain Guarantee


Independent production base covers an area of 8,000 square meters, located in the Pearl River Delta of China with world-leading supply chain advantages.

Supported by Marvell original factory, with obvious advantages in cost and delivery lead time.

Top-tier factory environment and production management system: The factory adopts information-based management（ERP）, 5S on-site management, Kanban management, and strictly implements SOP.

Equipped with 5 production lines, a full set of professionally certified anti-static grounding systems (covering production lines, warehouse shelves and laboratories), automatic full-port full-load traffic network testing equipment, and 5 sets of customized large-capacity high-temperature aging chambers.

Equipped with multiple IXIA network testers, surge testers, dry ice PCB cleaning machines, ESD guns, plastic sealing machines, vibration testers and other professional equipment.

Quality Control Core Assurance


We have established a full-link quality control system covering the entire New Product Introduction (NPI) process, including Engineering Verification Test (EVT), Design Verification Test (DVT), and Production Verification Test (PVT), to ensure product consistency and stability from design finalization to mass delivery.

Leveraging a comprehensive system encompassing Incoming Quality Control (IQC), In-Process Quality Control (IPQC), Final Quality Control (FQC), Outgoing Quality Control (OQC), and standardized system management, we implement full-life cycle closed-loop quality control from raw materials to finished products.

We adopt high-quality electronic components from leading European and American brands. Production is implemented in strict accordance with standard operating procedures (SOP). All products are subject to 8-hour full-coverage high-temperature aging test prior to shipment.

Main Environmental and Transportation Reliability Tests: High-low Temperature Test, Dust Protection Test, Vibration Test, Aging Test, ESD Test (Electrostatic Discharge Test), Surge Test, Drop Test.

     SMARTBYTE is specialized in industrial network interconnection, with business covering more than 60 countries and regions, and is a leading provider of industrial communication network solutions in the industry.
