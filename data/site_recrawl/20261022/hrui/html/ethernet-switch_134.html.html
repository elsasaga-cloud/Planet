HR-SWG0080

HR-SWG0080

The 8-port Gigabit intelligent Ethernet switch adopts high-quality and high-speed network ICs, and this product completely solves the problem of noise of small switches in the office environment through the fanless mute design. This switch has 8 Gigabit RJ45 ports, can provide seamless connection for 10/100/1000M Ethernet, the product can be widely used in Security network video surveillance, hotel, campus, small and medium-sized enterprise network engineering and other occasions.

    HRUI advanced 8-Port Gigabit Intelligent Ethernet Switch, designed with top-tier performance and cutting-edge technology. This high-speed switch utilizes premium network ICs to deliver unparalleled connectivity while completely eliminating the noise commonly associated with smaller switches, thanks to its innovative fanless, silent operation.


    Equipped with 8 Gigabit RJ45 ports, this versatile switch ensures a seamless connection across various Ethernet speeds—10, 100, and 1000 Mbps. Its robust capabilities make it an ideal solution for a wide range of applications, including security network video surveillance, hotel connectivity, campus networks, and small to medium-sized enterprise networking.


    Product Features:

    8 x 1000 Mbps RJ45 Ports: Experience lightning-fast data transfers and reliable connections.

    Standards Compliance: Fully compliant with IEEE 802.3, IEEE 802.3u, IEEE 802.3az, and IEEE 802.3ab standards to guarantee compatibility across different devices.

    Adaptive Port Support: Each RJ45 port automatically adjusts to 100 or 1000 Mbps speeds, ensuring optimal performance without manual intervention.

    Auto MDI/MDIX: Enjoy hassle-free installation and connectivity with support for automatic port flipping, eliminating crossover cables.

    Status Monitoring: Panel indicator lights provide real-time insights into the working status of the switch, aiding in efficient fault diagnosis.

    Flow Control: Incorporates IEEE 802.3x flow control for full duplex and Backpressure standard for half-duplex transmission, maintaining data integrity and network stability.

    Features and Highlights

    • 8*1000Mbps RJ45 ports
    • Compliant with IEEE802.3 and IEEE802.3u,IEEE802.3az,IEEE802.3ab standards
    • RJ45 port supports 100/1000Mbps adaptive
    • Supports automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)
    • The panel indicator lights will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis
    • Flow control method, IEEE802.3x standard for full duplex and Backpressure standard for half duplex
