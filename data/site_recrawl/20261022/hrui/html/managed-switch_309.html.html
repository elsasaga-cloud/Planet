HR530S-24S2CQ

HR530S-24S2CQ

NTP 0.1-1ms high-precision synchronization | lPSLA network monitoring | MLAG link aggregation | NAT address translation | VRRP redundancy protection | OSPF routing protocol | ERPS<50ms | 880Gbps Non-blockingswitching capacity

    HR530S-24S2CQ is a high-performance Layer 3 managed switch for modern data centers and enterprise core networks, designed to meet the network demands of high bandwidth, low latency, and high reliability. Equipped with 24 10GbE SFP+ optical ports and 2 100GbE QSFP28 uplink ports, the switch supports 40G/100G multi-rate adaptation and port splitting (which can be split into 25G or 10G modes). It also provides one console management port, one ETH management port, and one USB port for easy maintenance and configuration backup, offering flexible networking and efficient bandwidth expansion.


    Product Features:

    Layer 3 Management: Supports advanced Layer 3 features, including DHCP server, QoS (Quality of Service), ACL (Access Control List) control, and SNMP V1/V2/V3, for comprehensive network management.

    Standards Compliance: Compatible with IEEE 802.3, 802.3ae, 802.3ba, and other Ethernet protocols, ensuring high-performance data transmission and interoperability.

    Advanced Forwarding Mode: Supports cut-through forwarding mode for ultra-low fixed latency, ideal for high-frequency trading and real-time applications.

    High-Speed Connectivity: Multi-rate support of 10G/25G/40G/100G provides flexible bandwidth options and future-proof scalability.

    Powerful Management Options: Management via web, TELNET, SSH, SNMP, and RMON protocols provides comprehensive network control.

    Real-Time Monitoring: Supports standard 19-inch rack mounting for easy integration into data center environments.

    Rack-mountable design: Status indicator LEDs provide instant display of port status and system performance.

    Operating Temperature: Designed to operate from 0°C to 45°C, ensuring reliability in controlled data center environments.
