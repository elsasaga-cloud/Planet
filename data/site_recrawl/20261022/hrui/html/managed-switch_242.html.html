HR200-AFGM-SWTG3424S-400

HR200-AFGM-SWTG3424S-400

10G SFP+ uplink L3+ managed PoE switch, 24*Gigabit PoE ports + 4*10G SFP+ ports, complies with IEEE 802.3af/at standard, 400W PoE power budget, bandwidth is up to 128Gbps, supports ERPS ring protocol, Surge 6KV, ESD 8KV, black metal shell, double fans design, suitable for -10~50℃ wide temperature environment.

    HR200-AFGM-SWTG3424S-400, a high-performance L3+ managed PoE switch designed for modern enterprise demands. This robust switch features 24 Gigabit PoE ports and 4 10G SFP+ uplink ports, delivering outstanding power and speed. Complying with IEEE 802.3af/at standards, it offers a substantial 400W PoE power budget, facilitating versatile device connections. With a total bandwidth capacity of up to 128Gbps, this switch supports the ERPS ring protocol, ensuring reliable network redundancy and efficiency. The HR200-AFGM-SWTG3424S-400 is built to withstand rigorous environments, sporting a durable black metal shell and a double fan design, making it ideal for a wide temperature range of -10°C to 50°C. Additionally, it is equipped with surge protection rated at 6KV and ESD protection at 8KV, safeguarding your network from electrical disturbances.


    Product Features:

    Standards Compliance: Adheres to a comprehensive array of networking standards, including IEEE 802.1d, 802.1w, 802.1s, 802.1p, 802.3, 802.3u, 802.3x, 802.3z, 802.3ab, and 802.3ae.

    Layer 3 Management: Supports robust L3 management functionalities, including DHCP Server capabilities, Quality of Service (QoS), Access Control Lists (ACL), and SNMP (v1/v2/v3).

    Enhanced Network Stability: Features Spanning Tree Protocol (STP/RSTP/MSTP with ERPS) for loop detection and self-healing, along with remote loop monitoring and control to maintain optimal network performance.

    Flexible VLAN Partitioning: Offers versatile VLAN partitioning, allowing for both MAC VLAN and protocol VLAN configurations to segment network traffic securely.

    Robust Security Features: Offers features like IP address + MAC address + VLAN + port binding, DHCP Snooping, and IP Source Guard (DAI) protection to optimize security.

    IP Protocol Support: Fully compatible with both IPv4 and IPv6, and supports routing protocols such as RIP v1/v2 and OSPFv2, ensuring future-proofing for networking needs.

    Comprehensive Management Options: Manage and configure the switch through various interfaces such as WEB, TELNET, CLI, SSH, SNMP, and RMON, providing flexibility to suit your administration preferences.
