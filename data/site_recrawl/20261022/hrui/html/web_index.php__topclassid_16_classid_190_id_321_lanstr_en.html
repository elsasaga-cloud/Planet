HR200-BTG5-N901

HR200-BTG5-N901

5G IEEE 802.3af/at/bt PoE injector, 1*AC power port + 1*5G Ethernet Port + 1*5G PoE port, 90W maximum PoE power output, support 10/100/1000M/2.5G/5G self-adaption, working temperature range 0°C~+40°C, surge & ESD protection, PoE port common mode 6KV differential mode 2KV, ESD contact 6KV air 8KV

    The HR200-BTG5-N901 is a high-performance 5G PoE injector designed to deliver stable power and high-speed data transmission for bandwidth-intensive network devices. With up to 90W PoE output, it ensures reliable operation for high-power applications in demanding network environments.Its compact design supports both desktop and wall-mounted installation, making it an ideal solution for deploying high-speed PoE devices such as WiFi 6 access points, PTZ HD cameras, digital signage, and industrial terminals.


    Product Features:

    High-Speed Connectivity: Equipped with 1 x 5G PoE power supply port and 1 x 5G RJ45 data port, ensuring high-speed data transmission while supplying power simultaneously.

    Adaptive Speed: Ethernet Ports support 10M/100M/1G/2.5G/5Gbps self-adaptive.

    Standards Compatibility: Fully compatible with industry standards IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and IEEE802.3bt (90W), allowing for flexibility in powering a wide range of devices.

    Wide Input Voltage Range: Supports an input voltage range of AC 100-240V, making it suitable for global use without the need for voltage converters.

    High Protection Performance: Port surge protection (differential mode 2KV, common mode 6KV); ESD electrostatic discharge protection (contact 6KV, air 8KV)

    Durable Construction: Housed in a high-quality metal casing, the injector is designed for durability and enhanced thermal management.

    Environmental Resistance: With an IP20 protection level, the device is safeguarded against dust and requires minimal maintenance, making it perfect for various environments.

    Versatile Operating Conditions: Operates effectively in a temperature range of  0 to 40 °C, ensuring reliability in diverse outdoor and indoor environments.

    Flexible Installation: Supports desktop or wall mounting for easy deployment.
