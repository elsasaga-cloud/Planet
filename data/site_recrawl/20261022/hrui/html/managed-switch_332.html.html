HR210P-24T2MS-400

HR210P-24T2MS-400

6KV Port Surge protection | 8KV ESD protection | Dynamic LACP | IGMP Snooping | DHCP Snooping | IP DSCP/CoS Priority  | PoE Budget Indicator | Mgmt VLAN & DNS  | Enhanced PoE Setup

    HR210P-24T2MS-400 is a Layer 2 Web Managed Gigabit PoE Switch designed for SMB, enterprise and IP surveillance networks. Featuring 24 Gigabit PoE+ ports, dual 2.5G SFP uplinks and a 380W PoE budget, it delivers reliable power and high-speed connectivity for IP cameras, wireless access points, VoIP phones and other PoE devices. Advanced Layer 2 management including VLAN, QoS, LACP, STP/RSTP/MSTP, IGMP Snooping and PoE Watchdog ensures stable and efficient network operation.


    Product Features:

    ■ Support 24*1Gbps PoE ports++2*2.5Gbps SFP ports significantly reduce congestion

    ■ Comply with IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3x, IEEE802.3ab, and IEEE802.3z standards

    ■ Ethernet Ports support 10/100/1000Mbps self-adaptive

    ■ Support automatic port flipping (Auto MDI/MDIX)

    ■ Adopt a storage and forwarding exchange mechanism

    ■ All ports support line speed switching

    ■ The panel indicator LEDs will monitor the working status and assist in fault analysis

    ■ Support QoS data priority

    ■ Support web management
