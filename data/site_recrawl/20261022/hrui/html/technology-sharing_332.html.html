HRUI Long Reach PoE Technology Solution

2024-11-27

What is Long Reach Power over Ethernet(PoE) Technology?



When designing an IP networking infrastructure, administrators often face critical challenges related to distance, power sourcing, and cable deployment for optimal network coverage. These factors can significantly impact the effectiveness and reach of the network.

To address these challenges, HRUI has introduced a cutting-edge Long Reach PoE solution specifically engineered for modern networking demands. This innovative technology allows for the transmission of data and power over extended distances, exceeding the traditional 100-meter limit. As a result, it provides an efficient and cost-effective way to establish IP infrastructure in remote locations—eliminating concerns about power sources and the need for extensive re-cabling.

With Long Reach PoE Solution, network administrators can effortlessly extend their reach, ensuring reliable connectivity wherever it is needed. This solution not only enhances network coverage but also reduces overall deployment costs, making it an ideal choice for contemporary networking projects.


Harnessing the Power of Long Reach Networking Through Diverse Media Platforms


Single-port PoE Extender with Extend 800m Transmission

The Long Reach PoE Extender is designed to transmit power and data to the PoE IP camera, PoE outdoor Wireless APs, PoE indoor WirelessAPs and any 802.3af/at PoE PD in a remote area. Powered by the PoE switch, the Long Reach PoE Extender, a single-port 802.3at PoE+ device, provides a maximum of 30W power output over Ethernet cable where data and power are transmitted to any PoE PD at the same time. And the data and power transmission is up to 800m limited.




250-meter PoE Extender

The Long Reach PoE Extender is designed to transmit power and data to the PoE IP camera, PoE outdoor Wireless APs, PoE indoor WirelessAPs and any 802.3af/at PoE PD in a remote area. Powered by the PoE switch, the Long Reach PoE Extender, a single-port 802.3at PoE+ device, provides a maximum of 30W power output over Ethernet cable where data and power are transmitted to any PoE PD at the same time.



PoE Switch with Extend Mode

HRUI Long Reach PoE (LRP) solution is designed to extend IP Ethernet transmission and inject power simultaneously over any existing UTP for distance up to 1000m into PoE IP camera, PoE outdoor Wireless APs, PoE indoor Wireless APs and other 802.3af/at complied powered devices. The solution also eliminates the need for an additional power supply for remote sites when the existing single power source enables to provide power to both extenders and the PDs at long range.




Advantages of HRUI Long Reach PoE Solution


Fast Recovery and Reliable Long-Distance Transmission

The HRUI Long Reach PoE solution excels in delivering dependable data and power transmission over extended distances, making it suitable for various environments. In the event of a connection failure, it can restore network connectivity in under 1 second—30 times quicker than standard Ethernet technology. This ensures minimal downtime and maximum productivity for your operations.


Effortless Plug-and-Play Setup

Designed for convenience, HRUI Long Reach PoE switches and extenders feature a plug-and-play functionality that allows for immediate use without any complicated configuration. Additionally, these devices are fully compliant with a wide range of IP network protocols, simplifying the installation process for point-to-point network applications.


Intelligent Point-to-Multi-Point Solutions

HRUI Long Reach PoE switches facilitate the establishment of a centralized power system for IP cameras or wireless access points, all within a highly available network framework. This solution is not only quick and safe but also cost-effective and intelligently designed, making it an ideal choice for point-to-multipoint network applications. With HRUI, you can enhance your network capabilities while ensuring efficient power management and reliable connectivity.
