HR-BTG-N901

HR-BTG-N901

Gigabit BT90W PoE injector, 1*AC power port + 1*Gigabit Ethernet Port + 1*Gigabit PoE port, 90W maximum PoE power output, support 10/100/1000M self-adaption, metal shell, Surge 6KV, ESD 15KV, working temperature range -10°C~+50°C

    Experience seamless connectivity with the HR-BTG-N901 Gigabit BT90W PoE Injector. This powerful device features one AC power port, one Gigabit Ethernet port, and one Gigabit PoE port, delivering a maximum PoE power output of 90W. Engineered for versatility, it supports self-adaptation for 10/100/1000M speeds. Its robust metal housing ensures durability, while built-in protection features such as a 6KV surge rating and 15KV ESD protection safeguard your network. With a wide operating temperature range of -10°C to +50°C, this injector is designed to perform in various environments.


    Product Features:

    Multiple Ports: Equipped with 1 Gigabit PoE port and 1 Gigabit Ethernet port for efficient network connectivity.

    PoE Standard: Compatible with IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30W), and IEEE802.3bt (90W) standards, providing versatile power options for your devices.

    Wide Input Voltage Range: Accepts an AC power supply of 100-240V, making it suitable for global use.

    Rugged Construction: Made with a durable metal housing and rated at IP20 for dust protection, ensuring reliability in various settings.

    Operating Temperatures: Performs optimally in environments ranging from -20°C to +50°C.

    Enhanced Surge Protection: Features differential mode surge protection of 2KV and common mode protection of 6KV, along with ESD protection of 6KV (contact) and 8KV (air) to safeguard connected devices.

    Features and Highlights

    • Support 1* Gigabit PoE Port+ 1* Gigabit Ethernet Port
    • Support IEEE802.3af (15.4W), IEEE802.3at (30w) and IEEE802.3bt (90w)
    • Support power supply AC 100-240V
    • Metal housing, IP20 protection grade, -20 to 50°C operating temperature
    • Support port surge protection: Differential mode 2KV, common mode 6KV; ESD: Contact 6KV, air 8KV


    Application Environment

    • Metro Optical Broadband Network: Data network operators such as telecommunications, cable TV, and network system integration, etc.
    • Broadband private network: Suitable for financial, government, oil, railway, electric power, public security, transportation, education and other industries.
    • Multimedia transmission: Integrated transmission of images, voice and data, suitable for remote teaching, conference TV, videophone and other applications.
    • Real-time monitoring: Simultaneous transmission of real-time control signals, images and data.
    • Resistant to harsh environments: Suitable for networking in harsh environments with strong electromagnetic interference and long distances.
