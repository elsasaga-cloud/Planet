OVERVIEW

S3524 Series routing switches, high-performance Layer 3 Ethernet switch with 2.5G

Multi-Gigabit and 10G SFP+ Ports compact 1U form factor. The HOS delivers robust feature

support，Provide Layer 3 network features.

Built from the ground up with the combination of advanced hardware and software, the

switch provides a Layer 3 routing solution for next generation SMB / Enterprise / Wi-Fi 6 AX

Access/ high speed NAS networks.

Varied Port Types

Support 10M/100M/1G /2.5G /10G ports to meet different network requirements.
