The SmartByte LT-IS6320G-12GX8GT-D is the highly reliable managed industrial Ethernet switch with 8-port 10/100/1000-T, 8-port 100/1000Base-X SFP, 4-port 1G/2.5GBase-X SFP. It supports ERPS redundant network and the self-recovery mechanism less than 20ms on full load which allows you to scheme a reliable Ethernet network by building a redundant ring topology as your back-up solution. It also supports DI/DO/RS485, IPv4/IPv6 static routing, DHCP server, robust Layer 2 management and secure functions, which can help users expand their networks quickly and efficiently

 

LT-IS6320G-12GX8GT-D is the high cost-effective easy-to-use device, which provides essential industrial Ethernet networking function, including wide range power input 12-75VDC, redundant power design with polarity reverse protection, robust IP40 fan-less housing with Din-rail installation, wide operation temperature from -40°C to 75°C as well as high-level EMI/EMC capability. It is the best choice for heavy industrial factory, transportation, oil & gas, chemical, IP Surveillance and processing automation area where environmental conditions are harsh and critical.


➢ 8*10/100/1000Base-T RJ45, 8*100/1000Base-X SFP, 4*1G/2.5GBase-X SFP
