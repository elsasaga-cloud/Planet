                        10/100/1000M Media Converters

                       Enhanced Function with Dip switch

                                 LT-MC80G-SC Series


Overview:

LT-MC80G-SC enhanced function series are the 10/100/1000Mbps adaptive Gigabit Ethernet
media converter which adopt switching technology to fulfill media conversion. It complies with
IEEE802.3, IEEE802.3u, IEEE802.3z and IEEE802.3ab standards. They provide media conversion
between 10/100/1000Base-T and 1000Base-SX/LX interfaces such as multi-mode LC/SC
connectors (220m / 550m), single-mode LC/SC connectors (20//40/60/80/100km) and single
fiber connectors (WDM, 20/40/60/80km) for various fiber optic applications, which can be
widely used in powerful solution for FTTX projects. The key function of enhanced series are
supporting LFP, network storm suppression, forcing the electrical port and fiber port on 100M，
which can meet with the different application requirement.
