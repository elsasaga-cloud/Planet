      Overview

      The SmartByte LT-IS6528XG-4XS16GX8GC-U-SD is the highly reliable managed industrial Ethernet
      switch with 16-port 100/1000Base-X SFP, 8-port gigabit RJ45/SFP Combo and 4-port
      1G/2.5G/10GBase-X SFP+. In normal operation, it is a L3 managed Ethernet switch. It supports
      ERPS redundant network and the self-recovery mechanism less than 20ms on full load which
      allows you to scheme a reliable Ethernet network by building a redundant ring topology as your
      back-up solution. It also supports DI/DO/RS485, DHCP server, DHCP relay, IPv4/IPv6 static routing
      and dynamic routing protocols like RIP, OSPFv2, BGP4, IS-IS, robust Layer 2 management and
      secure functions, which can help users expand their networks quickly and efficiently.

      LT-IS6528XG-4XS16GX8GC-U-SD is the high cost-effective easy-to-use device, which provides
      essential industrial Ethernet networking function, including redundant AC+DC, DC+DC and AC+AC
      power design with polarity reverse protection, robust IP40 fan-less housing with Din-rail
      installation, wide operation temperature from -40°C to 80°C as well as high-level EMI/EMC
      capability. It is the best choice for heavy industrial factory, transportation, oil & gas, chemical, IP
      Surveillance and processing automation area where environmental conditions are harsh and
      critical.
