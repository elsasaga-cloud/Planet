      Overview

      The SmartByte LT-IS6528XG-4XS24GT-D is the highly reliable managed industrial Ethernet switch
      with 24-port 10/100/1000-T, 4-port 1G/2.5G/10GBase-X SFP+. In normal operation, it is a L3
      managed Ethernet switch. It supports ERPS redundant network and the self-recovery mechanism
      less than 20ms on full load which allows you to scheme a reliable Ethernet network by building a
      redundant ring topology as your back-up solution. It also supports DI/DO/RS485, DHCP server,
      DHCP relay, IPv4/IPv6 static routing and dynamic routing protocols like RIP, OSPFv2, BGP4, IS-IS,
      robust Layer 2 management and secure functions, which can help users expand their networks
      quickly and efficiently

      LT-IS6528XG-4XS24GT-D is the high cost-effective easy-to-use device, which provides essential
      industrial Ethernet networking function, including wide range power input 12-75VDC, redundant
      power design with polarity reverse protection, robust IP40 fan-less housing with Din-rail
      installation, wide operation temperature from -40°C to 75°C as well as high-level EMI/EMC
      capability. It is the best choice for heavy industrial factory, transportation, oil & gas, chemical, IP
      Surveillance and processing automation area where environmental conditions are harsh and
      critical.
