    Overview

    The SmartByte LT-IS3005F-5TX-M-AD is the compact size unmanaged industrial grade Ethernet
    switch with 5-port 10/100Base-TX RJ45. It is the high cost-effective easy-to-use device, which
    provides essential industrial Ethernet networking function, such as wide range power DC/AC
    input, redundant power design with polarity reverse protection, robust IP40 fan-less housing with
    Din-rail installation, wide operation temperature from -40 ℃ ~+75℃ as well as high-level
    EMI/EMC capability and so on. It is the best choice for heavy industrial field where installation
    space are limited and restricted
