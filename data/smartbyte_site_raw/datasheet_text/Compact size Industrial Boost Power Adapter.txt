  Overview

  The SmartByte LT-IBPA52-1O2-M is the compact size industrial booster power adapter with 1-port
  DC 12-48V input, 2-port DC 52V output. It is the ideal PoE solution that if the power source is less
  than 40V, the LT-IBPA52-1O2-M can booster the power source to redundant 52V output. The Max
  PoE power budget can be 60W if input voltage is DC 12-23V and Max PoE power budget can be
  120W if input voltage is DC 24-48V

  LT-IBPA52-1O2-M is also high cost-effective easy-to-use device, which is specifically designed with
  durable components and strong housing case to operate reliably in electrically harsh and
  climatically demanding environments in a wide temperature range of -40 to 75 degrees. The LT-
  IBPA52-1O2-M is packaged in a compact, IP40 rugged case that allows either DIN-rail or wall
  mounting to have the efficient use of cabinet space. It is ideal for service providers, campuses and
  public areas to deploy PoE wireless access points, IP cameras or IP phones in any places easily and
  efficiently.
