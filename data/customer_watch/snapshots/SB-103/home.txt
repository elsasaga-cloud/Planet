Product Series
The impressive new GRUNDIG Security portfolio comprises three product ranges containing everything you need to
create a modern, professional video surveillance system: Essential, Professional and Premium.
The “Essential” range products are equipped with all common features covering a wide range of video solutions for
various applications.
The “Professional” product range was designed for complex demands. With additional functions, these products offer
significant advantages for more specific tasks.
The "Premium" product range contains products whose functional scope is fully adapted to high-end applications.
CAMERA
The GRUNDIG Security camera portfolio is designed for a wide range of surveillance tasks. It includes both IP and HD-TVI cameras with resolutions from 2 MP up to 12 MP in various designs and with a wide range of features.
Grundig camera
RECORDER
GRUNDIG Security offers a wide product range of recorders: from simple embedded IP systems to analogue multinorm IP recorders to Windows-based recorders with the fully functional GRUNDIG Video Management System (VMS) powered by C-WERK®. All common standards are supported, including IP, TVI, CVI, AHD and CVBS.
Grundig recorder
MONITOR
The GRUNDIG Security monitor portfolio includes monitors from 25.6 cm (10.1 inches) up to 140 cm (55 inches). This range meets all requirements - from compact monitors for standalone workstations to a freely configurable video wall. Be surprised by the clear and detailed display of the GRUNDIG Security TFT LED monitors!
Grundig monitor
SOFTWARE
The GRUNDIG Video Management System powered by C-WERK® is fully scalable and features easy-to-use interfaces. Regardless of the requirements, all use cases can be easily covered with this software solution.
Grundig software
ACCESSORIES
All peripherals are made of first-class materials and match perfectly with the GRUNDIG Security product portfolio. Thanks to the exact fit and simple assembly of these original accessories, the installation of GRUNDIG Security products at any installation location is done in no time at all.
