About Us
Areca Embedded Systems Private Limited is formed by a team of professionals in 2007 with rich experience in a wide range of Telecom and Railway projects. It is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, engaged in the field of Telecommunication, Railways, Defence, Transportation and Power Sectors providing new product development, product re-engineering and product support services in Hardware, Software and Infrastructure Development areas.
