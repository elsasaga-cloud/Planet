Sourced for Performance. Trusted for Uptime.
Niaar connects EPC contractors and engineering teams across Middle East with high-spec industrial equipment, Precision instruments, and technical components.
Established in 2007 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, Niaar is a trusted partner for over 160 industrial firms, including petrochemical plants, steel refineries, automotive factories, oil plants, and high-tech electronics companies. We specialize in sourcing industrial technologies and raw materials, as well as providing comprehensive import, export, and trade solutions. By combining global innovation with local expertise, we ensure seamless operations across customs, banking, and worldwide logistics.
