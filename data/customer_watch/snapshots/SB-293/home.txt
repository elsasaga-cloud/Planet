Construction Site Security Cameras and Monitoring
Virtual Protective provides construction site security cameras and monitoring services to a number of jobsite managers, general contractors, commercial building contractors, and more. We take the time to visit your jobsite and provide you with cost-effective solutions to providing the proper security surveillance your site needs. Our AI-operated software is very advanced and allows us to detect intruders on your jobsite immediately with no false alarms.
​
We take great care to ensure that our construction site security monitoring systems and threat-detection systems have 100% uptime to safeguard your construction sites around the clock and with no interruptions. We provide our clients with a convenient app that lets you view the live feeds whenever you feel the need, and we can help turn existing CCTV cameras into an active alarm system that will immediately detect intruders prior to alerting the authorities. We can even provide you with solar-powered systems that can be used even in remote locations where direct power is not available.
​
If you’re in the Texas area and require the ability to observe your jobsite around the clock, contact us today to learn more about our construction site security cameras and monitoring services.
Intrusion Detection
Detect intruders instantly, and dispatch police for an arrest. Our advanced camera systems can distinguish real threats from false alerts, so the police know they are responding to a real intrusion.

License Plate Capture
Capture every license plate that enters your property. Our "Triple Threat" syestems include two Security Cameras and a Low-Mounted License Plate Capture Camera. Solar Powered to be Deployed Anywhere.
24/7 Recording
Sometimes theft happens during working hours. That is why we include 24/7 recording on our security units. When something goes missing, we can rewind and see what occurred, even if the system was disarmed.
