Our Partners
Vast Inventory, Rapid Delivery, Competitive Pricing, Satisfied Customers
Provo Ltd. distinguishes itself through a long-standing successful history of over 65 years, supported by substantial inventory held in Toronto and Montréal, which enables rapid delivery across Canada. This commitment to maintaining a vast stock ensures that over 95% of cataloged items are readily available, addressing the immediate needs of customers. Orders placed before  Eastern Time are shipped the same day, guaranteeing next-day arrival in major cities within Quebec and Ontario, and prompt shipment to other Canadian locations. This dedication to quick and reliable delivery underscores Provo's focus on exceptional customer service.
Beyond efficient service, Provo leverages its extensive experience and significant purchasing power as one of the largest distributors in its sector to offer competitive pricing on high-quality products. By buying in large volumes, Provo can pass on savings to its customers, a fact they recognize and appreciate. This combination of excellent products at affordable prices, coupled with a reliance on satisfied customers for promotion rather than traditional advertising for the past 30 years, has propelled Provo into Canada's leading distributor of electronic cabling, wire, and accessories. Furthermore, Provo is committed to ensuring equal access and participation for people with disabilities.
