Electronica India 2026
September 15, 2026
Next week, Electronica India 2026 will take place in Bengaluru, bringing together companies and technology leaders from across the electronics and networking industries.
During the event, visitors will have the opportunity to see Larch Networks software running on Marvell’s Ironman device, presented by our partners at SERA Network.
It will be a great opportunity to see the solution in action and learn more about Larch Networks technology.
📅 September 16–18, 2026
📍 Bangalore International Exhibition Centre (BIEC), Bengaluru
🏢 Hall 5 | Booth H5.H35
If you are attending electronica India, stop by the SERA booth to see the solution firsthand.
