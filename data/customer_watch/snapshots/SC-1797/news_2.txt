The event
The Snom VIP Distributor Event 2026 was a great opportunity to celebrate partnerships, exchange ideas, and explore new possibilities.
At Cohesive Technologies, we value strong partnerships and look forward to achieving greater milestones together with Snom Technology GmbH .
A word from Snom
🇮🇹 Grazie, Roma!
Last week, we welcomed our EMEA and APAC Platinum and Gold Distributors to the Snom VIP Distributor Event 2026 in Rome.
We spent the days celebrating shared achievements, exchanging ideas and looking at the opportunities ahead. Above all, we had the chance to spend time together in person with the partners who bring Snom to market every day.
Our success is built on the trust, passion and commitment of our distribution partners. Thank you to everyone who joined us. We’re proud of what we’ve built together and excited for the next chapter.
— Snom Technology GmbH
Why it matters to our partners
As a Snom Gold Distributor, Cohesive Technologies brings Snom's portfolio to resellers and system integrators across India and South Asia. Time spent with the Snom team and fellow distributors keeps us close to the product roadmap and to what is working in other markets, which we bring back to our partners here.
