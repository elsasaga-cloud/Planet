About us
Founded in 2007, COP Security System Corp. specializes in the design, development, and manufacturing of security surveillance systems, delivering complete end-to-end solutions for a wide range of applications.
With years of hands-on industry experience, we combine technical expertise with real-world insight to create reliable, practical, and high-quality security products. Our mission goes beyond supplying equipment—we strive to be a trusted long-term partner, helping our customers build safer and more efficient surveillance environments.
In addition to our own branded products, COP Security offers comprehensive OEM / ODM services, supported by strong R&D capabilities, flexible manufacturing, and strict quality control. We look forward to collaborating with partners worldwide to create lasting value together.
