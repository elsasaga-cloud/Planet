INTRODUCTION
Petawise is a Toronto-based video surveillance solution founded in 2015. We develop and sell CCTV and access control products in North America that are NDAA compliant.
Currently, Petawise sells IP cameras, NVRs, PoE switches, Turnstile gates, Wifi mesh kits, and Home automation devices through partners including distributors and integrators. We became a major and reliable provider of video surveillance products and services across Canada. Our technical team provides consultations and support in English, French, and Spanish from 8 am to 8 pm EST. With a local warehouse, we are able to deliver products within a business day.
