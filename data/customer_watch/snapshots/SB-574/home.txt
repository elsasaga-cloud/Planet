Rack cabinets
In this category you will find a wide range of rack cabinets, which are essential for the safe storage and organization of network and server equipment. Hanging and standing cabinets to suit different space requirements, as well as battery racks, open racks and hanging frames.
Rack accessories
All the rack accessories you need, including server enclosures, fiber optic switchers, patchpanels, shelves and cable organizers. You'll also find grommets, plugs, brush panels, fans and PDUs for proper organization and management.
Rack accessories
All the rack accessories you need, including server enclosures, fiber optic switchers, patchpanels, shelves and cable organizers. You'll also find grommets, plugs, brush panels, fans and PDUs for proper organization and management.
Structured network
In the structured networking category, we offer everything you need to build a reliable and efficient wired network. Here you'll find patchcords, LAN cables, plugs, network sockets, keystones and connectors that are key to ensuring robust network connections at home and in the office.
Fiber optics
This category focuses on fiber optic solutions, including fiber optic patchcords, pigtails, adapters and fiber optic switches. These products are ideal for high-speed, high-performance network connections that require high bandwidth and long data transmission distances.
Cables and adapters
A wide range of cables and adapters, including USB, HDMI, DP, VGA, DVI, audio, internal computer cables, power cables, as well as cable organizers and other accessories. These are essential items for audio/video, data and power connections in a variety of environments.
Active network
A sizable variety of active network devices, such as unmanaged switches, monitoring solutions, network cards, WiFi adapters and other network devices. These products are essential for building and managing effective, modern networks.
Tools
You'll find the tools you need to build and maintain your network, including cable management tools, meters, testers and cleaners. These products are key for professionals involved in network installation and maintenance.
Power supply
In this category we offer extension cords, power cables, surge strips, PDUs and flush-mounted and 45x45 outlets. These are solutions to ensure stable and safe energy supply to electrical and electronic devices in any home or office.
