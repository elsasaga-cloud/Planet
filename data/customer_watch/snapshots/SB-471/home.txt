SIMPLE BY DESIGN
Private 5G in Four Steps
From planning to a live network — no integration sprints, no cellular specialists, no months of waiting.
Step 1
Plan
Upload your site map, identify what you need to connect, and generate a propagation model to determine radio placement — all in the Moso IMP platform.
Step 2
Install
Mount the radio at your planned location then connect to power and your network. Moso radios work with standard infrastructure — no specialist labor, no local servers required.
Step 3
Activate
Activate the network on prem or in the cloud platform. The radio connects automatically and devices join via SIM or eSIM in minutes.
Step 4
Manage
Monitor 574+ KPIs, automate provisioning, and get instant visibility into every radio and device — from one cloud dashboard, across any number of sites.
