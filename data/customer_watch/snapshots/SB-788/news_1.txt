Founded in 1924 Wood Communications is Ireland’s Premier leading distributor of networking products for the Data communications, Data Centre, Telecoms, and Security industry. We are an Irish owned and managed Distribution company and provide networks solutions throughout the island of Ireland and mainland Europe.
Our Core objectives as a company are to bring the right products and solutions to our customer’s specification, on time and within budget. We operate a no-nonsense approach in delivering your requirements and service which is second to none.
