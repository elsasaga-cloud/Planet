About Baldota Group
Founded in 1961, the Baldota Group is leading business conglomerates that has a diversified footprint in Mining, Pelletization, Green Energy, Gases, Shipping, Aviation, Environmental Care and Mental Wellness.

Mining
Since inception, mining has been the flagship business of Baldota Group. We were pioneers in mining then and leaders now. In this core activity, we substantially build environment friendly mining practices with zero waste production. Baldota group integrate and amalgamate the economic activities principally focusing on sustainable development as its Moola Mantra.

Pelletisation
With our industry-acclaimed processes and standard operating procedures (SoPs) that have withstood the test of time, we lead the pelletisation industry. Using advanced Grate Kiln technology, our pelletization plant has a reasonable economic index and ensures robust processing.

Wind Power
In all our economic activities, balancing them with environmental conservation is paramount for us. This green mindset has inspired us to harness limitless clean energy from the wind. We boast of seven operational wind farms across India.

Gases
