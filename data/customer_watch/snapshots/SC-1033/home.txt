Bausch Datacom transforms innovation into market-ready products. The company develops, manufactures, and commercializes solutions under its own brand as well as for OEM partners. Its core expertise lies in electronic boards and software for data communication within the (Green) Energy sector. Key product domains include smart electronics for EV and solar applications, Remote Terminal Units for MV/LV substations, communication modems for metering and tokenization gateways.
Together with its sister company, Ritter Technologie, also part of the Ritter Group, Bausch Datacom emphasizes on 100% European R&D, data storage, production, and cybertechnology certifications.

State-of-the-Art
Bausch focuses on smart devices based on state-of-the-art communication experience and cost reduction by customizing hardware on the client's demand.
All-in-One
For its RTU's and Distribution Automation Boxes a concept of 'all-in-one' is used. Communication modules, (SCADA) protocols, metering, security are all united in one housing, always looking for the most cost-effective solution.
Building Blocks
Our engineering with its long experience going back to 1989 has all kinds of building blocks for industrial data communication. It uses proven technology for stability when possible but will always look for innovation.
Engineering
We offer services from the initial concept to schematics, PCB design and prototyping. Our engineering also does approvals, compliance testing and coordination of production if necessary.
Electromagnetics
Our GSM, GPRS, 4G modems for metering communication are designed according to the highest industrial electromagnetical immunity requirements and therefore greatly appreciated by our customers.
Production
The company works with Belgian based production centers, but has also foreign possibilities for manufacturing. Our productions are ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14.000 and ISO 27K covered.
