OPTICAL TRANSCEIVERS
Our extensive range of transceivers deliver consistent quality and reliability backed by ISO9001, ISO14001, and TL9000 quality standards and a lifetime warranty. We support all major form factors, modes, and speeds, including SFP, SFP28, QSFP, QSFP28, QSFP56, QSFP-DD, and OSFP, along with data rates from 100M to 800G.
LEARN MORE
AOC & DAC
Choose from our range of Active Optical Cables and Direct Attach Cables that support all form factors up to 800G. AOCs options include Environmentally Ruggerdized or RAOCs design, are available in various colors and industry lengths, and include the option for private labeling and custom coding. DOCs are available in active and passive forms, with custom pull-tab options, and private labeling of bulk cable and assembly.

EASYCODER
Code, re-code, and test optics for compatibility with hundreds of OEM brands using the T1Nexus EasyCoder. It’s designed to support XFP, SFP, and QSFP form factors, on-site troubleshooting, and custom code development.

MEDIA CONVERTERS
Connect 10Mbps, 100Mbps, 1G, or 10G copper to multimode or single-mode fiber with up to 160km extensions to the desktop. Standard, mini, rack-mounted versions, and POE options with up to 60W capabilities are available.
LEARN MORE
Passive Networking
Enjoy DWDM modules and chassis support up to 40 (C21-C61) wavelength channels through a single fiber over long distances at high-speed data rates.
CWDM modules and chassis support up to 18 wavelength channels through a single fiber and are flexible enough to be deployed on common fiber networks where distance is limited, such as telecom access networks or school campuses.
