What are unidirectional secure gateways?
To protect critical assets or utilities, the quickest and most efficient way is isolating your secured network from external networks.
However, with IIoT4.0, transferring information out from a secured network is unavoidable. How do we send the data without compromising network integrity?
A Unidirectional Gateway is the optimal solution for high-assurance applications.
A Unidirectional Gateway is a hardware device placed between two networks of
different security levels, and controls the flow of information.
Unidirectional Gateways:
the only Cyber Security solution that ensures traffic can only travel in one direction.
