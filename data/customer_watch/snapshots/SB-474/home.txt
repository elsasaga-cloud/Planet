Case Communications
Leaders in Industrial Telecommunications Products
Case Communications design, develop and manufacturer advanced industrial / rugged telecommunications products for reliable use in mission critical applications operating in harsh environments . These products are typically used for machine to machine communications (M2M) and are used world-wide by some of the largest corporations.
Formed in the 1970s, we have a unique blend of experience, expertise and professionalism. We specialise in the design, development and implementation of industrial / rugged corporate communication systems, focusing particularly on mission-critical applications in harsh environments. Our Markets include the Oil and Gas Industry, Utilities, Railways, Air Travel, SMART Cities, Transport Systems and M.O.D.
