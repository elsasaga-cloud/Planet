Hands on Security are up and running since 2000. This family business is situated in Meyerton and is operated by Thys Koekemoer. We passionate about security and combating crime in South Africa. Only the best service can be expected from well-trained site teams managed by the owner. Services are rendered over the whole of South Africa of which the owner will accompany the site team to destination. Wide spectrum of security products/services are offered which includes installations. Hands on Security offers high quality products and focus on customer satisfaction.
