Why my signal good but internet surfing always stuck?
Many users are often puzzled, why is my Wi-Fi signal good but internet surfing always stuck?
If my wireless AP got broken? Most of the time you just need to optimize AP’s setting.
As shown below, Wi-Fi can be operated at both 2.4GHz and 5GHz.
In general, users can choose 5GHz as their first priority to avoid interference since 2.4GHz has lots of equipment like Microwave and Bluetooth operated.
Even if 5GHz is selected, if everyone uses the same frequency, there will still be mutual interference. Users can download free software through the Internet to confirm the frequency with less interference.
Or you can choose Prism Wi-Fi, Prism Wi-Fi has a built-in spectrum scanning function, which allows network administrators to instantly understand the current wireless network environment and choose the best frequency of use.
If you don’t want to be so troublesome, try Prism AP’s automatic frequency selection function, which automatically selects the best operating frequency according to the current network conditions.
