At Wiesemann & Theis we have been producing and marketing electronic hardware for 45 years.
Our offering ranges from serial interface converters to I/O controller to edge computing and IT security solutions.
In addition to our short delivery times, we are especially proud of the long service life and reliability of our products (Made in Wuppertal) and our fast, constructive and technically expert support.
