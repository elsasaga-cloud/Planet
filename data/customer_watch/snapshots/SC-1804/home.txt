Indra Heera Group
The Indra Heera Group is a corporate consortium in India, Manufacturer & RDI (Research, Design & Innovation) in the fields of Telecommunication, Networking & Internet of Things. Its entity has been designated as " Trusted Source " under the Government of India’s National Security Directive on Telecom Sector ( NSDTS ) and has also been recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade ( DPIIT ) for its pioneering contributions to product development. The Group consistently delivers high-quality, reliable, and customized solutions across its domains. By strategically leveraging platforms such as Government e-Marketplace ( GeM ) & Open Network for Digital Commerce ( ONDC ), the Group serves B2G, PSU, B2B & D2C sectors across the nation.....
The Leadership...
The leadership team at Indra Heera Group includes seasoned professionals and Veterans from renowned organizations like the United Nations , the Indian Army , and the National Technical Research Organisation . Their extensive experience spans Security , Governance , and Technical Domains , reflecting a strong commitment to excellence and innovation. This exceptional expertise enables the group to address complex challenges effectively. With a focus on sustainability and transformative impact, Indra Heera Group drives meaningful change. It plays a key role in advancing India’s technological landscape. The group also contributes significantly to the nation’s economic growth and prosperity.....
Make in India...
The Indra Heera Group is unwavering in its commitment to innovation, research, and the promotion of the " Make in India " initiative. Our product portfolio stands as a testament to cutting-edge innovation, meticulously engineered through rigorous research and development processes, with over 60% of components sourced locally from within India . Renowned for their superior quality, reliability, and technological sophistication, our products are thoughtfully designed to address the dynamic needs of various industries while fostering a sustainable and self-reliant industrial ecosystem. Through our steadfast dedication to excellence, we continue to drive economic growth, reduce import dependency, create meaningful employment opportunities, and enhance operational efficiency, all while contributing to a more resilient and sustainable future.
