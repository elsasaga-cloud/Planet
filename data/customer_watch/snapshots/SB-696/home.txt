About AIRONIX
Aironix was founded in 2016 by prominent Israeli high-tech pioneers.
AIRONIX is committed to delivering advanced industrial technology solutions and high-performance engineering equipment to meet the evolving demands of today’s industries.
With over 30 years of combined experience in the industrial computing and technology markets, we bring extensive technical expertise, strong industry knowledge, and strategic partnerships with leading global manufacturers. This enables us to provide our customers with reliable, high-quality technology solutions tailored to their specific requirements.
Our commitment to quality and reliability is reflected in our fully qualified laboratory, audited by the Israel Standards Institute (SII) in accordance with ISO 9001:2015 requirements, and our Ministry of Defense certification (No. 11005959) .
