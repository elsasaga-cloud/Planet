Internet Services
We are an Internet Service Provider (ISP) with fully owned and dedicated international gateway and connectivity - providing differentiated and enriched experience of Internet leased line and committed to delivering very efficient and prompt support services while maintaining high reliability and quality of service.
Part of our ISP services include Network Connectivity and Operations, Networking & Network Technologies - Long Haul and Access, Networking - Routing & Switching, Internet of Things (IoT), IPv6 Implementation & Training
Managed Security Services
We are a Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP) providing all layers of protections from cyber threats and attacks including Web Application Firewall (WAF), DDOS Attack Mitigation System; all supported by our availability of 24x7 Emergency Respose Team with predefined response time and Service Level Agreement (SLA). In addition to these, we also provide solutions and services around physical security enabled by technologies such as IP surveillance and CCTV products.
Managed IT and Network Operations
We can fully, most effectivey and very reliably manage IT and network operations for customers whose core functions are none of these and don't have inhouse capabiities. We bring complete and combined synergy of all areas of expertise in IT and network operations, and with that utmost peace of mind to our customers.

NANO
"NOTHING IS NO"
NANO is one of the three fully-fledged and licensed Internet Service Providers (ISPs) in Bhutan to own independent international gateway, international connectivity and domestic networks built on the latest DWDM and fiber technologies. Driven by the very Internet-passionate people who are motivated by our belief in "Nothing is No," our team - the so called "NANO WARRIORS" is focused on delivering differentiated and enriched Internet Services Experiences, Managed Security Service and Enterprise (IT & Network) Services in Bhutan. Our differentiation is the team capabilities that we work on or build. We are a young entrepreneurial startup founded by the top-notch management and technical team that brought Internet and telecommunications (specifically FTTx services) development in Bhutan. Consisting of 11 employees in total currently and growing, the core composition of NANO WARRIORS have grown through being the most competent Telecom Engineers, Web Applications Developers, Network Engineers, System Admins, Managers for Network and System Administration, and Management at DrukNet (ISP & Enterprise IT Services) of Bhutan Telecom. We built and grew DrukNet (the largest ISP in Bhutan) along with which we built our own capabilities. Our pride and core competency reside in the strong core team composition that has an average of 17+ years of experience in the Internet/telecommunications industry.
Backed up by world’s leading technology partners in OEMs, operators, and region’s business and SI partners, we work in the areas of: Internet Services (ISP), Networking, Network Connectivity and Operations, Internet of Things (IoT), Cyber Security (Managed Security Service Provider), Cloud & Enterprise Services, Mobile and Web Applications Development, Payments, Unified Communications, Networking – Routing & Switching Technologies, Contact Center - Technology & Operations (BPO), Infrastructure Technologies, Data Storage, Backup & Recovery, Application Delivery & Load Balancing, Processes & Services Automation, Network Technologies - Long Haul and Access, Security & IT Audit, Professional, Consulting & Training Services specializing in IPv6.

Tshering Norbu
Managing Director
He is the Managing Director of NANO. He was part of the first team to have played catalytic roles in the introduction and development of Internet in Bhutan. Before founding NANO, he was the first person to head the newly created Technology Strategy (with the roles to that of a CTO) & Business Development (with the roles to that of a Chief Business Officer) Division in Bhutan Telecom. Prior to that, as the General Manager, he headed DrukNet - Internet Services and Enterprise IT Services of Bhutan Telecom from 2008-2014. He started his career in DrukNet as the Webmaster, and then moved on to become Network and Systems Engineer, Administrator and Manager.
He served as the 2nd Chairman of South Asian Network Operators Group (SANOG) until early 2016. He is still the Core Committee member of SANOG. He held positions of other recognition such as: Director of Thimphu TechPark Ltd. – Bhutan’s 1st IT Park, Chairman & IT Lead of IT CXO Group, Druk Holding & Investments – commercial arm of Bhutan’s government comprising of Bhutan’s largest corporations, and Administrative & Technical Contact for Bhutan's ccTLD (.BT).
He holds Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand and Bachelor of Engineering in Telecommunications from the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia.
Tshering Gyeltshen
Chief Technology Officer
He is the Chief Technology Officer of NANO who worked about 17 years in DrukNet (the Internet & IT Services of Bhutan Telecom) and grew to become the top-notch technical manager and engineer, and highest technical escalation point in Internet and IT Services and operations. His last positions in Bhutan Telecom were Sr. Manager of Internet & IT and before that the 1st Manager of (Cyber) Security Services.
He has Bachelor in Computer Application from Eastern Institute for Integrated Learning in Management (EIILM), India and Diploma in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Royal Bhutan Polytechnic, Bhutan.
Sonam Rinchen
Chief Operating Officer
He is the Chief Operating Officer of NANO and was instrumental for 20+ years in the development of telecommunications (especially fiber networks) in Bhutan having started his career in Bhutan Telecom in 1991. He was the First Manager to be involved directly in the deployment and operation of Fiber (FTTx) networks and services in the country for Bhutan Telecom. His last role in Bhutan Telecom was the Sr. Manager for Backbone & Access (FTTx) Networks Design & Operations, and prior to that he was manading Bhutan Telecom's nationwide backbone fiber connectivity.
He holds Bachelor of Technology in Electronics & Communications from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Roorkee, India and Diploma in Electrical Engineering from Royal Bhutan Polytechnic, Bhutan.
Kuenzang Dorji
General Manager
