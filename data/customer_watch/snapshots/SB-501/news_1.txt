DiversiTech® Acquires DITEK, Creating a Leading Provider of Critical Products
DiversiTech ® Acquires DITEK, Creating a Leading Provider of Critical Products Across Electrical, Fire & Safety, Networking, HVAC, and Security
DiversiTech ® Corporation and DITEK unite to drive greater product innovation, expanded services, and enhanced technical support across a combined portfolio serving the building trades.
DiversiTech ® Corporation, a leading manufacturer and supplier of installation, repair, and maintenance parts and supplies for the HVAC industry, today announced its acquisition of DITEK, an industry leader in surge protection design and U.S manufacturing. Together, the combined organization becomes a leading U.S. based manufacturer of critical products serving the Electrical, Fire & Life Safety, Networking, HVAC, and Security markets. Both companies are recognized leaders in their respective product categories, and the acquisition creates opportunities to leverage their combined scale, expertise, and capabilities.
DITEK brings long-standing leadership in surge protection, deep technical expertise, differentiated product design, and strong manufacturing capabilities that complement DiversiTech’s established HVAC/R portfolio. Through DiversiTech’s expanded service, support, and distribution network, contractors, wholesalers, technicians, and service professionals will have broader access to proven protection solutions as systems become more sophisticated, connected, and costly to replace, creating new opportunities to serve customers across a broader range of applications.
“Joining forces with DiversiTech creates an exciting opportunity to build on DITEK’s legacy of innovation, quality, and customer commitment,” said Wendy Gattis, Chief Executive Officer of DITEK. “Our team remains focused on delivering the surge protection solutions and technical support our customers rely on, while benefiting from DiversiTech’s scale and resources, to accelerate growth and expand our reach.”
“DiversiTech has built its reputation by helping HVAC customers simplify installation, repair, and maintenance with dependable products, broad availability, and practical solutions,” said Andy Bergdoll, Chief Executive Officer of DiversiTech. “DITEK brings outstanding surge protection design and manufacturing capabilities, along with a customer-centric approach and technical expertise that have made it a leader in its industry. We are excited to support DITEK as it continues to maintain the service, quality, and innovation its customers value while expanding the ways we can serve the HVAC/R market and Electrical, Fire & Life Safety, Networking, and Security markets together.”
DITEK will continue to uphold the customer‑focused culture, high‑quality products, responsive support, and technical expertise that have earned the company a strong reputation with contractors, technicians, integrators, engineers, and end users. DiversiTech is committed to supporting DITEK’s ongoing growth while preserving the approach that has made the brand a trusted leader in surge protection.
