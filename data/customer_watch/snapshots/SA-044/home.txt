Rugged Performance for the Most Demanding Environments
DYMEC’s industrial ethernet products are engineered to be hardened and flexible enough to operate in even the harshest conditions – without sacrificing performance.
Hardened
DYMEC’s rugged industrial ethernet switches deliver the performance you need even under rough industrial conditions, i.e. shock, vibration, and temperatures fluctuating between -40C and +85C. They are built to take the punishment in power generation and distribution, marine, rail, road and a wide range of transportation applications.

DYMEC® Protects Against the Full Range of Threats to Your Industrial Ethernet Network
