Fuzzy Logic Automation
Fuzzy Logic Equipment Trading Ltd, headquartered in Abu Dhabi, UAE, was established with a mission to deliver reliable, intelligent, and cost-effective automation and electrical solutions tailored to the dynamic needs of modern industries. Backed by a solid foundation in industrial automation and electrical engineering, our team of expert engineers and technicians has successfully completed over 100 projects across the UAE, including SCADA system implementations, electrical control panel design and fabrication, UPS installation and maintenance, PLC & HMI programming, and full-scale electrical infrastructure development. Among our key competencies, SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) stands out as a core specialization—we design and deploy advanced systems that enable real-time monitoring, data acquisition, and remote control, offering clients greater operational visibility and process automation. Our ongoing commitment to quality, technical innovation, and customer satisfaction has positioned us as a trusted partner in the region’s industrial automation landscape
