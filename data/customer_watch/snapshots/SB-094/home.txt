About Maxcom
Since its foundation in January 2000, Maxcom has been a leader in the telecommunications fiber optic industry. We design and manufacture fiber optic, long and short distance transport solutions for the following signals:
Audio and Video (Composite Baseband, Component, HDMI, SDI, ASI)
Ethernet and Gigabit Ethernet up to 10Gbit/s
DS3 ,T1, RS232, RS455
RF Broadband, CATV, L-band, and C-band
MPEG2, MPEG4, H.264, SMPE 310M, DVB-ASI, SDI, HDTV
