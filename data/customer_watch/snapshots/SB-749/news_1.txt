Bell Canada vLab Standardizes ECIN Optics to Simplify Operations and Reduce Total Cost of Ownership
Customer
Bell Canada – vLab (Virtual Lab)
Optics Partner
E.C.I. NETWORKS (ECIN)
Use Case
Centralized testing, staging, validation, demos, and training
Focus
Interoperable 1G, 10G, and 100G transceivers across multi-vendor platforms
Key Outcome
Lab-wide standardization, faster turnaround, and substantial TCO reduction
About Bell Canada vLab
The Bell Canada vLab (Virtual Lab) is a centralized, strategic facility that supports:
Network validation, staging, and Proof of Concept (PoC) development
Customer demonstrations, internal training, and practice enablement
A shared infrastructure for cross-functional teams and business units
Its infrastructure mirrors production environments using hardware from:
Cisco, Juniper, Arista, Palo Alto Networks, HPE, and others
Supporting speeds of 1G (SFP), 10G (SFP+), and 100G (QSFP28)
Challenge: Simplifying SFP Sourcing with a Canadian Vendor
Bell Canada's vLab is a facility for network validation, staging, and demos and faced challenges in sourcing interoperable 1G, 10G, and 100G transceivers across multiple OEMs. This led to long lead times, high CAPEX due to vendor-locked pricing, and complex inventory and procurement management.
Bell Canada vLab needed a partner who could simplify optics sourcing without compromising quality or compatibility.
Solution: ECIN Delivers a Unified Optics Strategy
Partnering with ECIN Networks, Bell Canada vLab was able to implement a unified optics strategy, using ECIN's fully interoperable, pre-coded transceivers.
These optics are locally stocked and tested for compatibility across all key platforms in Bell's lab.
Standardization of this solution across the Bell Canada vLab
Faster turnaround times
Seamless plug-and-play compatibility
Significant reduction in total cost of ownership (TCO)
50-80% CAPEX savings
Up to 80% OPEX savings on maintenance
Supplied Optics
1G SFP: SX, LX, Copper
10G SFP+: SR, LR, ZR, BiDi (standard and extended temperature)
100G QSFP28: SR4, LR4, CWDM4, PSM4
Breakout Support: 100G to 4x25G via AOC, DAC, and MPO-LC fanouts
Coded for: Cisco, Juniper, Arista, Palo Alto, HPE, Fortinet, and more
Value Delivered
Seamless plug-and-play interoperability
Single point of contact for all transceiver SKUs
Rapid fulfillment from Canadian inventory
Consolidated sparing and simplified procurement
ECIN's Financial Advantage: Minimize TCO, CAPEX and OPEX
Using high-quality, compatible third-party optical transceivers delivers significant TCO savings by dramatically reducing both CAPEX and OPEX.
The most immediate benefit is CAPEX reduction — ECIN optics are often 50% to 80% less expensive than OEM-branded equivalents.
Over the lifespan of the infrastructure, OPEX savings are realized through the elimination of costly OEM support contracts, in some cases, reducing maintenance expenses by up to 80%.
This combination allows Bell to reallocate capital toward lab expansion, innovation, and higher-value network initiatives.
