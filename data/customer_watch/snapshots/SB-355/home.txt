GAO (Global Advanced Operations) Tek Inc.
GAO (Global Advanced Operations) Tek Inc. is ranked as a top 10 B2B technology provider in the world. Headquartered in New York City, U.S. and Toronto, Canada, GAO Tek Inc. serves customers globally while focuses on the U.S. and Canada markets. We sell through our direct tech sales and tech support workforce, as well as a strong network of partners located in different parts of North America.
GAO Tek has worked with its sister companies GAO Research Inc., GAO RFID Inc. and its partners to research, develop and manufacture a wide range of leading technological products for businesses and institutions worldwide. Over the course of 3 decades, the GAO Group of companies have been at the forefront of innovation and have made significant contributions to the global high-tech industry.
