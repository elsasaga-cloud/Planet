KBC eCopper™ products offer a cost-effective way to connect the latest IP cameras using existing coax or copper (UTP) wiring to a remote monitoring station.
In addition to saving costs and time from cabling infrastructure upgrades to CAT5/5e/6, eCopper™ powers both the transmitter and remote cameras, eliminating the need for any additional power source at the camera site.
