Who we are?
MADALIX Ltd. is a value-added distributor of embedded hardware solutions, high-quality products, components, and accessories. We bring great expertise in engineering, sales, and marketing for Computer Hardware for any environment and different markets such as industrial, medical, and defense.
Through a growing network of partners and the continuous acquisition of new technologies, we can offer tailor-made solutions for machine automation, logistics and transportation, digital signage, intelligent retail, IoT, communication, networks, healthcare, security, AI, deep learning, and others.
The company focuses primarily on the following core product areas: Industrial and Rugged Embedded Computers, Industrial & Rugged Tablets, Industrial & Rugged Panel PC, Industrial Displays, Cameras, Network and Communication solutions.
Our company is certified with ISO 9001:2015 quality standard.
