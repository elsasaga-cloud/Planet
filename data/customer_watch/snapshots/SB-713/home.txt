ABOUT
Netronics Communications is a leading designer and manufacturer of networking and communications equipment based in Ontario, Canada. For over two decades, we have successfully leveraged our knowledge of the latest radio, laser and wireless communication technologies to provide high quality and highly reliable equipment. These equipments serve connectivity solutions for applications of different sizes and functionalities.
Besides a rich product portfolio, our radio network planning services offer a complete report on the achievable capacity, antenna type, tower height and redundancy/diversity requirements along with availability percentage; based on coordinates submitted by our clients.
