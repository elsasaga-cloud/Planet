Dedicated to Excellence
Unrivalled Range
We offer an unrivalled range of over 2,500 high-quality products, including cables, adapters, USB, and audio-video solutions. Our extensive selection is designed to meet both modern and legacy connectivity needs, ensuring exceptional performance and reliability. We specialise in innovative technologies for connecting, sharing, converting, and extending, with award-winning products recognised for their excellence and innovation.
Customer Focused
We are committed to providing exceptional customer service through our experienced support team. We offer comprehensive pre- and post-sales support from our UK-based experts, available via phone, email, and live chat. Our advanced testing lab ensures product quality, and we provide a hassle-free returns process. Additionally, dedicated account managers offer personalised service and regular visits, supported by specialised marketing assistance for trade customers.
The Lindy Academy
The Lindy Academy is our dedicated training and knowledge platform, designed to empower partners and customers with practical AV and IT expertise. We provide structured product training, application guidance, and technical insights to support confident specification, installation, and sales. As a CTS-accredited provider, the Academy enables AV professionals to earn recognised Continuing Education credits while strengthening skills, improving project outcomes, and reinforcing Lindy’s position as a trusted authority in professional connectivity. Visit The Lindy Academy.
At Your Service
Experience free technical support both before and after purchase. Our UK based Product Specialists have access to our full portfolio of products and can advise on features, performance, installation and troubleshooting.
Delivering The Goods
A comprehensive range of IT and AV connectivity solutions, available for immediate dispatch. Offering extensive selection and prompt availability, we ensure a seamless purchasing experience with fast delivery.
The Trade Specialists
We are the B2B specialists. Our partnerships with major distributors and our team of Account Managers help us build relationships to meet your business needs whether you’re a reseller, installer, or commercial user.
