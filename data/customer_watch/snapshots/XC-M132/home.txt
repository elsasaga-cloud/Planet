Since 2001
Built by a distributor who bet on IP early
ABP Technology was founded in 2001, when IP telephony and IP video were still unproven next to analog and TDM systems. The bet was on interoperable, open-standard IP technology, and on a distribution model built around a narrow, carefully chosen portfolio rather than a warehouse of everything.
That same approach — pick fewer products, know them well, and support the partners who sell them — is what the AI Voice and AI Video path is built on today.

Preconfigured. Remote. Managed.
More than half of what we ship leaves preconfigured
Preconfigured device fulfillment is a core ABP competency, not a side service — IP phones (Fanvil, Snom, Yealink, Grandstream), cameras, and the hardware behind advanced multisite deployments, including AI-enabled devices, speakers, pagers, door stations, and access control. Instead of configuring on-site, your team wires and mounts — the rest happens remotely through IPTECHVIEW.
IPTECHVIEW has been running since 2019 and today manages video, access control, and IP infrastructure for partner deployments worldwide.
ABP distributes and supports IPTECHVIEW as our primary solution for technology partners — one platform that scales from small projects to nationwide multisite deployments with thousands of cameras, from simple cloud video surveillance to AI video supervision.
