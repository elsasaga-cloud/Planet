How we chose
A distribution line card is easy to grow and hard to prune. Ours got pruned. We dropped vendors whose products had stopped evolving, whose support had thinned out, or whose roadmap no longer pointed anywhere our partners were going.
What is left is a curated set: vendors with current products, a real roadmap, open standards rather than closed ecosystems, and the kind of long-term value a partner can build a business on. Fewer names, better answers — and people here who have actually deployed what they sell you.
