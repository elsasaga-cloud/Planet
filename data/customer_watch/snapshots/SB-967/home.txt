Who are we?
Basithami Technology, formerly trading as PD Getyeza Security Services, has been operating in the Security Installation and Technology industry for the past 20 years.
The business was founded in the year 1999 by PD Getyeza. In 2020 the company changed its name from PD Getyeza Security Services to Basithami Technology following its strategic diversification and upscaling.
BASITHAMI Technology is one of the preferred service providers for companies like Standard Bank Insurance and SA Home Loans, and we have been serving on their panel of suppliers for the past 15 years.
Our service offerings include Assessing; Auditing; and Electronic Repairs, Maintenance and Installation.
We pride ourselves for having good trade relationships with our distributors and suppliers. This enables us to meet our clients' needs with a dynamic mix of offerings and solutions.
