Communications
Effective communication is vital for any organization to thrive. It ensures seamless collaboration, improved productivity, and better customer service. Cozzmic solutions provide clear and uninterrupted communication with our VoIP phones or traditional phone systems and paging systems, improved signal strength and coverage with our cell signal boosters, and quick and efficient messaging with our SMS partners. Trust us to keep your business running smoothly and efficiently. VOIP Phone Systems Paging Systems Cellular Booster
Security
Security is paramount for any organization to safeguard its assets and employees. Our alarm systems, alarm monitoring, access control, and video surveillance solutions ensure that your business is protected against theft, vandalism, and other security threats. Our solutions provide real-time monitoring, access restriction, and video evidence, ensuring that your business is secure at all times. Trust us to keep your
business safe and secure. Access Control Alarm Systems Video Surveillance

At Cozzmic, we are committed to providing the highest quality products and services, at a highly competitive rate. We bring exceptional communications, technology and security solutions to our customers. Our team of experts and professionally trained specialists are here to provide superior customer service and to allow businesses to run their businesses and not worry about the rest of it. Your success is our success.
Cozzmic serves clients across the Greater Toronto Area, including: London, Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Brantford, Hamilton, Woodstock, Haldimand, Norfolk County, Burlington, Oakville and the Niagara Region.
About Cozzmic
Cozzmic started as Cozzmic Communications in 2013 as a small business phone specialist in the new VoIP market. In 2019, Cozzmic rebranded and merged with BCT Communications and BCT Security which have been providing telecommunications services for 25 years, and security services for 35 years. Continuing to expand our technology services, we are proud to become your new one-stop-shop for innovative phone, internet, security, and infrastructure solutions.
