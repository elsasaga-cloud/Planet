ATI Overview
Accurate Technologies Inc. develops advanced ECU code rapid prototyping, measurement, calibration and data acquisition solutions for automotive, aerospace, defense and marine industries. ATI’s hardware and software tools support embedded controls development, real-time testing and network analysis, helping engineers accelerate innovation, improve performance and streamline validation across vehicle and system applications.
