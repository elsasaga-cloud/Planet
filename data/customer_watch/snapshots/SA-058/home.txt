Frequently Asked Questions
What areas does Alpha Net Communications serve?
Alpha Net is headquartered at 11313 Lone Shadow Trail in San Antonio and serves businesses throughout the San Antonio area, Austin and the I-35 corridor, the Hill Country, and South Texas, including New Braunfels, San Marcos, Boerne, Kerrville, Laredo and Corpus Christi. We also take on larger commercial projects in Houston and Dallas.
Do you provide residential home network cabling installation?
Yes. We install home network cabling using Cat 6 and Cat 6A cable, running Ethernet to home offices, TVs, gaming setups and access points, plus IP camera and Wi-Fi upgrades for homeowners across San Antonio and Austin.
Do you work with Google Fiber, AT&T Fiber, Spectrum, Astound and GVTC?
Yes. Many of our residential customers use Google Fiber, AT&T Fiber, Spectrum Fiber, Astound Internet and GVTC Communications on 1, 2, 5 and 8 Gig plans. Your provider brings fiber to the house; we wire the inside so that full speed reaches every room, not just the router.
Can you fix slow Wi-Fi or dead zones at home?
Yes. We test your home's wiring and equipment to find what is slowing things down, then fix it with new cabling, wired access points or a mesh Wi-Fi system.
What types of network cabling do you install?
We install voice and data cabling using Cat 5e, Cat 6, Cat 6A and Cat 6A shielded cable, along with fiber optics, demarc extensions, data drops and telephone cross connects for offices, warehouses, campuses and multi-site businesses.
Do you install fiber optic cabling?
Yes. We install single-mode and multimode fiber, including backbone cabling, terminations, splicing and testing, with LC, SC, ST and MPO connectors supporting speeds from 10 Gb up to 400 Gb.
Is your cabling tested and labeled?
Yes. Every cable run we install is tested and labeled, so your network is documented and easy to troubleshoot later.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Alpha Net Communications is a licensed and insured communications contractor.
Can you install after hours or on weekends?
Yes. We can schedule installations after hours or on weekends so your business keeps running without disruption.
How much does network cabling installation cost?
Cost depends on the number of drops, the cable type (for example Cat 6 versus Cat 6A or fiber), distances, the building's construction and ceiling access. Contact us with your project details for an accurate quote.
What Wi-Fi and wireless options do you offer?
We design and install Wi-Fi 7, Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 6 networks, as well as wireless point-to-point gigabit links between buildings.
Do you install business phone systems?
Yes. We install VoIP and hosted IP phone systems, including Yealink hosted IP phones and Avaya IP Office, for businesses needing anywhere from a few phones to a few thousand.
Do you offer emergency network support?
Yes. Alpha Net offers 24/7 emergency network support. Call 210-654-0653.
How long has Alpha Net been in business?
Alpha Net Communications has served South Texas since 1986. It was founded by Thomas J Cline and is now led by president Nathan "Nate" Cline.
Are you a low voltage contractor?
Yes. Alpha Net is a San Antonio low voltage contractor handling structured cabling, fiber, phone systems, Wi-Fi, IP cameras, access control and cellular signal boosters for offices, schools, medical offices, warehouses and industrial sites.
Do you repair existing network cabling?
Yes. We troubleshoot and repair existing network cabling, and we also handle server room and data center cabling and cleanup.
Do you handle cabling for new construction?
Yes. We plan and install voice, data and fiber cabling for new construction and tenant build-outs, working alongside your general contractor.
Do you offer a warranty on cabling installations?
Yes. Manufacturer-certified cabling installations with extended system warranties are available. Ask us about warranty options when you request a quote.
