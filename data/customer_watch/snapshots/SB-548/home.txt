G.fast (as used in the NV-450EKIT ) excels at delivering very high bandwidth at short to medium distances, making it ideal for high-speed point-to-point links where performance is critical. VDSL2 (as used in the NV-202GEKIT ) is optimized for long-range stability , providing reliable connectivity over extended distances using 2-wire cabling. For shorter cable runs that use Cat5 or coaxial cable instead of 2-wire pairs, the NL-230FKIT offers a simple, budget-friendly alternative with 100Mbps or 10Mbps negotiated performance depending on cable length, and extended reach up to 800 meters.
