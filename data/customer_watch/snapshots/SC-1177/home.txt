About Projexon
Who we are?
Projexon was established to be an international business consultancy provider committed to design and integrate solutions that are not only practical, but effective at generating sustainable economic value for large, middle and small organizations. Our clients base includes (Trading, Industrial & Commercial, Investment Management and Oil & Gas Industry).
Our team is comprised of leading international professionals who have a proven reputation for creating customer oriented solutions that allow clients to sustainably increase the shareholder value of their company. We do this by working with you to transform your organization via our strategic consulting services, allowing you and your business to begin achieving your goals as quickly and effectively as possible.
What we do?
At Projexon, we hold a vision to provide our clients top high quality advisory services, we analyze their requirements and deliver appropriate business solutions to meet the expected level of competencies set by their organizations. Our professional services include among others Corporate Strategic Consulting & Implementation, Corporate Growth & Innovation ,Business Process Mapping & Re-engineering.
Projexon has expertise in providing comprehensive, leading edge and relevant corporate seminars and Business workshops. We support to strengthen and update the knowledge and skills of personnel at all levels within organizations through professional business consultancy meetings and development.
Our Consulting Team are hand-picked for each project based on their skillsets and competencies. All our consultants come with a proven track record and extensive experience in their respective fields. The team is sourced from all over the world and work on client projects based on their match to the requirements of each project.
