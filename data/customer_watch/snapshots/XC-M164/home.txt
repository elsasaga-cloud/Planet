Your Trusted Automation Solutions Partner
Founded in 1977, TMMI helps industrial process and manufacturing teams across the Rocky Mountain region solve complex automation and control challenges. From improving system reliability and efficiency to modernizing infrastructure and supporting long-term scalability, we work alongside our customers to deliver practical solutions that fit real-world operations.
With decades of hands-on experience in automation, electrical controls, and industrial software, TMMI serves as a trusted partner—not just a supplier—helping organizations design, support, and evolve systems that perform today and adapt for what’s next.

Working with organizations across a wide range of industries, TMMI helps teams improve operational performance, streamline automation systems, and overcome complex technical challenges. We partner with customers to strengthen their processes, support innovation, and deliver consistent, high-quality results—without adding unnecessary complexity.
