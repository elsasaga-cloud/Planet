Project designer
By your side in the design of security systems
With the aim of providing a high-level service, we have a specific Systems Design team that assists you in the project development phase by drafting offers, technical reports, and operating diagrams in response to supply specifications.
Do you work in system design?
Our technical team offers specialized support during the design phases
Certified and guaranteed systems
Our ability to innovate is confirmed by the numerous patents and certifications obtained over the years. EL.MO. products are certified by IMQ and comply with the most stringent international standards .
A dedicated portal
To support the design process, we have developed a powerful work tool for engineering firms: the Designers’ Portal . With maximum ease, users can create bill of quantities, access technical manuals and product documentation, consult completed projects , and interact directly with our consultants .
Why choose EL.MO. as your technology partner?
A 50 years history
Quality is the foundation of all reliable security systems. Proof of this lies in product certifications such as EN 50131 and CPR 305/2011 , company certifications like ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and SA8000 , and our fifty years of experience.
Integrated solutions at the service of people
Our intrusion detection, fire detection, CCTV, and access control systems work in synergy and integrate seamlessly to provide comprehensive building security management.
Made in Italy
The Made in Italy philosophy is the foundation of each of our products: conceived according to criteria of functionality, technological innovation, and reliability , crafted with attention to every detail, and topped off with that distinctive style that makes its Italian nature unmistakable.
Consulting and support at every stage of the design process
The EL.MO. technical department stands alongside designers, offering highly specialized support throughout every stage of the project development process .
Our steps toward a winning solution
Data collection
Preliminary collection of all information
At this stage, we ask designers to share the available documentation, such as floor plans or images, in order to assess the condition of the premises, the customer's needs, any vulnerabilities and any existing technology that needs to be integrated and/or replaced. The type of activities carried out in the environment must then be assessed: this is useful for predicting any interference and carrying out a risk assessment.
Equipment positioning
Layout-based products simplify design
After examining the floor plans, we create a meticulous layout of the devices, maximizing system efficiency while complying with current regulations, design choices, and customer requirements. The layout provides an overall view that makes it possible to assess any critical points in the structure. At this stage, a collaborative exchange with the design firm begins to carry out modifications or additions.
Technical report
Summary document explaining the choices made
One of the most frequently requested supporting documents is the technical report. It is a summary document that explains the philosophy behind the project and justifies the technical solutions adopted. It is particularly important as it outlines the systems’ capabilities and the context in which they are implemented. Specific regulatory references are included, and the proposed products are described. The integrations between systems, as well as the methods for operating and managing the installations, are also presented.
Bill of quantities
The list of selected products, with prices and quantities
Following an in-depth study of the project development, our team of technicians can prepare the bill of quantities for the equipment related to special security systems, including specification descriptions, quantities, and both unit and total list prices for each product. The bill of quantities can be considered a sort of “product list” that details all the required devices. This documentation is then shared with the design firm to fine-tune the final details.
Block diagrams
Graphs illustrating the structure of systems
In the design of advanced systems, it is often the case that substantially different and heterogeneous installations are proposed, such as CCTV, intrusion detection, fire detection, and access control systems. However, these integrate with each other to provide a comprehensive security solution. To better understand the structure of such complex systems, the Systems Design team produces block diagrams for each subsystem or for the overall solution. This makes the structure of each system/block, as well as the interactions and integrations among the various systems, clearly visible.
Connection diagrams
Document illustrating the connections between elements
To support the documentation, a more advanced block diagram can be provided, showing the structure of the wired connections of the entire proposed system. It indicates some basic features, such as cable thickness or type (e.g., RS-485). This information represents highly valued support for those who will later install the system. This type of document makes the basic structure of a system even clearer and allows for quick understanding.
