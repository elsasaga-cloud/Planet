About Process Technology, Inc.
Process Technology, Inc., located in Salt Lake City, Utah, has been making your applications work since 1986. With branch offices located in Arizona and Colorado, we serve customers in Utah, Wyoming, Idaho, Northern Nevada, Arizona, New Mexico, Colorado, and Montana.
We are committed to being recognized as the industry leader in process instrumentation and automation control. We achieve this goal by providing:
Well trained, competent sales professionals
Technically trained, dedicated inside sales support professionals
Commitment and dedication to both our customers and our vendors
A large amount of inventory to meet our customers needs
Quality, industry leading products and support
Since 1986 Process Technology, Inc. has prided itself on outstanding customer service.
We have the products, we have the knowledge, we have your solution.
