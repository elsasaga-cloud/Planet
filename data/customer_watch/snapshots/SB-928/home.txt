Welcome to SEVEN STARS
Seven Stars group of companies, which has diversified business segments such as Car Rental, Transportation, Auto Garage, Auto Spare Parts, Software and IT Solutions. Seven Stars first started operating as a Car Rental company in 1991 with the base in Abu Dhabi, UAE. After been a successful Rental company, Seven Stars decided to expand the business to other emirates along the way to better serve our customers.
