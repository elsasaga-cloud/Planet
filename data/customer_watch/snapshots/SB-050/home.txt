About
Introduction,
PT. Anugerah Artha Guna (AAG) was founded in 2010. At the beginning of the establishment of AAG, we entered into a work contract with PT. Polymatech Indonesia to provide maintenance support for all computer devices and servers there.
Over time and experience, we have gradually obtained orders from various companies and industries, both in Jakarta and in the industrial areas in Bekasi, Karawang, and Cikampek.
For office and industrial applications, we are currently focusing on developing ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) applications using ERP adri ODOO (Open Demand Open Object).
The ODOO ERP that we will use depends on the Client’s request, it can use the free ODOO Community ERP or use the paid ODOO Enterprise ERP.
In addition to application problems, we are also experts in installing computer networks and building data centers.
Both Application and Computer Network design, as well as Data center construction will be adjusted to the budget owned by prospective customers.
