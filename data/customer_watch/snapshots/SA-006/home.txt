We build the Earth's digital connectivity in Asia Pacific.
Resonant Networks has been building optical networks since year 2013 from metro, backhaul to submarine cable networks.
ADC Subsea Cable System
We have extensive experience in deploying the Submarine Line Terminal Equipment (SLTE) for the ADC subsea cable system across multiple locations: Singapore, Hong Kong, and Japan. This work has been crucial in establishing a reliable and high-capacity data transmission network between these key regions in Asia, supporting the growing demand for international connectivity and data traffic.
AAE-1 Subsea Cable System
We successfully built the AAE-1 subsea cable system SLTE in Penang, Malaysia. Following this, we extended the terrestrial network, building critical links from Penang to Singapore, further enhancing regional connectivity and ensuring seamless data transmission across both the subsea and land-based segments of the network.
An Experienced and Passionate Team
We are a highly skilled and experienced team specialising in building optical networks across the APAC region. Our expertise spans the design, deployment, and maintenance of advanced optical infrastructure, ensuring robust, high performance networks that meet the growing demand for connectivity in the Asia Pacific. Our team’s dedication and deep technical knowledge make us a trusted partner for delivering critical optical network solutions in this dynamic and fast evolving market.
AI-Ready
in Data Centre Solution
We offer AI-Ready Data Centre solutions designed to meet the evolving needs of modern digital infrastructure. Our expertise includes Robotic Optical Fibre Cross Connect and OCS (Optical Circuit Switch). Additionally, we can build digital twin of your data centre network to avoid outages, verify compliance and accelerate change windows. As opposed to monitoring or testing live traffic, we can perform exhaustive and definitive analysis of network implementations and behaviour, including proving network isolation or security rules. This ensures seamless operation, optimal performance, and scalability for AI and other demanding workloads.
Future Now:
Photonic Compute and  DWDM network with PQC and QKD
We are working on Photonic Compute technology for AI, enabling faster, more efficient processing and with very much less power required. In addition, we provide advanced DWDM (Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing) network solutions integrated with Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) and Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) technologies. These solutions ensure secure, high performance data transmission, safeguarding networks against future quantum computing threats while delivering the bandwidth and speed necessary for AI driven applications.
Optical Technology is rapidly emerging as the future of telecommunications and computing due to its unparalleled speed, bandwidth, and energy efficiency. Unlike traditional electronic systems, optical systems use light to transmit data, enabling faster and more reliable communication over long distances with minimal signal loss. In computing, optical interconnects promise to break through the limitations of electronic data transfer, offering significant improvements in processing speed and power consumption. As data demands continue to grow exponentially, integrating optical technology into networks and processors is becoming essential for building the next generation of high performance, scalable digital infrastructure.
We stand at the forefront of these innovative optical technologies, seamlessly integrating such technologies to optimise and secure your digital infrastructure. Our approach is customer focused methodology, ensuring your digital infrastructure is resilient and highly scalable.
