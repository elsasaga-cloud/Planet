Foss Fiber is a Norwegian manufacturer of fiber optic solutions. The company specializes in delivering bespoke fiber optic solutions to customers in a range of industries, including telecommunications, oil and gas, and the public sector. Foss Fiber was established in 1987 and since then has become one of the largest manufacturers of fiber optic solutions in Norway. The company has a wide range of products, including standard cables, tailor-made solutions and special cables designed to withstand harsh environments. Foss Fiber has also specialized in developing advanced fiber optic solutions to meet specific customer needs.
