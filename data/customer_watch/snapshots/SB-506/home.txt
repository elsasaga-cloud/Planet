DIN Rail Fiber Patch Panel DPPY12 BK Series with LC Duplex Adapters
The DPPY12 BK Series DIN Rail Fiber Patch Panel with LC Duplex Adapters neatly and orderly stores up to 16 optical splices from up to two fiber optic cables. Made with powder-coated aluminium and designed to be compact (only 54mm in width), the DPPY12 BK Series can be installed alongside other fiber optic equipment in the same fiber optic server cabinet to reduce deployment costs.
Key Features:
* Time-saving installation – Simplifies assembly for faster deployment
* Cost-Effective Solution – Offers high performance at an economical price
* Space-Efficient Design – only 54mm in width, maximising rack space

10G Ethernet Media Converters (Unmanaged)

10 times faster transmission than Gigabit Ethernet
Plug-and-play with auto-media detection between copper or fiber media

Armoured Unitube Cable PE FUTGCP
FUTGCP is a uni-tube fiber optic cable with a single central tube filled with water-blocking gel to prevent water penetration. It also comes with a layer of corrugated steel tape armouring which serves as protection against rodent attacks. Therefore, the FUTGCP fiber optic cable is suitable for direct burial and harsh environment installations.
Benefits of FUTGCP fiber optic cable
- Ease of installation
- Suitable for direct burial
- Economical in cost
