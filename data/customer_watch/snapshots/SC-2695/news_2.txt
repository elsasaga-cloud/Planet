EverFocus IVS-50P0 Featured in Intel® Edge AI Catalog, Advancing Intelligent Transportation and Edge AI Computing
Share：
Powered by Intel® Core™ Ultra Series 3 processors, the IVS-50P0 delivers rugged edge computing capabilities for next-generation intelligent transportation applications.
NEW TAIPEI CITY, Taiwan, October 22, 2026 – EverFocus Electronics Corp.’s IVS-50P0 in-vehicle computer is now listed in the Intel® Edge AI Catalog as a Qualified Intel® AI Edge System. The entry features the company’s fanless platform for smart transportation, fleet management, and rugged edge computing.
Through Intel's Edge System Qualification (ESQ) framework, qualified systems are evaluated against performance, reliability, and compatibility requirements, helping solution builders and enterprise customers identify suitable platforms for edge AI deployments. The inclusion of the IVS-50P0 highlights EverFocus's continued development of Intel-powered edge computing platforms designed for intelligent transportation and demanding industrial environments.
Powered by Intel® Core™ Ultra Series 3 Processors
The EverFocus IVS-50P0 is a rugged in-vehicle computing platform powered by Intel® Core™ Ultra Series 3 processors, supporting up to 64GB of DDR5 memory.
Designed to support demanding edge computing workloads, the system combines advanced processing capabilities with flexible connectivity and industrial-grade reliability.
Key features include:
Intel® Core™ Ultra Series 3 processors: Providing the computing foundation for demanding Edge AI applications.
Wide-range power input: Supporting 9–55 VDC with ignition control for vehicle-based deployments.
Rugged industrial design: Designed to operate in temperatures ranging from -20°C to 60°C.
Flexible connectivity: Featuring CAN FD, isolated digital I/O, 2.5GbE Ethernet, and optional PoE connectivity.
Expandable architecture: Supporting dual front-access SSD bays and optional 4G LTE, 5G, and Wi-Fi expansion.
These capabilities provide a flexible computing foundation for integrating AI workloads, vehicle systems, sensors, and communication technologies.
Enabling Next-Generation Intelligent Transportation
As transportation systems increasingly adopt AI-driven technologies, the demand for reliable computing at the edge continues to grow. The IVS-50P0 is designed to address the requirements of intelligent transportation, commercial fleet management, and vehicle-based edge computing.
By supporting local data processing and providing multiple industrial communication interfaces, the platform enables system integrators and solution providers to develop intelligent transportation applications that require responsive computing and reliable connectivity. Its rugged design and flexible expansion capabilities also make it suitable for industrial edge deployments operating under challenging environmental conditions.
Strengthening EverFocus's Edge AI Ecosystem
The IVS-50P0's inclusion in the Intel® Edge AI Catalog represents another milestone in EverFocus's continued development of Edge AI and intelligent transportation solutions.By integrating Intel processing technologies with industrial computing expertise, EverFocus aims to provide scalable and reliable hardware platforms that help businesses implement AI across transportation and industrial applications.
EverFocus will continue expanding its Edge AI portfolio and collaborating with technology ecosystem partners to support the development of practical AI solutions across industries.
