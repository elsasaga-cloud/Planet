What We’re Showcasing at ITCN Asia 2026
Live demos of the Cisco-powered networking, security, and collaboration technology Comtech deploys for enterprises across Pakistan, stop by our booths to see it in action.
View all solutions
Switch
Cisco Catalyst 1200/1300 and 1300X series managed switches for SMB and branch deployments, alongside Catalyst 9200L and 9300 series switches, Cisco’s enterprise-class stackable access-layer platform used for campus and data center networking.
Firewall
Cisco Firepower 1010 Next-Generation Firewall (FPR1010-NGFW-K9), a compact, fanless NGFW built for small offices and branch locations, delivering malware protection, intrusion prevention, and daily threat updates from Cisco Talos.
Network Router
Cisco Catalyst 8300 and 8200L series Edge Platforms, purpose-built for SD-WAN, security, and multicloud connectivity at the branch, the same routing platform behind Comtech’s SD-WAN deployments.
Cisco Wireless (Access Points)
The latest Cisco Wireless 9172I, 9176I, and 9178I Wi-Fi 7 access points, alongside the Catalyst 9130AX Wi-Fi 6 AP, built for the coverage, density, and low latency modern campuses and hospitals need.
Cisco DESK
Webex Desk Mini, Desk Pro, and Board Pro devices, Cisco’s all-in-one collaboration hardware for hybrid meetings, whiteboarding, and huddle spaces, run on Cisco’s RoomOS platform.
Server
Cisco UCS C220 M7 rack server, a 1RU, 2-socket general-purpose server built on 5th Gen Intel Xeon processors, supporting virtualization, AI/ML, and data-intensive workloads.
IP Phones
Cisco IP Phone 8941, 8865, and 7841, enterprise VoIP endpoints ranging from HD video-capable executive phones to cost-effective desk phones for general staff.
Meraki
Meraki MX68 and MX95 cloud-managed SD-WAN security appliances, alongside the MS130 cloud-managed switch, Cisco’s fully cloud-native networking and security platform, deployable and monitored remotely with zero on-site configuration.
