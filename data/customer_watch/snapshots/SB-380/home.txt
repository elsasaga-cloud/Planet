How it works
One system working for everyone.
Easy for your staff, hands-off for facilities, and fewer lockers for your property team to manage.
Your people
Book with an existing credential.
Staff card, phone wallet or PIN: the kiosk allows your staff to book and open a locker directly at the bank. No keys to hand out.
Your people
Open with mobile.
Staff can also book ahead and open their locker from our Yellowbox app, Outlook or Teams, so it’s ready before they arrive.
Facilities
The admin runs itself.
The dashboard flags unused lockers and emails staff an automatic reminder. Custom workflows, locker types, curated reports and an access audit trail handle the rest.
Property
Fewer lockers, more space.
Temporary locker settings, booking limits and automations keep lockers free for whoever needs one.
