Do you do your repairs in-house, or send them out to be repaired?
Unlike many of the other repair and warranty companies, we do ALL our repairs in house by our professionally trained repair technicians.
Do you take school POs ?
Yes. We accept blanket purchase orders to cover multiple repairs, or single POs to cover just one lot of repairs.
Who pays for shipping?
We pay the shipping both ways. We’ll email you shipping labels to send the items to us. If you are close enough to us, we offer free pick-up and delivery.
Does your Pay as Needed service come with a warranty?
Yes, all units repaired come with a One Year warranty for the parts and labor we performed.
Can you sell me Chromebooks or Apple units?
Yes, we carry both New and Refurbished Chromebooks, and have a substantial inventory of Apple products in stock. We are also a Google partner so we can handle your Google EDU licenses for you.
