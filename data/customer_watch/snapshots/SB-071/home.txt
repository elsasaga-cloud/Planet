Corporate business
Established in 1996, Nayon Kontrol Systems is a technology-driven company specializing in smart SCADA and automation solutions for electric utilities and various industrial sectors. Since its founding, the company has grown into a full-fledged corporation while remaining committed to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction. Throughout its 30 years of operation, Nayon Kontrol Systems has proudly retained its original name, reflecting its stability, strong reputation, and enduring presence in the industry.
