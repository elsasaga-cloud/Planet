OUR COMPANY
TGM Global Pte Ltd (TGM) is the global service provider to owners and operators of online Condition Monitoring including the Partial Discharge, Distributed Temperature Sensing and Vibration online monitoring & services for major companies on their electrical assets. The company expanded to provide SF6 purification/recycling solution and IoT and Cybersecurity solution.
Our office is strategically located in the Singapore for our valuable customers in Asia.
We are also closely associated with reputable suppliers and together with our strong team provides full system integration and maintenance support for our valuable customers with the installed based at site.
