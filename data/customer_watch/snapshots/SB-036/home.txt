Inthouse Hub and Inthouse Hub Pro are cloud gateways with enhanced features. Each Hub can connect up to 9 PLCs to the cloud providing remote user and service connection, notifications, Wi-Fi hotspot and client and a lot of additional features. Every Hub has lifetime cloud access included in the price.
