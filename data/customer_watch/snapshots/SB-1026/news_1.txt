EticSIM: Simplified IoT Connectivity
EticSIM: Simplified IoT Connectivity for Your Industrial Equipment.
In the world of connected industry, data availability is essential. Yet, managing cellular connectivity often means multiple contracts, recurring bills, and administrative complexity.
With EticSIM, Etic Telecom offers a simple and effective approach:
✅ A prepaid offer with no subscription fees
✅ Optimized multi-operator coverage throughout Europe
✅ Data volume tailored to real-world needs
✅ Centralized management of your SIM card fleet
✅ Advanced options: public IP, outgoing SMS, pre-installed SIM
Designed for users of IPL VPN routers and RAS machine access devices, the EticSIM solution offers a true one-stop shop for seamlessly deploying and operating your connected infrastructure.

EticCUBE solution: 4G antenna extension up to 100m
Connecting industrial equipment from an area with poor coverage is a challenge. Many constraints must be addressed (antenna extensions, equipment power supply, on-site work).
EticCUBE s a simple and robust turnkey solution for deporting connectivity as close as possible to the field up to 100m.
Advantages of the solution:
✅ Waterproof and durable outdoor enclosure (IP68 / IK08)
✅ Complete solution with integrated PoE injector and splitter
✅ Compatible with RAS-C-100 and IPL-C-100 4G VPN routers
✅ 5-year warranty
✅ Prepaid SIM card for 36 months pre-installed (optional)
Designed for demanding industrial environments, EticCUBE facilitates the deployment of remote maintenance, monitoring and communication solutions on the most challenging sites (mines, quarries, basements, etc.).
