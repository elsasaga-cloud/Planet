Five football stadiums
The Closed Circuit Television Systems at five football stadiums.
The Cyprus Sports Organization trusted Wave Electronics with the implementation of CCTV systems in five football stadiums throughout Cyprus namely Antonis Papadopoulos, G.S.Z, Tasos Marcou (Paralimni), Paphiaco and Ethnikos Ahnas. These systems from CBC (Europe) include high resolution fixed and PTZ cameras which are monitored and controlled via matrix and keyboard / joystick equipment from the Control Rooms. The events and images are continuously been recorded on Digital Video recorders for further Police investigation.
