We are Arcobel: supplier of industrial pcs, embedded IoT systems and network solutions for the industrial sector in the Benelux and Germany. We are experienced in composing multi-vendor systems that consist of the best and most suitable components. We develop and assemble standard and custom made embedded hardware solutions that seamlessly fit our customer needs and expectations.
Our experienced team approaches each project from a technical perspective. A proven mix of our in-depth understanding of our customer needs, technical knowledge, extensive experience and a reliable worldwide network of suppliers results in the best possible solution for our customers.
We look forward to meeting you and discussing how our technical knowledge can enable you to enhance your industrial processes or activities. Whether you need specific advice or are looking for a sparring partner; please contact us, we are happy to help.
