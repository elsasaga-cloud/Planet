SERVICES
Automatic Number-Plate Recognition (ANPR) Systems
ANPR systems are effective in helping businesses deter security threats such as theft, fly-tipping, unauthorised parking, and more. In addition to managing security threats, ANPR systems enable businesses to manage and secure their car parks. Here at Safeguard Systems, we work with leading brands such as Hikvision, Dahua, and Avigilon. Interested in an ANPR security system for your business? Our experienced team of security system installers is here to help.
Business & Commercial CCTV Systems Installation
As experts in business and commercial CCTV, we’ll install a security system to specifically cater to your requirements. The range of business CCTV systems we design and install includes high definition (HD) ultra HD, IP, and AI Solutions. Working with CCTV manufacturer brands such as Dahua, Hikvision, and Avigilon we can install a CCTV system for all needs and budgets.
Access Control and Door Entry Systems Design and Installation
Looking for an access control system to secure entry to and from your business premises? We can help. The suite of access control systems we install includes door entry, fob access systems, IP, video, and audio intercom. These access control systems help manage access to your premises and monitor time, attendance, staff productivity, and more. We work with access control manufacturer brands such as Interlogix, Gallagher, and Paxton.
Security & Intruder Alarms for Businesses
Deter criminals with a robust, highly effective security alarm system. Reduce insurance costs and create peace of mind with a fully installed intruder alarm. The range of security and intruder alarm systems we install covers wired, wireless, and remotely monitored solutions. TRK Solutions works with leading alarm brands such as Orisec, Honeywell & Texecom. Looking for a commercial security alarm to protect and secure your business? We’re on hand to help.
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Security Systems
Artificial intelligence for video surveillance utilizes computer software programs that analyse the audio and images from video surveillance cameras in order to recognize humans, vehicles, objects and events. The A.I. program functions by using machine vision. We have regular training sessions to ensure we are on the cutting edge of products that can reduce false alarms and allow better solutions with less cost.
ABOUT
We design and install highly reliable, robust, and cost-effective security systems to meet a range of security needs and objectives. When it comes to security system installation, our experienced team are experts in CCTV, video intercom and video door entry systems, access control, and security alarm installation.
CCTV installation: High Definition & IP CCTV video surveillance systems. Monitored CCTV installation and CCTV monitoring systems.
Access control & video door entry systems (video intercom): Access control systems, keyless door entry systems, video, audio Intercom, and video door entry systems.
Security alarm installation: Wired and Wireless security alarm systems. Monitored alarms, remote alarm monitoring, and perimeter alarm systems.
TRK Solutions employees are experts in security system installation for a range of industries. We can install CCTV, access control and security alarm systems for construction sites, schools, office-based businesses, gyms, nightclubs, hotels, pubs, shops, retail outlets, warehouses, farms, factories, car dealerships, abattoirs, and car parks.
Operating from five locations across Berkshire, Hampshire, Oxfordshire, and Wiltshire, TRK Solutions LTD are big enough to deliver for the customer but small enough to care. Our team of professional, friendly security system installation engineers in Newbury, Southampton, Reading, Oxford & Swindon are all DBS checked, carry ECS cards and are PASMA & IPAF qualified.
Whatever your security system requirements, our experienced team of CCTV, access control and intruder alarm experts are on hand to understand your needs and provide you with the right security system installation for you.
