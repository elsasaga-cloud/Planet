How We Can Support You
Connectivity Without Limits
Ground Control works with multiple satellite networks, including Iridium , Viasat , and Globalstar , to ensure uninterrupted global coverage. Whether your devices are stationary, mobile, or airborne, we match the right network to your needs, now and as they evolve.
Total Control Over Your Data
Cloudloop gives you real time visibility and management of your IoT devices, airtime, and data, all through a single platform. With API-first integration, you can automate workflows, control costs, and ensure your data is exactly where you need it, when you need it.
Scalable, Future-Proof Hardware
We design and build our own satellite IoT and tracking devices , ensuring they’re rugged, power efficient, and optimized for real world applications. Our modular approach means you can scale from a single unit to a full fleet with minimal integration effort.
Reliable and Secure Data Transmission
Our unique Delivery Network enhances data security, while providing unparalleled insight into deployed devices. With end-to-end encryption and customizable security levels, your data stays protected while flowing seamlessly between devices and applications.
How Remote IoT Comes Together
Understanding the Mission
We start by understanding what your application needs in the real world. That includes where your assets are, whether they move, how much data you need to send, how secure it needs to be, what power is available, and how quickly that data needs to arrive.
These details matter early, because they shape the constraints of the job, and help define what a workable solution looks like from day one.

Gateway & Network Connectivity
Once that’s clear, we design the right gateway and communications path. That can include sensor connectivity, edge processing, and the satellite link itself. Because we work across multiple satellite networks, we can choose the mix of throughput, latency, coverage, and cost that fits the job.
Cloudloop Platform & Integration
Cloudloop is the layer that brings devices, subscriptions, and data together. It gives customers clear control over deployments and a straightforward way to route data into the systems they already use. Access is available through a simplified, well-documented API, making integration easier.

Environmental Monitoring
Ground Control keeps remote environmental sensors online in places other networks can’t reach. From water level gauges and flood monitoring stations to data buoys and coastal cameras, we provide low power satellite IoT and mixed satellite / cellular links that slip easily into your own hardware.
You get a simple, robust path from sensor or logger to cloud, so manufacturers can ship connected products, and operators can keep critical sites visible without constant site visits.
For research teams and government agencies, we turn hostile, off-grid locations into routine data points, streaming measurements for flood risk, water quality, soil moisture and even iceberg and sea ice movement in near real time.

Maritime
Ground Control delivers resilient connectivity and positioning solutions tailored to the maritime sector. From small fishing vessels needing trusted tracking for regulatory compliance, to coastguard and patrol fleets that rely on precise situational awareness, our vessel tracking services provide secure, real time visibility wherever you operate.
For merchant shipping, our Assured PNT (Positioning, Navigation and Timing) solutions offer a robust alternative when GPS is jammed, spoofed or otherwise unavailable, ensuring continuity of safe navigation.
Complementing this, our out of band management services keep you connected to critical onboard systems even when your primary satcoms are offline, and allow you to separate IT and OT networks for stronger cyber resilience and operational control.
