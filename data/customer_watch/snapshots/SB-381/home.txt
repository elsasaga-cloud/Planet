Our Services
We offer a curated selection of products catering to the unique needs.
On-Device Standard Solution
Secure reliable data acquisition at the edge. With over 80 protocol drivers from Modbus and OPC UA to Siemens, ABB, and IEC energy standards we connect virtually any industrial or energy device and deliver its data securely to the cloud, ready for analysis.
On-Device Advance Solution
AI-powered edge monitoring for data, video, and intelligence. The SG600 combines data acquisition, video surveillance, AI analytics, alarm management, and automated control in a single station enabling real-time visibility and automated response.
