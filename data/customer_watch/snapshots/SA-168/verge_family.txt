VERGE
Network Switch Family
VERGE is the edge switch family of Angora Networks.
VERGE Ethernet Switch Family offers reliable, high-capacity and new generation manageable solutions for your corporate network infrastructure that can meet your different access needs.
Helps protect sensitive data by maximizing the availability of critical business applications. In addition, it is equipped with features that will enable more efficient use of the bandwidth required by the application and data.
Thanks to its 8, 24, 32 or 48 fixed gigabit Ethernet and 4 10 gigabit fiber backbone connection interfaces, it has the capability and power to meet the unified communication needs of corporate networks in ever-growing working environments. It provides you with a more efficient and end-to-end, better connected working environment than ever before, with the ideal combination of installation and usability, top-of-the-line service facilities and capabilities to meet corporate networking needs.
Enterprise Applications
VERGE offers high performance solutions for all your needs related to connecting your corporate network traffic, connecting user computers to a central network or transferring data, audio and video services.
