IT Solutions by Wood Communications
Networking Experts for Enterprise, Data Centres & Telecoms
Wood Communications is a leading distributor of IP, infrastructure and electronic security solutions for Data Centres.
Wood Communications can provide our partners with an end-to-end datacentre solution. Working with Global leading manufacturers including , AFL Hyperscale, Corning, Mobotix, Raritan, Servertech, Sumitomo, Riello , Great Lakes, and Many more. IT Infrastructure Training by Wood Communications
Network Training Experts
Wood Communications have two purpose built modern training facility, which can successfully deliver a comprehensive range of Certified training (City & Guilds) and tailor-made introduction courses to your requirements.
Our level of training caters for all subject areas including Fibre, Copper Cat5e/6/6a, Wireless, Data Centre, Portable Appliance Testing & IP CCTV. Everything from “how to install a Cat6a structured cabling” through to advanced networking and electrical testing of a data centre, our portfolio of courses is virtually unlimited.
