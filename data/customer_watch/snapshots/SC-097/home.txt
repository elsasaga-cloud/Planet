3Lines Advanced Technologies Company
We provide Advanced Technologies Globally
3Lines Advanced Technologies Group was established in 2019 in alignment with Saudi Vision 2030 to localize military and civilian services within the Kingdom. The Group delivers integrated supply chain and technical services — procurement, logistics, maintenance, repair and manufacturing — supporting operational readiness across the defense and advanced technology sectors, locally and internationally.
