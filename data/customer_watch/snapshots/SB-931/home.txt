Comprehensive wireless communication solutions for every need
Wave1 delivers diverse wireless telecommunications networks and infrastructure solutions, from licensed microwave systems to cost-effective licence-free options. We also create custom infrastructure designs that streamline network integration, enabling reliable and efficient communication systems. Explore our offerings to find the perfect fit for your organisation.
Licensed Microwave Systems
Gain guaranteed, interference-free performance with high speed and long-range capabilities. Our licensed microwave systems offer privacy and reliability, suitable for organisations of all sizes using 8-38 GHz frequencies.
Self-Coordinated Licensed Systems
As they are optimised for short-haul, high-density areas, our self-coordinated licensed systems provide high bandwidth over shorter distances, offering multi-gigabit Ethernet links with flexibility and low licence costs.
Licence-Free Radio Links
With 5.8GHz and 2.4GHz bands, our licence-free radio links offer long distances and substantial throughput rates. It is ideal for low-use air space regions, eliminating annual telco fees and cutting overhead costs.
Infrastructures
We offer expert infrastructure design that supports wireless telecommunications networks. From licensed or unlicensed microwave networks, our custom metalwork includes mobile trailers, bespoke towers and durable poles for a range of applications.
