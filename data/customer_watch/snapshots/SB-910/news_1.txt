Safe remote control of the PLC
You can now remotely control your PLC via the web pages hosted on the Etic Telecom device.
By using the secure M2Me connection and initiating a simple HTTP session from your PC, smartphone, or tablet, an operator can now:
✅ Easily read and write to the PLC’s industrial variables, utilizing Modbus TCP/RTU, OPC UA, and Uni-Telway protocols.
✅ Benefit from granular permission management based on individual roles and levels of responsibility.

05/11/2026
Remote maintenance for your machine: MFA for the M2Me solution
Attuned to the needs of the industrial sector and its connectivity challenges, we are enhancing the capabilities of our machine-access gateways with a major upgrade: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for our M2Me remote maintenance solution.
🔐 Enhanced security through Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
In addition to a password, a one-time code is sent via email with every login attempt.
The benefits:
Improved protection against password theft.
No additional hardware or licenses required.
Functionality integrated directly into the router’s firmware.
🎯 This new feature is available for download from our website (firmware 4.13.6) or included with any new purchase of Etic Telecom gateways.
