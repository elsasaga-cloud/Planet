Fibre Optic Manufacturers & Suppliers
Fibre Optic Systems (FOS) is a fibre optic manufacturer, distributor and solutions provider headquartered in Knoxfield, Melbourne. FOS has operated for 25 years and holds a passion and commitment to bring the latest technology to the industry.
Initially founded out of a desire to provide high volume, efficient and economical local production of all fibre optic assemblies, FOS has since grown to become an industry leading solutions provider. With expertise, excellence in manufacturing and commitment to innovation, FOS is well equipped to remain the experts in connectivity.
What We Do
FOS specialise in rapid production of fibre optic and copper products. With years of experience in the industry, we are capable of manufacturing and delivering products in a timely manner. Significant investment in manufacturing technology and our refined manufacturing process allows FOS to offer industry leading turnaround from design to manufacture and delivery.
Fibre Optic Systems sets itself apart from the competition by working closely with our clients to ensure we meet or exceed any project delivery and budget requirements.
