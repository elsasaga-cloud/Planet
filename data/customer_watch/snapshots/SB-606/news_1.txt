Asset Insight
Transform your operations with Asset Insight, providing real-time data and analysis to inform data-driven decisions, ensure optimal fleet health, and improve mining productivity.
​
Asset Insight is a cutting-edge digital solution that captures and analyses telemetry data from your entire fleet of mining equipment, including: Mining trucks , Shovels, Loaders, Dozers, Graders.
Key Benefits
Data-Driven Decision Making: Make informed decisions with real-time data and analytics, optimizing mining efficiency and productivity.
Real-time Fleet Health Analysis: Monitor the health and performance of your fleet in real-time, identifying potential issues before they become major problems.
Operator Driving Analysis: Evaluate operator driving behaviour and provide personalized feedback to improve safety, reduce fuel consumption, and lower maintenance costs.
Customizable Dashboards: Create personalized visualizations to track KPIs, identify trends, and pinpoint areas for improvement.
Predictive Maintenance: Establish custom rules and alerts to predict potential issues, reducing downtime and extending asset lifespan.
Seamless Integration: Integrate Asset Insight with existing systems and services, ensuring a unified and efficient operational framework.
In-Depth Analytics
Equipment Utilization: Track equipment usage and identify opportunities to optimize asset allocation and reduce idle time.
Fuel Consumption: Monitor fuel usage and identify areas for improvement, reducing fuel costs and minimizing environmental impact.
Maintenance Scheduling: Use predictive analytics to schedule maintenance, minimizing unplanned downtime and reducing maintenance costs.
