ABOUT US
Established in 2014 as a subsidiary of Al Rajhi Building & Construction
Al Rajhi Telecom Company is a Saudi-based technology solutions provider serving government, commercial, industrial, and telecommunications sectors. Specializing in ICT infrastructure, MEP systems, ELV solutions, software development, cybersecurity, telecommunications, and data center and power services, the company delivers comprehensive, end-to-end solutions.
Backed by a team of certified professionals, Al Rajhi Telecom offers everything from consultation and design to implementation and ongoing support. By partnering with leading global vendors, the company ensures tailored, cutting-edge solutions that meet the evolving needs of its clients.
Al Rajhi Telecom remains committed to empowering organizations through technology that drives digital transformation and operational excellence.
