AI Networks
AurCore provides a zero-loss, high-radix networking foundation purpose-built for AI and Machine Learning. By eliminating fabric bottlenecks, we ensure your data moves at the speed of innovation while maximizing the ROI of every GPU.
Data Center Networks
AurCore provides a high-density, open networking foundation engineered for the demands of modern hyperscale and enterprise data centers. By delivering a scalable, high-bandwidth fabric, we eliminate connectivity bottlenecks and lower the total cost of ownership (TCO) for your digital infrastructure.
Enterprise Networks
AurCore delivers a secure, L3-intelligent foundation for modern campus environments. We provide seamless 1G, 2.5G, and 10G connectivity optimized for high-density IoT, Wi-Fi 7, and hybrid work.
Industrial Networks
AurCore delivers a rugged, deterministic networking foundation purpose-built for the transition to Industrial AI. We provide unbreakable 1G/10G connectivity from the factory floor to the control room, managed via our unified ACC Cloud or ACN On-Prem platforms.

AurCore Networking Operating System
Intelligent automation, lossless performance, and always-on reliability for modern data centers
Intelligent Automation
Zero-touch provisioning and VXLAN-EVPN overlay networks enable rapid, scalable deployment across data centers and enterprise environments.
Lossless Performance
RoCEv2, ECN, and priority flow control deliver the low-latency, congestion-free forwarding that AI and HPC workloads demand.
Always-On Reliability
MLAG, VRRP, and sub-50ms ERPS failover ensure continuous operations with seamless redundancy.
Device Intelligence Meets Centralized Control
AURC-NOS
AURC-NOS is a high-performance, modular network OS purpose-built for AI and cloud. It delivers deterministic performance, ultra-low latency, and lossless Ethernet for resilient, large-scale fabrics.
ACC Cloud & ACN On-Prem Platforms
AurCore’s ACC and ACN platforms provide a unified network management ecosystem, offering flexible cloud-based oversight and secure on-premises operations for our entire 1G to 400G portfolio
SONiC
AurCore offers a production-ready SONiC solution validated by PalC Networks, providing a scalable, disaggregated alternative to proprietary systems for high performance switching infrastructures.
