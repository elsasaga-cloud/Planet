LITECH Telecommunication Products
Established in 1999, Litech Sdn Bhd has developed a successful synergy between the evolving global telecommunication requirements and innovative technical solutions with our proudly presented brand – LITECH.
With the passage of time, the demand for LITECH products grew beyond borders and the thoughts of its global presence also strengthened. In 2018 LITECH Pakistan Private Limited was established to cater to Pakistan’s local market demands.
In Pakistan we are serving the high profile customers like PTCL, Wateen Telecom, Zong, Mobilink, and other recognized Fiber Optic laying companies. We are also working with our distributors all around Pakistan to establish LITECH brand and supplying our products to the major Telecommunication Operators and turnkey contractors including Huawei, ZTE and other operators.
LITECH Pakistan is still striving to achieve its strategic goal to promote further growth of LITECH brand to become a globally recognized brand. We are also determined to provide the finest quality yet cost effective solutions to our customers by continuously developing and improving our products.
