Fiberdyne Labs, Inc. is a privately held, 100% U.S. owned, corporation located in Frankfort, New York.
The company was established in 1992 with a focus on Fiber Optic Cable Assemblies . Within a few years we started making Fiber Termination Boxes, Ethernet Media Converters, Attenuators, Fiber Splitters & Filters ( DWDM’s, CWDM’s, WDM’s ).
We continue to offer all of these products and more including: Cabling Installation Services , Fiber Characterization (FC) Services and Digital Signage.
Fiberdyne Labs, Inc offers all kinds of stock and custom-made BABA Compliant Fiber Optic Products. Ask your Sales Representative for more information.
