November 7, 2023 – ThinkParQ GmbH and System Fabric Works Announce New Joint Venture Company, ThinkinQ , and Appoint Kevin Moran as CEO
ThinkinQ’s primary objective to increase the presence of BeeGFS, a market-leading parallel filesystem, across North America through offering localized USA based sales and technical support services directly to their network of partners and end users. The company will be led by CEO Kevin Moran, who has many years of experience with delivering high performance solutions in HPC, AI, Federal Government and Oil and Gas throughout the USA. [more]
December 7, 2017 – System Fabric Works and ThinkParQ Partner for Parallel File System
AUSTIN, Tex. and KAISERLAUTERN, Germany, Dec. 7, 2017 — Today System Fabric Works announces its support and integration of the BeeGFS file system with the latest NetApp E-Series All Flash and HDD storage systems which makes BeeGFS available on the family of NetApp E-Series Hyperscale Storage products as part of System Fabric Work’s (SFW) Converged Infrastructure solutions for high-performance Enterprise Computing, Data Analytics and Machine Learning.
