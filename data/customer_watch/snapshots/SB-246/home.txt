ABOUT THE COMPANY
IS Networks
From the very beginning of the IS NETWORKS Sieci Komputerowe company, the main aspect of our activity is to provide professional services in the field of design, assembly, repair, rework and modification, and maintenance of current computer networks based on copper cables, fiber optic cables, and wireless networks. We carry out complete IT systems installations based on structured cabling systems from various manufacturers, eg AMP, LEGRAND VDI, 3M, KRONE, Telegartner, CobiNET, BELCONN and others.
