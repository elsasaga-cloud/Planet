SOLUTIONS
This is where we provide
more security
Our solutions are used in areas where a reliable and resilient video network is indispensable – for example, in city and traffic surveillance, in corrections, or in the protection of critical infrastructure.
ALL AREAS
Road Traffic
Road traffic video surveillance detects violations, accidents, and traffic flows, thereby improving safety and efficiency on our roads. Our solutions play a significant role in all areas of road traffic where a resilient network is essential.
Justice and the penal system
High security standards apply in justice and penal system. Our switches have proven their worth there thanks to their reliability, unique cybersecurity functions, and their ability to process large amounts of data.
Freight and Logistics
In modern logistics, comprehensive video surveillance of storage areas, transshipment points, and transport routes is essential, and it contributes to the optimization of operational processes. Leading companies in the freight and logistics sector rely on our Switches to obtain a reliable solution for their demanding video and process monitoring requirements.
INTEGRATION
Seamless Integration with VMS and PSIM Systems
By seamlessly integrating barox switches into leading VMS and PSIM platforms, you can optimize the management of your network infrastructure. Network events and device status information are visualized centrally, enabling faster detection and resolution of issues. Remote reboots and automated responses to predefined events minimize manual effort and reduce costly on-site service visits. This saves time, simplifies troubleshooting, and increases operational efficiency in day-to-day operations.
