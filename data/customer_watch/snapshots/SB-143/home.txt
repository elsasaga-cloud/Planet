THE PROBLEM
Your Residents Deserve Better Than Big Cable.
Every year, HOA boards and property managers across Florida hear the same complaints from their
residents: slow internet during peak hours, service outages with no explanation, technicians who've never
been to the property before, hold times measured in hours, and rates that creep up without warning.
The reason is structural. National providers like Comcast and Spectrum use shared coax infrastructure that
was never designed for today's bandwidth demands. They outsource their service calls to third-party
contractors who are evaluated on speed, not quality. And when your board calls corporate, you're one of
hundreds of thousands of accounts - nobody knows your property, nobody owns the relationship, and
nobody is accountable.
Your community is paying for a service model built around shareholder returns, not resident satisfaction.
THE BROADSTAR ADVANTAGE
A Better Model. Built Around Your Community.
Dedicated Fiber (AON)
Your own fiber pair to every unit. Not shared with your neighbors. Not throttled during peak hours. Consistent speeds 24/7.
Dedicated On-Site Technician
A full-time BroadStar tech assigned to your property. They know your building, they know your residents, and they provide same-day service. Never outsourced to a third-party contractor.
In-House Support Center
Our West Palm Beach team answers every call. Monday through Saturday 8am to midnight, Sunday 8am to 10pm. Plus, your community gets its own dedicated phone number - no general 1-800 maze.
Dedicated Account Manager
Your board's direct line to BroadStar corporate. Quarterly check-ins, marketing material updates, event coordination, and a single point of escalation for anything your community needs.

OUR TECHNOLOGY
Why Dedicated Fiber Matters
Most fiber providers use a technology called G-PON. It’s cheaper to deploy because it uses a passive splitter to share a single fiber strand among up to 64 units. The problem: when your neighbors are all streaming at 8pm on a Tuesday, everyone’s speeds degrade.
BroadStar uses AON, or Active Optical Network. Every unit in your community receives its own dedicated fiber pair from our central equipment. Your bandwidth is yours alone. Peak hours don’t affect your speed. Your neighbor’s 4K binge session doesn’t touch your Zoom call.
It’s the same architecture used by hospitals, financial trading floors, and government facilities. We just happen to bring it to condos and apartments.
