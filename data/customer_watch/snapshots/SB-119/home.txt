Build Future-Ready Networks at Scale: Managed Wi-Fi & Connectivity for Multifamily, HOA & COA Communities
Wi-Fi for Multifamily Properties: Internet, Security, and Infrastructure Built to Scale
We design and manage enterprise-grade networks for multifamily and MDU communities to boost resident satisfaction, reduce network downtime, and stabilize your network. Every solution is custom-built, clear, and ready to scale with your property portfolio.
MANAGED SERVICE FORWARD THINKING
Our deep roots as a telephone company and managed Wi-Fi provider may have shaped us, but the evolution of technology has driven us to leverage all the possibilities modern technology solutions can provide. We pair our unique ability to find and build fiber optic connectivity with best-in-class hardware manufacturers and vendors to simplify your technology project.
We invest heavily into network and process automation and ensure only the most state of the art cloud based building technology systems make it to your property, resulting in a seamless and cost saving standardized approach whether your portfolio has one property or hundreds.
TELECOMMUNICATIONS CARRIER HERITAGE
WireStar has a rich history in fiber optic and carrier operations. We leverage this experience to bring the most reliable and affordable fiber optic communications to your property. Our status as a CLEC (Competitive Local Exchange Carrier) is a unique advantage for our clients and allows WireStar to build fiber optic communications infrastructure in the right of way.
Still planning your project? Let WireStar analyze proximity of fiber optic resources in your area so you can make the most informed decision about where to build.
