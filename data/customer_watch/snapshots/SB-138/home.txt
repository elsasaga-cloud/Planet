Why Volktek
Established in 1994, Volktek is a professional original design and manufacturing service company of market certified networking technologies headquartered in Taiwan with sales offices in the USA, India. We design products with focus on the end-user, and manufacture a wide-range of high-quality radiation-free optical devices in house and deliver them to our network of partners and distributors across the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia-Pacific. Our products support current and next-generation Ethernet platforms for Metro, Surveillance and Industrial applications.
