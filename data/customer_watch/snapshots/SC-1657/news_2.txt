SAN TELEQUIP
Over the last three decades, SAN Telequip has evolved from process control and security surveillance solutions to advanced VPN Routers, Managed Industrial Ethernet Switches, and IIoT Gateways etc. Today, we serve diverse sectors including Building Automation, Oil & Gas, Telecom, Power & Energy, Renewables, Metro & Rail, ATMS/TMS, Data Centers, Mining, Healthcare, Infrastructure, and Industrial Automation. We design and manufacture rugged, high-reliability devices that connect systems, processes, and people in mission-critical environments. Driven by strong in-house R&D, we are advancing in Edge Computing, Secure Industrial Networking, TSN, Cybersecurity, 5G Technology and Cloud Connectivity – delivering future-ready solutions for next-generation infrastructure and Industrial Networks.
