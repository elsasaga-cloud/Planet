We choose Sony imaging sensors because image quality is fundamental to effective CCTV. Consistent colour accuracy, strong low light performance , and proven reliability are why Sony remains our standard across the Zxtech range.
We design with 4K as the baseline, not an upgrade. Higher resolution delivers clearer identification, stronger evidence , and systems that remain relevant as security expectations continue to rise.
Fusion Light is Zxtech’s proprietary approach to CCTV illumination, designed to deliver colour evidence only when it matters . Cameras run discreetly in infrared as standard, activating white light on the detection of people or vehicles, then returning to IR automatically. This intelligent, integrated design improves evidence capture while reducing unnecessary light and visual impact.
CCTV systems are expected to run continuously, often for years. Zxtech products are built with durability in mind, using carefully selected components and testing standards suited for long term operation.
We believe IP is the future of CCTV because it offers flexibility, scalability, and control. Our systems are designed to integrate seamlessly into modern networks and adapt as security needs evolve.
Security requirements never stand still, and neither do we. Zxtech continues to invest in imaging, system design, and technology development to deliver CCTV solutions that move forward with the industry.
