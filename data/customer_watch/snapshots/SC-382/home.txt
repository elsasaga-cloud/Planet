Trade
Over the past years, Security Warehouse has grown into one of South Africa’s leading imports and distributors of electronic security equipment. We are committed to delivering exceptional customer service by providing personalised, high-quality, and cost-efficient solutions to meet our client’s needs.
Our continuous research and development ensure that our customers receive proven solutions and products that meet our stringent application and operational standards.
With eminent quality brands, Security Warehouse can offer our clients a much larger choice of security solutions . We continuously search to find the latest technology within the security industry globally, ensuring our product offering remains diverse and suitable to our client’s specific needs.
Africa Exports
At Security Warehouse, we’re proud to extend our trusted security solutions beyond South Africa to serve the entire Africa continent. With decades of experience in delivering state ‐ of ‐ the ‐ art systems, we’re now offering the same commitment to quality, innovation, and customer service to every region in Africa.
Building on our trusted legacy in South Africa, Africa Exports is our initiative to empower businesses, communities, and governments across Africa with cutting ‐ edge security solutions. Our mission is to provide tailored, reliable, and technologically advanced security systems that safeguard lives and assets, ensuring peace of mind wherever you are on the continent.

Training
The Security Warehouse Training Academy was established in 2005 and serves as a private training institution. We have over 20 years of reputable experience in the electronic security equipment, project, and training industry. The Training Academy is registered with both PSIRA & SASSETA.
Our objective is to provide practical training and knowledge for a professional level of competency in the electronic security industry. Our training staff is well qualified and hold relevant qualifications. Our bespoke training courses are designed to deliver maximum benefits in the key areas of today’s industry. We are committed to ensuring that our clients and their staff are always ahead of their game by providing personalized, high-quality, and cost-efficient training.
