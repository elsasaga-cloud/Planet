AETEK NDAA & TAA COMPLIANT PRODUCTS
Understanding the NDAA
The John S. McCain National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year 2019, signed into law on August 13, 2018, and effective from August 2019, is a significant piece of legislation. Specifically, Section 889 prohibits federal agencies, their contractors, and grant or loan recipients from procuring or using &ldquo;telecommunications and video surveillance equipment or services&rdquo; from specific vendors. This section contains several provisions that impact existing and future U.S. federal government-related video surveillance deployments.
&nbsp;
Complying with NDAA Regulations
The NDAA enforces rigorous regulations concerning the utilization of products and services derived from banned component vendors. Fortunately, numerous exceptional alternatives are available. AETEK has carefully vetted our supply chain to ensure none of our products or components are sourced from the banned vendors listed in the NDAA.&nbsp;
&nbsp;
What is TAA?
The Trade Agreements Act (TAA) of 1979 is another crucial regulation for government procurement. TAA mandates that products sold to the U.S. Government must be manufactured or substantially transformed in a designated country with which the U.S. has a trade agreement. This ensures that the government only purchases high-quality products from trusted sources. Customers can trust that AETEK solutions will seamlessly integrate into their government, military, and critical infrastructure projects without any regulatory concerns.
&nbsp;
AETEK&#39;s Commitment to Compliance
AETEK is dedicated to adhering to the strict standards of security, reliability, and quality set by the U.S. Government. Our NDAA and TAA compliant product lines are designed to meet these rigorous requirements, ensuring that our customers can confidently deploy our solutions in any setting or application.
No matter the setting or application, AETEK has the solutions for your enhanced PoE network.
