Oct 27, 2026
Security notice: AETEK devices not affected by PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619)
AETEK devices run no PHP and are not affected by PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619). No action is needed on AETEK devices. Status: not affected Products: AETEK PoE switches, media converters and battery backup products Action: none needed on AETEK devices On 21 October 2026, two critical vulnerabilities in PHP, known as PHP2Shell, were disclosed. CISA has urged immediate patching. AETEK devices run no PHP. Not in the web management, not in the embedded NTS. A reminder: PHP may run on other systems in your network. Keep PHP up to date on any server or PC that uses it. When a critical vulnerability is disclosed, we check our products right away and tell you clearly where you stand. Questions: sales@aetektec.com.
