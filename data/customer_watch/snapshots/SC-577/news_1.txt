Leap Networks has been Awarded ADNOC ESTIDAMA Package 3 Project - UAE
Leap Networks has received the multimillion dollar contract for the telecommunication and security system for ESTIDAMA Sales Gas Pipeline Network Enhancement Package 3 - New Pipelines and Associated Facilities Project in Abu Dhabi from ADNOC.
Our EPC Client has selected LEAP NETWORKS as its Telecom Systems Engineering & Integrator for the ESTIDAMA Package 3 Project.
Leap Networks scope of work as the telecom Systems Integrator includes Detailed design, engineering, Project Management, Systems Integration, procurement, FAT, IFAT, installation & commissioning of Telecommunication systems in the Project.
The scope of supply covers SDH System, CCTV, PIDS, IDS, Telephone and Structured Cabling System.
Leap Networks Secures Jazan Refinery and Terminal Project - KSA
Leap Networks has received the formal purchase order for the telecommunication systems package for the Jazan Refinery and Terminal Project Pkg-12 in Saudi Arabia by our esteemed client.
The main EPC contractor has selected LEAP NETWORKS as its Telecom Systems Integrator for the Jazan Refinery and Terminal Project.
Leap Networks scope of work as the telecom Systems Integrator includes Detailed design, engineering, Project Management, Systems Integration, procurement, FAT, IFAT, installation & commissioning of Telecommunication systems in the Project.
Leap Networks Secures RAPID Project
Leap Networks with its two Joint Venture partners in Malaysia has been appointed by a leading oil & gas operator of Malaysia as Nominated Telecommunication System Integrator for Refinery And Petrochemicals Integrated Development (RAPID) Project and its packages in Malaysia.
RAPID aims at building a world-scale integrated refinery and petrochemical complex. The refinery will have a capacity of 300,000 barrels per standard day and will supply naphtha and liquid petroleum gas feedstock for the RAPID petrochemical complex, as well as produce gasoline and diesel that meet European specifications.
