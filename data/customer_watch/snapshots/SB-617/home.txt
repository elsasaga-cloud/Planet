IPC2U — Industrial Computing with Character
Industrial Personal Computer 2U GmbH is an international supplier and system integrator of industrial computing solutions with more than 25 years of experience. Operating across Europe and the EMEA region, we support customers with manufacturer-independent consulting, fast response times and technically validated systems.
We design and supply industrial PCs , embedded PCs, panel PCs , industrial edge servers, workstations , 19 inch Rackmount Computer and industrial networking solutions – from high-quality standard products to fully customized platforms. Our services cover the entire project lifecycle, from professional consulting and platform selection to system assembly, OS pre-installation, testing and documentation.
With ISO 9001 quality management certified processes, long-term available platforms and close partnerships with leading manufacturers in Europe and Asia, we ensure reliability, lifecycle stability and future-proof solutions for industrial, edge and AI applications.
