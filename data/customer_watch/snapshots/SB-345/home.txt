Fibre Optic Solutions Built Around Your Requirements?
Meeting the Demands of Modern Connectivity with Anderson Corporation
Since 1988, Anderson Corporation has helped Australian organisations select and source fibre optic products matched to their applications, environments and project requirements.
Established Experience: Australian owned and operating since 1988, Anderson Corporation brings more than 35 years of specialist fibre optic experience to every project.
Solutions for the Application: From indoor and duct installations to aerial and harsh-environment networks, we help customers select cable construction suited to the actual installation conditions.
Configured to Your Requirements: Specify the fibre type, fibre count, cable length, connectors and configuration you need. We supply custom cable lengths, patch leads and pre-terminated assemblies.
Complete Fibre Capability: Source fibre optic cable, connectivity, enclosures, transceivers, termination equipment and test instruments through one specialist supplier.
