COMMON TECHNICAL QUESTIONS
Knowledge Base FAQs
Quick answers below. Refer to your exact product manual for specifications, electrical connections and installation procedures.
Where can I find instructions for my exact Tycon ® model? Open the Knowledge Base tab on the relevant Tycon product page, or use the product-family category links above. Tycon maintains these product articles in its support Knowledge Base.
What should I check if an off-grid power system keeps shutting down? Record the battery and load voltage under operating conditions, check the charge controller for alarms, and note any change in connected load or charging conditions. Follow the relevant RemotePro ® or UPSPro ® manual before adjusting settings or wiring.
How do I know if a PoE injector or switch is compatible with my equipment? Verify the device's required voltage and wattage, whether it supports IEEE 802.3af/at/bt or passive PoE, and the correct pinout and power budget. Do not assume that all equipment using the same connector or voltage is compatible.
What information do I need to size a remote solar and battery system? List each device's power draw in watts, operating hours, installation location, required backup duration, system voltage and any environmental constraints. Use the Tycon calculators or request free system design assistance .
Should I install firmware updates before troubleshooting? Confirm the exact model and hardware revision first. Read the applicable release notes and update procedure, and preserve settings or logs if instructed. Installing firmware intended for another product or revision can create problems.
What if the article does not solve my problem? Send the model number, a description of the fault, relevant voltages or network information, firmware version and any error messages through the technical support request form .
