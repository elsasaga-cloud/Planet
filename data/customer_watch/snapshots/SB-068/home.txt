Multirate Optical Transceivers
ICT Integrators provides OEM and Branded Multirate Optical Transceivers to enterprises for enhancing the bandwidth and ensuring the reliability of their networks.
We have quite a wide range of 100M, 1G, 10G, 40G and now 100G single fiber and dual fiber optical transceivers with both simplex and duplex connector type options, we also offer GPON Optical Transceivers to our customers.
ICT Integrators guarantee longer transmission distance and low signal loss of our transceivers to our customer, our solutions cover the networks based on Ethernet, SONET, SDH and FiberChannel.
The optical transceivers we offer are also capable of Digital Diagnostic Monitoring (DDM) that helps our clients to monitor supply voltage, transmitter output power, receiver input power, module temperature, laser bias current in real-time as long-term analysis of these parameters can help network administrators in prediction and avoidance of incoming failures.
