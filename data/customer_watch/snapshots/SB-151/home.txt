ABOUT
COMPLETE SECURITY
We are a security solutions provider specialising in the development, deployment and management of a wide range of dynamic technology-driven remotely monitored security solutions, services and systems primarily for the construction industry.
BACKGROUND
Rooted In Security
Optiwatch Limited was established in 2015 to provide remotely monitored video surveillance or cctv solutions primarily for the construction industry in London as the demand for more dynamic and cost effective technological security solutions increased.
The company has now quickly grown to offer other virtual services like access control, intrusion detection and fire prevention solutions making it a one-stop-shop for all site security and safety solutions.

SERVICES
FAILSAFE SUPPORT
We offer inclusive key holding and alarm response services along with all our
remotely monitored services, as well as static guards and mobile patrols to stand in for any unforeseen system or service failures.
STATIC GUARDING
We provide professional SIA licenced security personnel for temporary short or long term contracts as on-site static guards, gatemen, banksmen or traffic marshals. We work with our service partners to not only select the most suitable fit for your project requirements but we also equip them with the right skills and tools to carry out an outstanding job.
MOBILE PATROLS
We provide strategic mobile patrol services through our trusted partners who will deploy local units to look after your site as and when you’d like it monitored. This service is a good cost-saving alternative to a full-time static presence or CCTV system for sites that still need to be monitored on a regular basis or during specifically vulnerable times in the day or week.
KEY HOLDING
We provide specialised key holding services independently or in conjunction with our remotely monitored video surveillance, intrusion detection and fire prevention services to ensure a quick, effective and seamless response to any verified emergencies. We also partner with localised key holders where a site is more than 20-30 minutes from us for a quicker turn-around.
ALARM RESPONSE
We usually provide local alarm response services to support our remotely monitored or virtual security solutions for a proactive response to any triggered electronic alarms received, analysed and verified by our 24/7 remote monitoring centre. Our response teams will also usually hold the keys that grant necessary site access for effective threat mitigation.
