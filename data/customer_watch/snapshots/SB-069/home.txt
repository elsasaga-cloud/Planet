FAQs Questions We Hear Most From Clients
Covering software development, AI and agentic systems, cybersecurity, cloud deployment, and hardware manufacturing — the five pillars where Mutex Systems works alongside clients every day.
Software Development What does a custom software development company actually do?
A software development company designs, builds, deploys, and maintains bespoke
software tailored to a client's business. That covers the user-facing application, backend
systems, integrations, cloud infrastructure, security controls, and handover documentation.
Mutex Systems delivers the full lifecycle — from mobile apps and SaaS platforms to ERP,
marketplaces, and fintech applications — treating every project as a long-term product rather
than a one-off contract.
Software Development How much does custom software development cost in the UK?
A focused web or mobile MVP typically costs between 15,000 and 40,000 GBP.
A production-grade SaaS platform or marketplace usually runs between 60,000 and 200,000 GBP.
Larger multi-region enterprise programmes can exceed that. Mutex Systems provides a
fixed-scope written quotation after a paid discovery phase — never a ballpark guess on the
first call — so the budget is fully transparent before any build work begins.
Software Development How long does it take to build a custom software product?
A focused MVP — web or mobile — typically ships in 10 to 16 weeks. A full
production platform with integrations, payments, and admin tooling usually takes four to
seven months. Larger enterprise programmes run in phases over 12 to 24 months. An honest
timeline is always provided after a discovery phase, not on the first call, because
estimating without understanding the actual scope helps nobody.
AI, Agentic Systems & Automation What AI and agentic systems can Mutex Systems build?
Mutex Systems builds AI-powered applications, agentic workflow systems, LLM-powered
tools, AI chatbots, copilots for SaaS platforms, RAG-based knowledge systems, and secure
enterprise AI platforms. The practice covers both net-new AI product development and
integrating AI capabilities into existing business software — including CRM, ERP, and
operational workflows — so every build operates as a production system rather than a lab
experiment.
AI, Agentic Systems & Automation Which business processes are best suited to AI-powered automation?
High-volume, rules-driven tasks with structured inputs deliver the fastest returns:
invoice processing, data entry, report generation, customer routing, compliance checks, and
inventory updates. AI adds value beyond pure rules where judgement is involved — email triage,
lead scoring, document classification, and anomaly detection. Mutex Systems maps existing
workflows before recommending which processes will deliver the most reliable return on
automation investment.
AI, Agentic Systems & Automation How long does AI automation implementation take?
A focused automation for one workflow — such as invoice processing or customer
support routing — typically goes live in four to eight weeks. A broader programme covering
multiple departments generally runs over three to six months in phased rollouts. The timeline
depends on how well-documented the current process is and how clean the underlying data is.
Mutex Systems starts with a process and data audit before any technology decisions are made.
Cybersecurity What is penetration testing and why does my business need it?
Penetration testing is a controlled, authorised attempt to compromise your systems
using the same techniques a real attacker would use — finding exploitable vulnerabilities
before an adversary does. Any business handling customer data, financial transactions, or
regulated information benefits from regular penetration testing. Mutex Systems conducts web
application, mobile, API, network, and cloud infrastructure pen tests, each delivered with a
detailed findings report and remediation guidance mapped to OWASP and regulatory standards.
Cybersecurity How does Mutex Systems help with GDPR, PCI DSS, or ISO 27001 compliance?
Compliance is treated as an engineering discipline rather than a paperwork exercise.
For each framework — GDPR, PCI DSS, ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, or sector-specific regulators
such as FCA, SBP, and SAMA — Mutex Systems maps current controls against requirements,
identifies gaps, implements technical and procedural fixes, and produces evidence packs ready
for audit. The goal is a sustainable compliance posture built into operations, not a one-time
certification sprint.
Cloud, DevOps & Deployment What is DevOps and how does it speed up software delivery?
DevOps integrates software development and IT operations through shared tooling,
automated pipelines, and a culture of continuous improvement. A well-implemented DevOps
practice reduces deployment time from weeks to hours, catches defects earlier through
automated testing, and makes rollbacks safe and fast. Mutex Systems implements DevOps as a
full engineering discipline — CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, automated security
scanning, and observability stacks — not just a tooling layer on top of a slow existing
process.
Cloud, DevOps & Deployment How do you handle cloud migration without disrupting live operations?
Cloud migrations are planned in phases — assess, pilot, migrate, optimise — with
each phase fully validated before the next begins. Mutex Systems starts with a current-state
audit covering architecture, dependencies, data volumes, and compliance requirements, then
produces a migration plan with risk ratings for each workload. Critical systems migrate last,
after the team has built confidence on lower-risk workloads. Customers stay live throughout
using traffic-splitting and fallback mechanisms.
Hardware Manufacturing & Engineering What is custom business hardware manufacturing and what devices can Mutex Systems produce?
Custom hardware manufacturing involves designing and producing purpose-built
electronic devices and embedded systems for specific business operations. Mutex Systems
designs and delivers smart POS terminals, kiosk hardware, attendance and access control
devices, IoT sensors, edge AI devices, industrial control systems, and retail hardware.
The full lifecycle is covered — concept, PCB design, firmware development, prototyping,
quality assurance, and field deployment — for businesses that need hardware built around
their workflow rather than adapted from off-the-shelf products.
Hardware Manufacturing & Engineering Can Mutex Systems integrate custom hardware with software platforms?
Yes. Mutex Systems designs hardware and software as a unified system rather than
two independent components. Embedded firmware, cloud connectivity, IoT data pipelines, and
management dashboards are built alongside the physical device. This means the hardware and
software stack share a common architecture, security model, and update pathway — ensuring
the device operates reliably in production and can be managed, monitored, and updated
remotely at scale.
