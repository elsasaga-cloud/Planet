Owner
James Clendenan
James Clendenan is the founder and owner of James Clendenan Systems. James is a Computer Engineer, specializing in network management for internet service providers and small and medium businesses. A firm believer in start-to-finish solutions, James works with clients to ensure their needs are met every step of the way - from designing the network to installing the devices and pulling the cable to making sure the design works.
Prior to founding James Clendenan Systems, James worked in the media and entertainment, visual effects and virtualization industries. His past experience include stints with Pixar Canada, Sophos, HootSuite, and the Olympic Broadcasting Service (Vancouver and London). James Clendenan holds a B.SC in Computer Engineering from Queens University.
Outside of work, James is passionate about theatre and music. He is the Technical Director and a performer with the Broadway Chorus of Vancouver, and co-produces Broadway on Broadway Open Mic.
