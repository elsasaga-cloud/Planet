Why Choose Us?​
Quality is at the forefront of everything we do at Parallax Digital Technologies; from capturing requirements for products or project, through design and implementation, and finally this is carried through to manufacturing and in-life support. Our Life Cycle Management Process has been developed over many years and has been proven in many ODM/OEM organizations. We may not be the cheapest, and we may take a little longer to go through development processes than some other companies, but you will receive deliverables On Time in Full (OTiF), and Done Right First Time (DRFT) …. and within Budget.
