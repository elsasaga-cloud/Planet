Network connectivity starts at the physical layer.
Wirewerks designs and manufactures the cabling infrastructure networks are built on – the physical layer your network, your applications, and your business all depend upon.
For 35 years, Wirewerks has built connectivity solutions alongside the people who build networks. Our best products started with customers bringing us their hardest problems – and our team going deeper to solve them.
That’s 35 years of learning what holds, what fails, and what it takes to deliver a connection you can count on. We take no shortcuts. Every product ships factory-tested to one standard – so you don’t have to think about Layer 1, you can build on it.
