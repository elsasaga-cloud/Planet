About MTCO
Founded in 2004, MTCO is a leading supplier and distributor of advanced security automation solutions, providing a full suite of technologies for surveillance, access control, alarm systems, and more. As a trusted partner to Hikvision, MTCO delivers high quality, innovative security solutions designed to meet the complex needs of residential, commercial, and governmental sectors. With a commitment to excellence, MTCO serves clients across Lebanon, Ivory coast and beyond, offering scalable, customized solutions for large-scale projects and everyday security needs.
