NDAA Compliance: The International Passport to the Security Surveillance Industry
Originating from Section 889 of the U.S. National Defense Authorization Act, NDAA requirements have become a common language across global supply chains. Products that fail to meet NDAA standards may be excluded from government projects, multinational corporations, and critical infrastructure deployments throughout North America, Europe, and other regions.

The Evolution of Security in the AIGC Era: From Intelligent Surveillance to AI Visual Agents
In recent years, breakthroughs in Artificial Intelligence (AI) have transformed security surveillance systems from traditional video recording and forensic review tools into intelligent platforms capable of real-time analysis and inference. However, with the rapid rise of Generative AI (AIGC, AI-Generated Content), conventional AI-assisted surveillance is no longer sufficient to meet the increasingly complex demands of modern security environments. The industry is now entering a new era—the emergence of the AI Visual Agent. This evolution represents not only a technological advancement but also a significant milestone in the development of the security industry.
