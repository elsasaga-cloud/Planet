WELCOME TO THE Avensia Group
Avensia Group based in Dubai is a consortium of companies offering advanced services in General Trading, Information Technology, Facility Management and Technical Solutions. We started off as a general trading company in Dubai and soon extended our realms by contributing the diverse services to customers across the world. Avensia is now one of the best it equipment suppliers in Dubai offering solutions like installation of CCTV camera, PBX system etc. We are now contributing the best to clienteles in UAE, Middle East, Africa, India and Europe.
