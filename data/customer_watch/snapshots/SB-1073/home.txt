What Is Underground Seismic Security?
Buried sensors that feel an intruder coming, before a camera ever sees them
Most perimeter security relies on cameras or fences, things an intruder can see and plan around. SensoGuard buries seismic sensors underground instead, where they feel the vibration of footsteps, vehicles, or digging the moment someone crosses the line, with no visible hardware to spot or bypass. Each detection is then verified by an AI-powered camera before any alarm fires, so you get confirmed threats instead of false alarms from wind, rain, or wildlife. The result is a perimeter that works day and night, in any weather, without anyone ever knowing it's there.
Compare underground seismic to fence-mounted sensors and IR beams in our full perimeter security comparison , or see which product fits your site: Seismic Shield for private properties, Seismic Shield Pro for critical infrastructure, or InvisiFence Plus for a wired, camera-free virtual fence.
Field-Proven, Trusted by Government & Enterprise
SensoGuard's seismic detection technology was built by a team with over 20 years of combined seismic-sensor experience from Rafael Advanced Defense Systems, founded in Israel in 2012. Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about how SensoGuard's underground seismic detection works.
How does SensoGuard's seismic detection work?
Buried sensors feel ground vibration and use adaptive, DSP-driven algorithms to classify what's causing it, footsteps, a vehicle, or digging, the moment a threat crosses the line. There's no line of sight requirement and no visible hardware for an intruder to spot or bypass before the system has already detected them.
Does weather affect detection?
No. SensoGuard sensors are IP68 rated to MIL-STD-810G and operate from -40°C to 85°C (-40°F to 185°F), so rain, wind, fog, snow, and extreme heat or cold don't affect detection performance.
What's the difference between Seismic Shield, Seismic Shield Pro, and InvisiFence Plus?
Seismic Shield is built for private residential properties. Seismic Shield Pro pairs buried sensors with AI-verified PTZ camera tracking for critical infrastructure, borders, and large-scale sites. InvisiFence Plus is the wired sensor-cable-only system for sites that want a buried virtual fence connected directly into an existing alarm system, without a camera layer.
Can SensoGuard integrate with my existing alarm system or VMS?
Yes. Dry contact outputs connect directly to any existing alarm panel, and the system integrates with the VMS platforms most security teams already run, including IDIS, NX Witness, Milestone, FLIR, Digifort, mPrest, and Axis. See the full list on our integrations page .
Is SensoGuard used by government or military customers?
Yes. SensoGuard has supplied more than 3,500 unattended ground sensor systems to the Israeli Ministry of Defense since 2013, and the underlying technology was developed by a team with over 20 years of combined experience from Rafael Advanced Defense Systems.
How is this different from cameras or fence-mounted sensors?
Underground seismic detection has no visible hardware, no wires to cut, and no line-of-sight requirement, unlike fence-mounted sensors, which can be bridged or climbed between units, or cameras and IR beams, which can be spotted, avoided, or degraded by weather. See the full technology comparison .
