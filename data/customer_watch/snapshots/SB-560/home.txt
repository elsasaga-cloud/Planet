INE610 Industrial 10G Networking for AI Servers and Edge AI Systems
The INE610-LPSL is a high-performance, dual-port 10GbE industrial network adapter powered by the Intel® Ethernet Controller E610-XAIT2. Designed for AI servers, edge AI computing, and rugged industrial networking applications, it delivers reliable, high-speed connectivity with flexible multi-gigabit data rates of 10G, 5G, 2.5G, 1G, and 100Mbps through dual RJ45 Ethernet ports and a PCIe 4.0 x4 interface.
Featuring a compact Low-Profile Short-Length (LPSL) form factor, the INE610-LPSL is ideal for space-constrained industrial computers, high-density servers, and embedded AI systems. Its industrial-grade design supports an extended operating temperature range of -40°C to +70°C, ensuring dependable operation in demanding environments.
Engineered for enhanced electrical resilience, the adapter provides 8kV Hi-Pot isolation, surge immunity of 4kV line-to-earth / 2kV line-to-line, and IEC 61000-4-2 Level 4 ESD immunity (8kV contact / 15kV air). Backed by a 5-year warranty, the INE610-LPSL delivers a robust, compact, and reliable networking solution for next-generation industrial AI infrastructure.
