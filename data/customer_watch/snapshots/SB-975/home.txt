CCTV GLOBAL LONDON LTD
We are an authorised distributor for Hikvision, HiLook and AJAX supplying the worlds leading security products to the UK market. Based in Chobham near Woking, Surrey we stock a large range of CCTV cameras, recorders, alarms, intercom and access systems, supplying to customers throughout the UK & Ireland.
Online orders are shipped using DHL next working day delivery.
Customers are welcome to collect from our trade counter and visit our store first hand to view the latest security and access solutions.
Hikvision
Hikvision is the world leading manufacturer of CCTV equipment with a huge range of products and solutions from CCTV cameras, intruder alarms, intercom and access control.
Hikvision
HiLook
HiLook by Hikvision is a range of IP and Analogue CCTV products which offer great performance at a fantastic value, ideal for home and business, with easy set up and powerful Hikvision App.

AJAX

Toshiba

HAYDON
