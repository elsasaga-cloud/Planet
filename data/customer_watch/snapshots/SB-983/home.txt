Lighting the Way with Advanced Underground Technologies
For more than 40 years, NLT has led the industry in cap lamp and lighting innovation, introducing the world’s first intrinsically safe cordless cap lamp and the first Smart Cap Lamp designed to integrate third-party tracking and proximity tags. Today, we offer a complete line of lighting, secure charging and networking solutions purpose-built for the demanding conditions of mining, tunneling and heavy industry. From award-winning cap lamps and rugged IP67-rated strip lighting to compact charging lockers and high-speed network solutions, NLT products are trusted worldwide for safety, durability and dependable performance in the most challenging environments.
With a legacy of innovation and a focus on user-centered, AI-ready design, NLT continues to power the digital transformation of underground operations. Our serviceable, customizable Smart Cap Lamps integrate seamlessly with third-party technologies, while our ruggedized network features a modular plug-and-play system. Whether you’re outfitting a crew or preparing for the digital mine of the future, NLT delivers the reliable tools you need to stay productive and connected.
