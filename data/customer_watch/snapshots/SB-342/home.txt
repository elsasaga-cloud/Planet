UK Wide CCTV Solutions for Vehicle Usage
Servicing, Installation and Retro-fit
We can offer retro-fitting, installation of new products and servicing of vehicle CCTV equipment. Across the UK we currently employ eight fully trained engineers all operating a SURE Transport van. Alongside these engineers, we have access to an array of subcontractors to ensure no job is left undone.
This website is filled with information and specification sheets on the product range we can offer but if you cannot find something quite right for your vehicle then give us a call or fill out our form and we may be able to source a product similar to what you’re after.
Finally, with regard to fitting security to your vehicle we can offer plug and play CCTV kits for an easier installing experience. Software can be accessed using a desktop or mobile device for live view, tracking, and video/audio recording.

What Does SURE Enterprise Do?
SURE can provide and advise on suitable technology to monitor health and location of an entire fleet of public service vehicles. Enterprise is able to download video footage remotely from the CCTV systems on vehicles via WiFi and/or 4G.
Not only can the vehicles be tracked and monitored but you are also able to identify any errors in the equipment on-board the fleet vehicles. Engineers and support vehicles will also be added into SURE Enterprise so that all the footage, vehicle health and information you need in under one centralised management system.
These features and can be deployed within an operator’s own network or on a secure cloud-based platform.
