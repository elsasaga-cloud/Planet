Outdoor Security Lights
POE SECURITY LIGHTING
SMART | SECURITY | ACTIVE DETERRENT
PoEWit’s PoE Security Lighting is the active deterrent half of any AI-camera security system. Powered and networked by a single Cat cable using Power over Ethernet (PoE) — safe to install, simple to retrofit, no electrician required.
The moment your security camera detects a threat, the lights respond. Forced entry triggers RGBWW fixtures to flood red. After-hours motion pulses the affected zone. Vehicle in the lot after hours brings perimeter lighting to full brightness. Your camera saw it. Your lights stopped it.
PoE Security Lighting integrates natively with Verkada, Rhombus, Avigilon Alta, Eagle Eye Networks and more to come AI cameras. Models with PoE Pass-Through send power along to connected motion detectors and security cameras through the same Cat cable. Controllable with Control4, Crestron SIMPL, URC, and PoEWit Wall Switches.
A well-illuminated property remains the #1 theft deterrent. PoEWit makes that lighting intelligent, networked, and part of the security stack.

Recessed Lighting
RESPONSIVE LIGHTING
SMART | RESPONSIVE | MULTI-PURPOSE
PoEWit recessed lights. Drop-ceiling troffers. Custom architectural fixtures. Whatever shape and form the project demands — every PoEWit fixture is built on the same idea:
We don’t make lighting. We make programmable response infrastructure that happens to also be lighting.
Every PoEWit fixture is RGBWW and addressable, powered and networked by a single Cat cable. Pair it with any sensor on your network and the lights respond. Modern IP-connected sensors talk directly to PoEWit Cloud through webhooks. The other 95% — vape detectors, water leak sensors, glass break, panic buttons, fire panel outputs, HVAC alerts, door contacts — output dry contact closures or voltage signals because they predate the cloud. The PoEWit IP-1 brings them in. One PoE-powered device reads any dry contact or voltage trigger and makes it cloud-native. No sensor replacement. No new wiring. The infrastructure already in the building becomes responsive overnight.
Pulse blue when a vape sensor trips in a school restroom. Flash at the source when water hits the floor. Flicker visually for residents who are deaf or hard of hearing when the doorbell rings. Shift color when CO2 climbs in a conference room.
One installation. One Cat cable. Limitless applications.
Existing dealers know us for our recessed lighting. New customers are finding we’re not selling lighting at all — we’re selling a category that didn’t exist before: lighting that does whatever the building needs it to do. Control it through the free PoEWit mobile app, Control4, Crestron SIMPL, URC, or a PoEWit Wall Switch. Add a PoEWit Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) and the same fixtures keep working through utility power outages.
When something happens, the lights respond.

Smart Drivers
SMART DRIVERS
SMART | DRIVERS | SENSOR-READY
The MV-1 PoE Constant Voltage Driver is responsive infrastructure for everything that isn’t a PoEWit fixture. Two independent channels, each adjustable from 12V to 30V. Powered by Power over Ethernet (PoE) — safe for outdoor installation, and built to do what no other lighting driver does: act as a programmable response hub for third-party devices.
Run security or landscape lighting on one channel. Run an alarm siren, deterrent strobe, DC device (under 72W), or addressable LED strip (WS2811, WS2814, WS2805) on the other. Tie either channel to any sensor input on the network. A motion sensor fires both channels and the entire property responds — path lighting up, perimeter floods to full, audible deterrent triggered. A vape sensor fires the driver and a wall-mounted alert strip pulses. A camera detects a forced door and the driver fires a strobe at the entry point.
Compatible with Control4, Crestron SIMPL, URC, and PoEWit Wall Switches. Integrates with Verkada, Rhombus, Avigilon Alta, and Eagle Eye Networks security platforms through the same network it lives on.

Next-Level Networking
NEXT LEVEL NETWORKING
SMART | NETWORKING | CONVERGED-INFRASTRUCTURE
PoEWit’s enterprise-class networking equipment is cloud-managed, app-controlled, and built for the converged load of modern installations — PoE lighting, AI cameras, sensors, access control, intercoms, and wireless access points, all carried on the same Cat cable infrastructure.
PoEWit offers Layer 3 PoE+ and PoE++ network switches with industry-leading power budgets. Wi-Fi 6 access points include a stadium-grade 8×8 model, a high-performance indoor model, and an ultra-tough outdoor model for high-density environments.
