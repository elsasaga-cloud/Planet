WHO ARE WE?
15+ Years Of Undefeated Success
Foppex is a leading Communications Solutions Provider; with a deep know-how of telephony, security, access ,consultancy, network and VoIP solutions. Foppex Ltd is registered in the Republic of Cyprus by shareholders of Akçaba Communications in April 2010. The company’s main focus is the sale of PBX licenses and marketing of optical fiber products, mainly single-mode and multi-mode fiber optic pigtails and duplex patch cords of various length, connectors, adaptors, rack-mount patch panels, and wall-mount termination boxes.

Our Services
Foppex delivers innovative and integrated solutions in networking, telephony, security, and fiber optic infrastructure. Our services are designed to adapt dynamically to evolving environments and optimize real-time application performance, ensuring your business stays connected, secure, and future-ready.
Network Solutions
Foppex Technologies offers a comprehensive range of services for wired and wireless network solutions, optical fiber network solutions, and wide area network solutions. For wired network solutions, we specialize in the creation and arrangement of cable infrastructure to increase its efficiency, as well as the location and configuration of active network devices.
We provide optical fiber network solutions, including the creation of fiber optic cable infrastructure, detection, and repair of fiber optic infrastructure failures, and the location and configuration of optical fiber network devices. We also provide wireless network solutions
Telephony Solutions
Foppex Technologies offers a variety of PBX and VoIP solutions, including Dinstar , Xorcom , Wildix , VitalPBX , 3CX , and PigeonPBX . These solutions are tailored to meet the unique needs of businesses of all sizes, from small businesses to large enterprises. Foppex provides both on-premise and cloud-based PBX systems, offering flexibility and customization options to their clients.
With advanced features such as auto-attendants, call recording, voicemail, and call routing, among others, our telephony solutions enhance communication and collaboration among employees and customers. We also offer round-the-clock technical support to businesses.
Security Solutions
Foppex Technologies offers security solutions to help businesses protect their networks and sensitive information from cyber threats. Our security services include firewall positioning and configuration, VPN security, network access control (NAC) solutions, and network users security (EPM) software solutions.
With the firewall positioning and configuration service, Foppex Technologies helps businesses prevent unauthorized access to their networks and secure their data from external threats. We also provide VPN security solutions to ensure that remote workers can access the network securely and without risking data breaches.
