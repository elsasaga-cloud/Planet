The Physical Far Edge Has Not Kept Pace
AI Has Advanced. Its Physical Infrastructure Must Catch Up.
AI is moving to cell towers, streets, utilities and remote locations. Yet deploying it still too often means assembling separate power systems, networking, communications and computing equipment in the field. Each connection adds complexity. Unstable power, extreme conditions and disrupted connectivity make reliable operation harder. Modern intelligence requires a robust, secure, integrated physical foundation—with governance embedded by design.
INTEPO Establishes a Governed Infrastructure Foundation
INTEPO brings power, networking, communications, computing and storage together in a compact, ruggedized platform—with governance embedded directly into the hardware. Factory integration reduces field assembly and simplifies deployment through a plug-and-play approach. Local governance authorities preserve mission priorities while adapting operations to available power, connectivity and physical conditions. The architecture supports modern nodes for new installations and governance adapters for suitable existing infrastructure.
Engineered for Unpredictable Conditions at the Edge.
Designed for Continuous Operation.
INTEPO’s compact, ruggedized nodes are designed for the edge, where heat, cold, storms, unstable power and disrupted communications are operational realities. Sealed enclosures, passive thermal management and resilient power architectures protect the hardware. Embedded governance manages available resources, preserves essential services and controls recovery as conditions change.
Governed Locally. Coordinated Across Distributed Systems.
Three embedded governance authorities work together at each node. Sentinel governs power and physical operating conditions. Hermes provides authenticated command, communications and operational truth. Prometheus manages mission intelligence and workload priorities within those boundaries. Each node retains local authority when connectivity is disrupted, while coordinating with the wider system when communications are available. Infrastructure governs to maintain mission priorities.
