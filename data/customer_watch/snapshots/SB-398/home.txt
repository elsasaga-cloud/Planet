INDUSTRIAL NETWORKING. ENGINEERED IN AUSTRALIA
Optical Systems Design Pty Ltd (OSD) is an Australian designer and manufacturer of industrial networking and fibre optic communication products. Since 1987, OSD has developed reliable, high-performance solutions for critical communications networks in transportation, security, defence, mining, telecommunications and other demanding applications.
From our facility in Sydney, Australia, OSD designs, manufactures and supports an extensive range of Industrial Ethernet switches, PoE products, media converters and specialised fibre optic communication systems. Our in-house engineering capability allows us to provide both standard products and customised solutions, backed by experienced technical support from initial system design through to installation and operation.
OSD products are deployed throughout Australia and around the world, providing reliable communications in applications ranging from roadside and rail infrastructure to mines, security networks, telecommunications and broadcast systems. With almost 40 years of engineering experience, OSD is committed to long-term product support, quality and dependable performance.
