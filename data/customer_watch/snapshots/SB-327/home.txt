Solutions for Critical Infrastructure
and Industrial Operations
The Challenge
Organizations operating critical infrastructure and industrial environments face increasing demands for digitalization, cybersecurity, operational resilience, and efficiency. Whether modernizing power grids, managing distributed energy assets, or advancing industrial automation, success depends on secure computing platforms, reliable networking, and scalable software-defined architectures.
Our Approach
Welotec and Westermo deliver mission-critical computing and networking solutions that connect, secure, and virtualize the systems powering modern energy infrastructure and industrial operations. From IEC 61850-3 certified substation computing platforms to industrial edge computers , our combined portfolio helps organizations build resilient, future-ready operations.
Open Ecosystem
Our experts help shape the standards behind the digital substation — contributing to IEC and CIGRE working groups, the UCA International Users Group , the vPAC Alliance , and Linux Foundation Energy ,. As part of Westermo, we build on open standards instead of proprietary lock-in, supporting organizations worldwide in their digital transformation.
Digital Substation
Virtualized Protection, Automation and Control (vPAC) for the Software-Defined Grid
The transition toward digital substations is transforming how utilities design, operate, and secure critical grid infrastructure. Modern architectures require high-performance computing platforms capable of consolidating protection, automation, control, and cybersecurity workloads into resilient virtualized environments.
Welotec and Westermo enable utilities, transmission and distribution system operators (TSOs and DSOs), and system integrators to deploy software defined substation architectures based on open standards such as IEC 61850. Our combined substation computing and networking portfolio provides the foundation for vPAC deployments — reducing hardware complexity while improving flexibility, scalability, and operational efficiency.

Secondary Substation
Secure Connectivity and Resilient Communication for Grid Edge Assets
As electricity networks become more decentralized, reliable communication and remote visibility are essential for maintaining grid stability. Secondary substations require secure networking technologies that ensure continuous operation — even in challenging environments and during critical events.
Welotec and Westermo provide resilient communication solutions for monitoring, telecontrol, and remote operation of secondary substations. Combining industrial cellular routers, rugged Ethernet switches, and high-performance antennas, our joint portfolio helps grid operators maintain reliable connectivity across distributed energy networks.

Machinery & Manufacturing
Industrial Edge Computing for Smart Machines and Connected Production
Modern manufacturing demands more than connectivity. Machine builders and industrial operators need scalable edge computing platforms capable of processing data locally, running containerized applications, and securely connecting operational technology with cloud environments.
Welotec enables digital manufacturing through open edge computing solutions, fanless industrial computers, and vendor-neutral software platforms. Together with our sister company Beijer Electronics and through open-source initiatives like SEALMAN organizations can deploy scalable edge applications, improve machine uptime, and create new digital services without vendor lock-in.
