What We Offer
One strategy. Five Pillars
Real-world environments are not single-vendor. They are built using the best solution for each requirement, and those solutions need to work together seamlessly.
Duxbury's five pillars cover every layer of your infrastructure: Networking, Wireless, Cybersecurity, Surveillance and Services. Each pillar represents a specialist practice. Our Story
Duxbury Networking
Duxbury Networking was founded in 1984 by Graham Duxbury and Barrie Neunborn – not as a reseller, but as a technology developer. We designed and manufactured modems before distribution was even part of the plan. That engineering background shaped everything: how we evaluate vendors, how we train our staff and how we engage with our partners.
Today we are more than a distributor. We work alongside our resellers as infrastructure architects, technical advisors and implementation partners, bringing the expertise to design the right solution, the support to deploy it correctly and the commitment to stay involved long after the sale.
From our branches in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban, we support 8,000+ resellers across Africa. We employ more certified engineers than sales staff — because we believe a distributor’s value is measured by the quality of its technical support, not the size of its catalogue.
