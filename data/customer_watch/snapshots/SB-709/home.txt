MORE ABOUT IC INTRACOM
IC INTRACOM was founded in 1992 and has been operating on the international market ever since. In 2009, the company established a subsidiary specifically to meet the needs of customers in Bulgaria. We manufacture and distribute a carefully selected range of products in the field of computer and telecommunications networks, and we are committed to continuously updating and expanding our portfolio with the latest technologies. IC INTRACOM partners with world-renowned manufacturers from Asia and Europe, and the equipment we offer undergoes the strictest quality control before being released to the market.
We place particular emphasis on the comprehensive training of our employees and consultants, striving to always stay up to date with the latest developments in the world of technology and in compliance with the highest international standards. That is why, in 2013, our company successfully developed and implemented an integrated quality management system in accordance with ISO 9001:2008 and OHSAS 18001:2007. This enables us to continue providing you with services at the level you are accustomed to and deserve.
