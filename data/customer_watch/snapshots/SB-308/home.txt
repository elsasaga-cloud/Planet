Corporate Info
We understand the need to intelligently integrate the business process with technology to create modern, scalable solutions.
Alcon India was migrated from Alcon Telecommunication-Taiwan and established as an independent unit on 2010 in India. Alcon India is a leading global provider of IP-based Smart Surveillance products, PoE Switches and WIS Range of broadband Wireless LAN series and WLAN controller series products and solutions for Enterprises, Small-to-Medium-sized businesses and SOHO markets. ALCON India has been dedicated to high-quality enterprise IP Surveillance solution with the most complete range of products offering to satisfy the growing demand of various entities. Our innovative technology forms a strong foundation for breakthrough in high performance and next generation solution. Alcon IP surveillance products and solutions are now available in Middle East, SAARC countries (Nepal and Bhutan). In India Alcon has three importers and 16+ distributors and 200+ System Integrators dedicatedly working on ALCON product solutions.
