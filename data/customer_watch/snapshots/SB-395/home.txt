The operator's reality
Poor in-home Wi-Fi costs you twice. First in support calls, then in churn.
Your core network can run at spec while subscribers still blame you for dead zones and dropped calls. The router in the living room decides how they rate your service.
01
No view past the modem
Your team can see the network up to the gateway. Inside the home, agents troubleshoot blind, and simple fixes turn into long calls.
02
Slow provisioning
Hardware that needs hands-on setup at every install ties up your team. Deployment time grows with every new subscriber.
03
Subscriber churn
Subscribers judge your service by the Wi-Fi they feel at home. When it drops, they start comparing providers.
