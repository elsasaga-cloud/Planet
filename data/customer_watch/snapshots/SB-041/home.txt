How We Go The Extra Mile For Your Protection
Grade 2, 3 & 4 Alarm Systems
All our systems are fitted to a NSI Gold standard and fully insurance approved. With our professional team we will guide you through the whole process until you are satisfied.
Secure Connect App
Our systems all come with a free mobile app which allow you to set and unset your system conveniently from your mobile phone. Also, the app notifies you if the system is activated and tells you exactly which room was triggered.
Full 24/7 Support
We have an emergency call-out team active 24 hours a day. Any problems you can easily contact us and get full support. We take pride in our customer service!
Custom Area Setup
With our advanced system, we can configure it to arm certain parts of your home. This gives the comfort of being able to have an active alarm downstairs when you sleep.
We’re Certified & Accredited Security System Providers
Telcam is a UK fire and security firm protecting homes, businesses and public buildings. We are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and we put the customer first on every job, from the first site visit through to aftercare.
Our engineers design, install, maintain and support the systems we specify. Around 75% of our work comes from referrals, because the installation has to stand up once we have left.
