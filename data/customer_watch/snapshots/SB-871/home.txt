Teiaş OPGW Solutions
OPGW (Optical Ground Wire) Fiber Optic conductor solution is TEİAŞ’s communication system via Fiber Optic Cables drawn on 154 KV and 380 KV lines. Both ends of the OPGW lines are provided by pulling the non-metallic fiber optic cable into the telecom chamber after ending in the OPGW box on the OPGW pole and installing SDH devices and Multiplexer devices at the end

TESAS RTU Solutions
RTU provides central control in sacada systems with its shortest definition and performs the task of communication between the center and the sacada system. It was formed by combining the bar letters of the English words Remote Terminal Unit. As can be understood in English, physical equipment in the field collects sensors, inputs and outputs and measuring device data and transmits it to the central unit. At the same time, the information in the central unit provides remote control in the lede field
