Our Solutions
​
RoomAlert Monitoring
RoomAlert Monitors computer rooms, data caters and other facilities for conditions such as temperature, humidity, power flood / water leakage, smoke / fire, air flow, room entry, motion, cameras and more.
Rapid Deployment System
Instant, intelligent and highly mobile security modular system that supports customized integration of equipment such as IP cameras, wireless devices, emergency lighting, PA equipment, etc. on retractable pneumatic mast. Powered by batteries, AC power or solar power ensuring uninterrupted operation.
Wireless Solutions
Outdoor and Indoor wireless Access Point and Routers. Fiber-like performance and intelligent network technology and solutions provide extended reach, incremental capacity, and redundancy to any existing city infrastructure
Thermal Camera Solutions
Thermal cameras with high temperature accuracy can help detect elevated body temperatures which may indicate the presence of a fever. Thermal cameras can be used for the fever screening of travelers, shoppers, and office workers.
Industrial GSM Gateway
Innovative industrial secure LTE router designed for IIoT connectivity by single or dual high-speed 4G LTE routing or Wi-Fi networks.
Data Center Solutions
Data Center Infrastructure markets, including Modular Data Centers, Container Data Centers and our pioneering new product, the Micro Data Center. To support these systems, we also provide infrastructure capabilities that include cooling subsystems, power supply and power distribution subsystems, Data Center cabling subsystems, along with intelligent data center infrastructure management subsystems.
Copper & Fiber Solutions
Structured cabling solution & telecommunications cabling system with a set of cabling and connectivity products that should be able to support the transmission of voice, graphic, images, data multimedia, security monitoring, and sensing, etc. It mainly consists of six subsystems.
2 Way Radio Solutions
Icom is an outstanding, comprehensive radio manufacturer that produces radio communication equipment of all genres, from amateur radios to terrestrial, marine and avionics radios, as well as LTE, WLAN and satellite radios.
