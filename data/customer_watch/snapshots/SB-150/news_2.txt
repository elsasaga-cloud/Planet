Turn Any Legacy Wired Alarm Into a Smart Ajax System — No Rewiring Required

The Problem With Legacy Alarms
Old Panels Are Running Out of Runway
Copper phone lines are being switched off nationally, spare parts for discontinued panels are drying up, and clients increasingly expect to see and control their alarm from a phone — not just a beige keypad by the front door. None of that is optional anymore; it's what clients now assume "a security system" means.
✕ Copper/PSTN phone-line dialers are being decommissioned across Australia
✕ No smartphone app, no push alerts, no remote arm/disarm
✕ Zero jamming detection — an easy target for a determined intruder
✕ Replacement panels, keypads and boards are increasingly hard to source
The Fix
One Small Module Bridges Old Wiring Into a New Ajax System
The Ajax MultiTransmitter connects up to 18 wired zones — from any brand of PIR, reed switch, glass-break or panic button — directly onto its terminal block, exactly like the old panel did. It then encrypts and relays every one of those zones wirelessly over Ajax's Jeweller protocol to a Hub 2 up to 2km away. The cable stays in the wall. The sensors stay on the wall. Only the panel changes.
18 wired zones 2 tamper-monitored inputs Backup battery + 12V input Encrypted Jeweller radio ~1 visit typical swap
★ The Biggest Upsell In This Whole Job
This Isn't a Like-for-Like Swap — It's a Hub With Room for 100 Devices
The Hub 2 supports up to 100 devices in total . Every wired zone landed on the MultiTransmitter only counts as one of those 100 — so a typical 8–12 zone change-over still leaves 80–90+ slots free for genuine wireless Ajax devices, added with zero extra cabling. That headroom is the real conversation to have with the client — this quote isn't just "replace the old panel", it's "lay the foundation for a full smart security and automation system".

System Architecture
How It Works, End to End
When a detector triggers, the signal reaches the client's phone and the monitoring centre within seconds — across multiple communication paths at once.
1
Existing Sensor Trips
A wired PIR, reed switch or glass-break triggers exactly as it always has.
2
MultiTransmitter Relays It
The zone terminal picks up the signal and sends it encrypted over Jeweller to the Hub 2.
3
Hub 2 Notifies Everyone
Alerts go out over Ethernet, Wi-Fi and dual SIM — to the client's phone and monitoring centre at once.
4
Response Dispatched
Client checks the app in real time; the monitoring centre verifies and dispatches — faster, more reliable.
