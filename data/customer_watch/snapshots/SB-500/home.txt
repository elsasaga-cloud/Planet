About Our Company
CET Electric Technology is the manufacturer behind Singapore’s most-deployed digital power monitors, energy meters and power-quality analysers. Industrial, commercial and data-centre operators across Singapore trust CET Electric Technology hardware for accurate, reliable power monitoring solutions Singapore teams use to support BCA Green Mark, the Energy Conservation Act, ISO 50001 and the rising Singapore Carbon Tax — and to feed integrated IoT energy platforms like our partner EcoXplore Singapore .
MORE ABOUT OUR COMPANY
Energy Management Systems
Thousands of metering points across factories, commercial buildings and data centres — integrated for monitoring, control, cost allocation and Singapore Carbon Tax / SGX sustainability reporting.
BMS & Software Integration
Modbus RTU/TCP, BACnet, Ethernet — CET meters integrate with Singapore building management systems and our partner EcoXplore’s IoT energy platform.
Remote Sites Monitoring
Connect remote meters across substations, telecom towers and utility infrastructure via cellular and IoT networks.
Transformer & Power Quality Analytics
Detect transformer degradation, harmonic distortion and voltage events early. PMC-680i power-quality monitors deliver IEEE 519-aligned data for preventive maintenance.
Vision & Mission
Develop accurate, reliable digital metering and power-monitoring instruments — and the integrated automation that connects them — for every Singapore industry that depends on dependable electrical infrastructure. Help our customers run safer, more efficient and more sustainable power systems, from single-meter retrofits to facility-wide energy management deployments.
