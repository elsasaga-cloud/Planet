Welcome To Luxrite Security
Security You Can Trust
At Luxrite Ltd, we are proud to be a UK distributor of CCTV, Intruder Alarms, Cable and Video Door Entry products, offering not just equipment, but complete and dependable solutions.
We understand that every installation is different, which is why our focus is on providing products that genuinely suit your requirements—never forcing a one-size-fits-all approach. We work closely with installers, integrators, and security professionals to ensure you receive the support, expertise, and high-quality technology needed to deliver outstanding results.
Every camera, DVR, and NVR undergoes rigorous testing during product evaluation and again before it leaves our warehouse, guaranteeing performance you can rely on. If you’re looking for a supplier who values partnership, reliability, and tailored solutions, we’d be delighted to speak with you.
