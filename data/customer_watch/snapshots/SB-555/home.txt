AT TACEL
Tacel is a Canadian owned company specializing in traffic control systems and intelligent transportation solutions. Since 1979, we have been at the forefront of traffic engineering innovation, providing cutting edge products and expert services to municipalities, contractors, and consulting engineers across Canada.
Collaboration
At Tacel, collaboration is the cornerstone of our success. Our teams work cohesively across departments and with industry-leading partners to deliver comprehensive traffic solutions. This synergy is evident in our partnerships with companies like Applied Information and Sensys Networks, enabling us to provide cutting-edge technologies such as Connected Vehicle solutions and advanced detection systems.
Commitment
Our unwavering commitment to quality and safety is demonstrated through our ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System, ensuring that our operations meet the highest standards. Being 100% Canadian-owned and operated, we take pride in serving communities across the nation, tailoring our solutions to meet diverse regional needs.
Determination
Tacel's determination drives us to tackle complex challenges in traffic management. Our expansion into Smart City technologies, including Emergency Vehicle Preemption and Transit Signal Priority systems, showcases our proactive approach to enhancing road safety and efficiency.
Passion
Passion fuels our innovation and dedication to improving transportation infrastructure. Our team is deeply invested in developing solutions that make communities safer and more accessible. Through initiatives like the deployment of Accessible Pedestrian Systems in collaboration with Polara, we strive to ensure inclusivity and safety for all road users.

Agencies & Institutions
We support agencies overseeing hospitals, schools, and government facilities with safe, inclusive traffic systems. Our solutions promote accessibility, protect the public, and help institutions maintain orderly, efficient site circulation.
Construction
We assist construction teams by supplying certified traffic control systems for safe, compliant work zones. From temporary signals to custom setups, our solutions minimize disruption and help maintain flow during active construction projects.
Cities & Municipalities
We partner with municipalities to deliver traffic systems that improve safety, reduce congestion, and support local goals. Solutions are tailored to each city's infrastructure, ensuring efficient operation, community accessibility, and long-term value.
Transportation Departments
Our team collaborates with transportation departments to implement adaptive signals, traffic monitoring, and communication systems. These tools improve day-to-day operations, streamline response times, and align with broader regional traffic management strategies.
Consulting Engineers
We work with consulting engineers to design and implement effective traffic solutions. Our product knowledge and technical support ensure projects meet specifications, timelines, and performance goals across diverse roadway and infrastructure designs.
