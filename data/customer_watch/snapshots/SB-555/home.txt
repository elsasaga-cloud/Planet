That's where Tacel comes in
Since 1979, Tacel has been 100% Canadian-owned, bringing 45+ years of ITS expertise to safer, smarter roadways across Canada.
Collaboration
At Tacel, collaboration is the cornerstone of our success. Our teams work cohesively across departments and with industry-leading partners to deliver comprehensive traffic solutions. This synergy is evident in our partnerships with companies like Applied Information and Sensys Networks, enabling us to provide cutting-edge technologies such as Connected Vehicle solutions and advanced detection systems.
Commitment
Our unwavering commitment to quality and safety is demonstrated through our ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System, ensuring that our operations meet the highest standards. Being 100% Canadian-owned and operated, we take pride in serving communities across the nation, tailoring our solutions to meet diverse regional needs.
Determination
Tacel's determination drives us to tackle complex challenges in traffic management. Our expansion into Smart City technologies, including Emergency Vehicle Preemption and Transit Signal Priority systems, showcases our proactive approach to enhancing road safety and efficiency.
Passion
Passion fuels our innovation and dedication to improving transportation infrastructure. Our team is deeply invested in developing solutions that make communities safer and more accessible. Through initiatives like the deployment of Accessible Pedestrian Systems in collaboration with Polara, we strive to ensure inclusivity and safety for all road users.

Traffic Products Built on Expertise
Traffic cabinets engineered to shield critical intersection electronics from weather, vibration and roadside impact, configured to your agency's standard and delivered field-ready.
The decision-making core of the intersection: adaptive, standards-based controllers that keep traffic moving, integrate cleanly with your central system and stay serviceable for years.
Radar, video and loop-free detection that watches every approach in any weather, giving your controllers accurate, real-time data to act on without cutting a single loop into the road.
Conflict monitors and malfunction management units that watch the intersection continuously, fail it safe the moment something goes wrong and log exactly what happened for the crew.
Backup power platforms that keep signals live through outages and brownouts, with batteries, enclosures and monitoring sized for the site and specified to survive a Canadian winter.
Central software that connects every controller on the network: remote monitoring, timing changes and diagnostics from a single operations screen, across one corridor or a whole city.
High-visibility LED signal heads, arrows and optics built for long service life, low power draw and unmistakable indication in sun, snow and fog, on every approach of the intersection.
Wired and wireless networks carrying every intersection back to the traffic management centre, built to stay up through weather, power events and the years between upgrades.
Accessible pedestrian signals and pushbutton stations that guide every pedestrian across the intersection with audible, tactile and visual cues, to Canadian accessibility standards.

Controller Database Programming
phasing · detectors · overlaps
We build and load the controller database for each intersection: phasing, detector assignments, overlaps, pedestrian intervals and every local convention your agency runs by. You get a configuration that matches the drawings, is checked before it leaves us, and is documented well enough that the next person can pick it up years from now.

Solutions
Signal Performance & Engineering
detector health · split failures · queueing
Our traffic engineers audit how an intersection is actually behaving rather than how it was meant to: detector health, phase utilisation, split failures, queueing and pedestrian service. You get findings tied to measurements, so the changes that follow are ones you can defend to a council or to a resident who called in.
Learn more — Signal Performance & Engineering Talk to a specialist — Signal Performance & Engineering
Solutions
Advanced Signal Timing
corridors · EVP · TSP
Timing plans built for the corridor rather than the single intersection: coordination and offsets across a route, time-of-day plans, emergency vehicle preemption and transit signal priority. We build them, load them, and stay reachable through the first days while you watch how they actually run.
Learn more — Advanced Signal Timing Talk to a specialist — Advanced Signal Timing

Custom Traffic Cabinets
layout · terminals · labelling
Cabinets wired and assembled to your standard, not ours: your layout, your terminal conventions, your labelling. Every one is tested before it ships and arrives with the drawings that match it, so a crew opening the twentieth cabinet of a program finds what they found in the first.
