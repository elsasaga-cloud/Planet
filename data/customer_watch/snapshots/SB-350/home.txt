Products
Ethernet Switches

Ethernet Media Converters
Managed or Unmanaged Media Converters with up to 4TX channels and optional PoE of up to 90W

Equipment for harsh environments
Edge Networks products are designed for lifetime operation in the most hostile conditions including temperatures from -40 to +75 degrees Celsius and high vibration areas such as those found on roads, bridges, and railway track sides. Military derating guidelines are used to insure long life under severe conditions.
