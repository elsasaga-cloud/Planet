ABOUT US
Technoverse Business Solutions
IT Infrastructure & Technology Solutions | Consulting | Global Sourcing
Established in 2019 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, Technoverse Business Solutions is a B2B technology solutions company focused on helping organizations address their IT infrastructure and technology requirements.
We work across enterprise networking, cybersecurity, computing, storage, unified communications, surveillance, and related technology infrastructure. Our expertise covers a broad range of technologies from leading global brands, including Cisco, HPE, Dell Technologies, Aruba, Fortinet, Avaya, Arista, Seagate, Grandstream, Juniper, TP-Link, Ruckus , and others.
​
Our technology portfolio includes routers, switches, servers, wireless access points, firewalls, storage systems, SSDs, HDDs, server components, GPUs, CPUs, GLC/SFP transceivers, unified communications, video surveillance solutions, and other IT infrastructure technologies.
We support organizations by understanding their technical and business requirements, identifying suitable technology solutions, evaluating configurations and compatibility, and coordinating the sourcing and fulfilment of required infrastructure through our global technology and channel network.
​
With a focus on technical understanding, solution-oriented consulting, reliable sourcing, project coordination, and responsive customer support, we aim to help businesses build and maintain dependable technology infrastructure.
Our operations and international network enable us to support technology requirements across multiple markets while maintaining a strong focus on transparency, business ethics, quality, and customer satisfaction.
