1-21-2022, Recently, F-engine PoE series Ethernet switch have got large-scale application in Telecom operator’s network in multiple provinces such as Chinese Guangdong, Liaoning, Hunan, Yunnan, Sichuan, etc. Aim at full-service operation and network IP-based reconstruction, FHN-NA released PoE Ethernet switch based on over-all analysis of inherent product advantages F-engine Ethernet switch process. This product family include 1000M carrier Ethernet switch F-engine S2200ME-PAF and carrier-grade campus Ethernet switch F-engine S3900-PAF, F-engine S3500-PAF series, which address a broad variety demand from different customers.The PoE series Ethernet switch that used in large-scale support full wire-speed forwarding, PoE, QinQ, VLAN-based service control, powerful ACL, multicast, QoS, etc. functionalities. F-engine PoE switch offers visual and powerful GUI as well as cluster network management that fulfill the broadband networking requirement in various application scenarios.Moreover, F-engine PoE series switches support PoE( Power over Ethernet) technology, allow IP phone, wireless AP, IPC and many other connected appliances to receive power as well as data over existing LAN cabling, without needing to use additional power supply that facilitate the network deployment and management. In actual operation, PoE switch could provide over-temperature and over-power protection, offer power supply management over different port, which fully meet the latest power supply demand from industry users.
