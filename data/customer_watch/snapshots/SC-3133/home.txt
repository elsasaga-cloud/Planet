FIBER OPTIC
Stallard is a manufacturer and master distributor specializing in premium Premise and Outside Plant materials. We proudly offer an extensive range of high-quality products tailored to meet your connectivity needs. Our premier premise solutions include Fiber Optic Cables (Indoor and Indoor/Outdoor rated), Wall and Rack Mounted Fiber Enclosures, Custom Fiber Assemblies, and Cable Management systems. For outside plant applications, we provide top-tier products such as Fiber Optic Cables, Plastic and Polymer Concrete Handholes, Conduit, Aerial Strand, and Aerial Hardware.
