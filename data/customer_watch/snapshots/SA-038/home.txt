About Us
Established in the year 2011, DNC Technosolutions Pvt. Ltd. is one of the well recognized Manufacturers and Solution makers of Fiber Optic Products and Technology . Our range comprises Fiber Optic Patch cords , Pigtails , Connectors , Adapters , Couplers , Splitters , Patch panels , Data to Fiber converters, Video to Fiber Converter , Telephone to Fiber Converters , SFP Modules , MPO Systems , Breakout Systems . These are known for salient features like higher transmission & conductivity, durability and enhanced insulation.
Our company is located at Kochi, Kerala (India), wherein we follow advanced fiber optic communication technologies in order to ensure international quality standard products. We have highly qualified and talented professionals who work in tandem so as to meet the exact demands of our prestigious customers. Our quality controllers test the entire range stringently to ensure zero defect products. Further, our production unit is fitted with latest machines that enable us to undertake the bulk consignment of our customers.
