Rock Solid Blue
Used by AV and entertainment professionals around the world, ProPlex Cables, Drives, Devices, Software, Switches and Tools are designed to help the modern entertainment/AV technician with even the most challenging and complex tasks. ProPlex technology is proven to be rock solid; rugged and reliable, performing flawlessly in extreme conditions, again and again and again.
Simply Intuitive
The ProPlex family of data distribution devices and drives features uniquely intuitive operating systems that maximize simplicity and accessibility for the user. We are committed to providing state-of-the-art, powerful products, with the understanding that, in most applications, simplicity of use is critically important. A prime ProPlex design criteria therefore is to make operation easier for both the on-site technician, and for their support team back at HQ. This real-world approach simplifies the most complex challenges, and provides a turnkey solution for A/V and entertainment professionals at any level.

Rugged, Reliable
First exhibited in our famous ProPlex Portable Data Cables, and now in our comprehensive range of ProPlex Devices and Drives, professional users worldwide know they can depend on the optimum performance, peerless reliability and ruggedness that the ProPlex name represents. ProPlex portable products, originally developed for use in touring Rock and Roll, are built for the most extreme conditions of use, with standard design features including super-rugged housings, shock-mounted circuit boards, and advanced thermal management. A wide variety of enclosure options are available, including RackMount, PortableMount, Mini, and Micro, plus IP Rated formats of various sizes. We know “Life on the Road” can be unforgiving, so we design and build ProPlex Devices to withstand wear and tear show, after show, after show. The ProPlex dependability assurance also extends into ProPlex architectural products: which include various application-specific data cables and DIN Rail Devices and Drives.
