// the circular lifecycle
Equipment one operator retires is the spare part another still needs.
Telecom equipment moves through a lifecycle: it is used, maintained and extended while an operator needs it, then recovered and redeployed when they don’t. Carritech works on both sides of that journey — so hardware keeps earning its keep instead of being scrapped.
↻
Responsible recycling is the route for equipment that genuinely cannot be reused — not the final stage of every journey. Reuse always comes first.
