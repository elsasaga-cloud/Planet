WHO IS VOYGAR
VOYGAR Connecting System is a leading manufacturer of telecommunications, wiring, and data equipment.
At VOYGAR , we offer quality network solutions, from structured cabling system (SCS) to networking components and data center equipment that support all of today’s network infrastructure.

Warranty
VOYGAR SYSTEMS offers 25 years project warranty against manufacturing defects for end-to-end cabling solutions based on the approval of test report submitted by an authorized system integrator. The warranty does not cover for any other product or brand integrated with VOYGAR systems. For the Raised Floor System, 5 years warranty against any manufacturing defects up on the approval of the concerned first party executive. The warranty does not cover for any damage due to the application of excess load than what specified in specification document of VOYGAR. For all other items, 1 year warranty against any manufacturer defects and the warranty time starts at the time of delivery of materials to the project or customer.
