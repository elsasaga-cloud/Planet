PIXCOM TECHNOLOGIES LLC is a full-service solution provider of digital signage, LED Displays interactive kiosks, Media network solutions. Our comprehensive suite of products and services includes system consulting and design, content management software, interactive solutions, installation services, network hosting and management, remote monitoring and display diagnostics, reporting capabilities and technical support.
Turnkey Solution Provider
We provide effective , scalable, versatile , end-to-end solutions for your digital signage needs.Innovative visual display solutions from scratch to finish that help your business thrive.
Design & Project Consultancy
We help you steer through the maze of hardware and software choices. We suggest the best offering that meets your goals and objectives while taking into consideration your timeline and budget.
LED Design & Integration
We provide industry-leading LED display technology that revolutionizes your space and businesses. We pride ourselves on creating and delivering custom, intelligent LED display solutions for our customers.
Installation,Maintenance & Support
