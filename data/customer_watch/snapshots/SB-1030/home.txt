Fibertronics is a USA-based manufacturer of fiber optic connectivity products serving defense, telecom, industrial, and data center customers nationwide. We manufacture fiber optic patch cables, MTP/MPO assemblies, MDC and MMC connector assemblies, fiber optic flex circuit assemblies (FOFCA), MIL-SPEC cable, and custom cable assemblies in-house, all 100% optically tested before shipment. We also stock a full range of fiber optic infrastructure products including MPO cassettes, patch panels, termination boxes, MSTs, splice enclosures, adapters, and transceivers. Fibertronics is TL 9000 and AS9100D certified, ITAR compliant, and offers BABA and TAA compliant product options.
