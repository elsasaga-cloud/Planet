11/04/2024 ERM1: 1-Port Ethernet A/B switch with integrated I/O
Boost your network reliability and security in critical infrastructures with ERM1 . The remote-controllable network selector ERM1 offers the ability to physically change over Ethernet connections or switch them on or off, enabling precise manage­ment of network traffic and protection against unauthorized access or failures. ERM1 supports 10 Gigabit Ethernet connections and is expected to be available as a hardware module for DIN rail mounting as from Q3 2024.
